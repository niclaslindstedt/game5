title: Butters and presses on a snowboard
---

Lean well forward or back on a snowboard at a crawl or a cruise and the board presses onto its nose or its tail, the other end lifting off the snow; steer through the press and the board pivots round on that end — a butter, half round to ride away fakie or a whole turn back. A soft board butters quicker than a stiff one.

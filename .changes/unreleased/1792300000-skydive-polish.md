title: The skydive polished

At full canopy the pilot chute and its bag now ride the bridle off the middle of the canopy's top skin. The bag lies on the cloth and the pilot chute trails above and behind it. Through the opening both stay straight over the top. The stabilizers off the end cells are smaller and hang toward the lines, as a sport main's do.

The jumper leaves the door laid onto the wind. He is about 30° into the arch within half a second and belly to earth by the third second. Before, he fell upright for the first second.

A canopy caught in a tree is spread over its top few metres, held out by the boughs, with his lines running down from it to where he hangs. Before, it wrapped the spire like a sock.

Aboard the jump plane, HELMET is now his own eyes in the door, the same eyes the skydive gives HELMET. The cockpit has moved to TIPS, as the helicopter's TIPS is its nose lens.

Under the canopy, working the toggles or the risers can now be heard: the brake line zipping through its ring and the tail's strained flutter.

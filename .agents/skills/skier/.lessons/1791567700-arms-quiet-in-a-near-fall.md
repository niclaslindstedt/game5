The cliff's windmill and the save's arms flung up and wobbled side to side
read in the game as a skier losing it, however measured the circle was —
the owner asked for both out. The fall now spots with the hands forward
and still at every height, and a save braces both hands forward and rocks
the body once and back. The earlier lessons about the windmill's circle
and its poles are history: there is no windmill to tune. Read any new arm
motion in a near fall against this — the legs and the hips do the work,
the hands stay where he can see them.

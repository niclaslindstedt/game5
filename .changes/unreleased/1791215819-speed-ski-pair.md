title: A speed ski joins the catalog
---

An eighth pair of skis, the Peregrine, built to speed skiing's top-class rule: 240 cm, under 10 cm wide with next to no sidecut, heavy and damped to keep its tips down, with short bent poles — and under it a racer in an airtight suit with fairings behind his calves, a tuck with a quarter of a downhiller's drag. Straight down the fall line nothing comes near it; it will not turn. Speed skiing's field races it. The ski card bills every pair on an eighth bar, FAST BEND — the bend a pair holds at a downhill's 130 km/h, where the downhill ski is the best of them.

title: Far crags no longer flicker
---

The rock on the far mountain faces no longer flickers as you ride. Each small facet of the rock used to keep its own shade and lighting however far off it was. A few pixels across, those facets twinkled from frame to frame, and far out the snow underneath fought the rock for the same pixels. Now the rock eases into a smooth shading of its face with distance and is always drawn in front of the snow it stands on. Up close it looks the same as before.

---
type: Changed
title: The free ride's mountains come up much faster, and the same ones for everyone
---

Building a mountain is about a third quicker everywhere, with every map exactly the mountain it was. The free ride's start card now opens on the same first mountain for every skier — the one the front door stands on, so its chart and its ride come off a mountain already built — and ANOTHER MOUNTAIN steps through a fixed list of them, building the next one in the background while you look at the one on the card. A chart you have seen before shows at once on your next visit, and pressing RIDE no longer builds the mountain a second time on the loading card. A seed you type is still a mountain of your own.

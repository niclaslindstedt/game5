title: The chase camera leans down the mountain and keeps the skier in view
---

The chase, far and high cameras now read the slope ahead and tip down a steep face with it, most of the way but not all, so the drop is felt under a horizon that stays near the top of the frame instead of half a picture of sky. The skier is held at the same place in the frame on every pitch, a little under the middle with the piste ahead above him. He is bigger in the frame at speed than before. The lens moves with weight now: it gathers into a swing, settles out of it and breathes after a landing, and it starts to tip before you reach the edge of a drop.

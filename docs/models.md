# The models

Every kind of object the game draws, and where what the player sees comes from: a **Blender** model (made off the game's own data by `make models`, committed in `pwa/models/`, drawn by every build — the `blender-assets` skill) or **code** (built by the game at run time). A modelled asset keeps its code builder: it is what the model is judged against, and a build switched back (`VITE_MODEL_*=0`, in the environment, the root `.env`, or `make ci-models MODELS=off` for CI) draws it instead.

The ground, the sky, the trails, the spray and the snow cloud are surfaces and effects rather than objects, and are always code. Only the skis — the rigged hero asset — and the free ride's heli-ski helicopter (three rigid nodes: `heli_body`, `heli_rotor` and `heli_tail_rotor`, every dimension read off `HELI`; `make models KIND=heli`) are models. The skier is DRESSED in code: his outfit (`outfit.ts`) cut onto the rig in its bind pose and skinned on its bones (`dress-loft.ts`, `skier-dress.ts`), since the gear is a catalog to mix and a modelled skier is one suit (`make gear --sheet=compare` sets the two side by side; the `skier-gear` skill says why). The trees, the birds, the animals and the course's marks are built procedurally on one bench (`pwa/src/game/tree-mesh.ts`), chunky, faceted and low-poly.

The table is generated from `pwa/src/game/model-registry.ts` by `make model-registry` and never hand-edited; `tests/model_registry_test.ts` holds it, and holds the registry's Blender rows to exactly the files the build packs. **Modelling a new kind** is a row flipped from `code` to `blender` there, in the same change that ships its models.

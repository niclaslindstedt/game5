---
type: Changed
title: Lifts turn round their bullwheels
---

Every lift's carriers now come round the bullwheel at each station instead of vanishing near it and reappearing on the other rope: a chair creeps round a horseshoe rail under its terminal's hood (now rounded about the wheel and carried on a central pedestal), a gondola's cabins are let down onto a rail under the platform roof and creep round in the open, and a drag's T-bars swing round their wheel on the rope and ride back down a return rope on the other side of the towers. Hung carriers lean out of the turn, the rope is drawn round each wheel, and the station houses stand back clear of the swing.

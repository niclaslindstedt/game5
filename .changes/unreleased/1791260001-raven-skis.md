title: The Raven, a big air ski
---

A tenth pair joins the ski card: the RAVEN, a stiff competition twin-tip for big air (1.80 m, 118/88/114 mm, a 20 m sidecut, mounted near the centre), built to take the biggest landings of any pair in the catalog. Picking big air opens the ski card on the Raven and the dress card on the medium build, the build nearest the measured weight of an elite freeski field; the player can still change either.

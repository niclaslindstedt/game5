The skier now rides on his legs like springs. Over rollers and uneven snow his skis go up and down with the ground and his knees fold and stretch to take it, while his upper body rides on far steadier, about a third of the bounce. His trunk also holds its angle while the skis rock fore and aft under him. A landing folds him deep into his knees, trunk over, and he stands back up within the second. Skimming off the top of a bump for a moment no longer snaps him into his jump pose or interrupts his poling, and a jump he springs himself carries his body up with it.

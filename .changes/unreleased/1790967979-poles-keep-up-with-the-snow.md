---
type: Changed
title: Poles that stay planted, poled harder the faster you go
---

The skier's poles now bite the snow where they are planted and stay there while he goes past, instead of sliding along with him. The faster he goes, the quicker and harder he works his arms. When his arms can't keep up with the snow any more, around 22–27 km/h on the flat, he stops poling and folds into his tuck if you're holding it. The push from the poles now runs out at 34 km/h instead of 40.

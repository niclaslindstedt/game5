title: Fly the jump plane and skydive
---

A free ride can now start in the door of a jump plane. Pick PLANE on the start card's RUN row, or open a `?plane=1` link. The plane has its own keys, which can be rebound under OPTIONS ▸ KEYS. Hold the stick back and it will loop. On touch it flies on the helicopter's two pads.

ENTER (a double tap on touch) jumps from the door, opens the canopy, and cuts it away. The HUD says which one comes next. On screen you get the plane's airspeed, height, climb, power and flaps, and on the skydive an altimeter with a PULL cue.

The plane has its own camera views:
- CHASE, FAR, HIGH and ORBIT circle the airframe.
- TIPS looks out of the door.
- HELMET is the pilot's seat.

Under the canopy the paramotor's views take over. The airframe, canopy and container are drawn as simple stand-ins for now.

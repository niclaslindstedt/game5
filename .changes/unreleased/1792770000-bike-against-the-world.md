The downhill bike now meets the summer mountain properly. Its handlebar ends catch on a trunk or a post they clip, and from about 20 km/h that throws you off. A bike thrown against a tree lies against it instead of pressing on into it. Parked cars and the village's street lamps are there to hit on a summer ride, and a car knocks you off the bike.

Rolled into a chairlift's or a gondola's load zone at a walking pace, the bike now boards every lift. Before, the bars flopped over at a crawl and the bike ran past the first chair on the main mountain.

Stepped off the bike, the rider now pushes it beside him with a hand on its grip, and the machine press puts him back on it wherever he stops.

The bar-cam now sits on the stem, with the bar and both grips across the foot of the picture.

Dying on the bike now says the next rider starts on the bike at the trail's head.

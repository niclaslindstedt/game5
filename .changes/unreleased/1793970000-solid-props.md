title: Signs, buildings and poles are solid
---

A skier no longer passes through what is built on the mountain. The steel struts under a lift station's hood, the corral poles, the lift signs, the start hut and start house, the cross gate, the finish arch with its floodlight masts and net posts, the ski jump's towers, piers, stairs and floodlight masts, the resort's buildings and props (picnic tables, deck chairs, parasols, ski racks, flags, toboggans), and the street's bus shelter, sign poles and snow poles all stop him now, where they are drawn.

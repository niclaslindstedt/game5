---
type: Changed
title: The wind tunnels have no top speed
---

A wind tunnel no longer holds a skier at its wind's speed. Its blowers push at full strength up to that speed and at a fixed power past it, so the push eases off the faster he goes but never stops: 150 km/h comes in about five seconds and a hundred metres, and a whole lane lets him go at 300 km/h and more.

**The snow shows its age.** The loose snow off the pistes is drawn as the days since the last fall have left it: new snow the cleanest, smoothest white with a fine dust of glitter; older snow a shade greyer with fewer, bigger glints; wind-packed slab rippled across the wind with scoured, polished patches; a glazed **sun crust** on the faces the noon sun stands on; **surface hoar** flashing like sequins in the shade; and, under a high spring sun, grey, glossy, sun-cupped wet snow. Looking into a low sun, the snow now **glares** — a broad sheen off powder and a hard streak off crust, ice and polished piste.

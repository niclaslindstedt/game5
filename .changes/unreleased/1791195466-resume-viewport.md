type: Fixed
title: The installed app fills its screen again after coming back from the background
---

Reopening the installed app on an iPhone, especially after turning the phone while the app was away, could leave the game squeezed into a sliver along the top of the screen over a plain sky-blue page until the app was restarted. The game now takes the whole screen whenever nothing is being typed into, and measures the screen again once the phone has settled after a resume or a rotation.

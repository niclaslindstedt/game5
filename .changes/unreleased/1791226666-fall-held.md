---
type: Changed
title: A fall is held long enough to see
---

When you are thrown off your skis, the game no longer cuts back to the piste after a second or two. The fall camera plays out over you, and you have time to read what the fall did on the body's plate. For the first three seconds nothing gets you up, not even the reset. After that, press W, press R or tap anywhere on the screen to get up, and the HUD says so. If you do nothing, you are stood up at six seconds. The fall camera climbs more slowly so you stay in the frame. Rivals get up as they did before.

The rider and gear cards stand the skier on the snow of a mountain instead of on a turning stand: on the rider card he waits idle in his boots, shifting his weight and looking about, and on the gear card he stands on the pair picked, which you can walk round by dragging across the picture. On a phone every menu card now fills the screen, and the rider and gear cards lay their head and sheet over the stage rather than scrolling a box.

The gondola is walked aboard: the skier steps out of his skis short of the station's door, carries them on his shoulder through the hall, stands them in the rack on his cabin's door and steps in on foot, and at the top carries them back out onto the pad and clicks back in. Nothing of him, and no ski, passes through the station's walls or his cabin's any more, and his own cabin is drawn to the same detail as every other cabin on the line — the shaped body, the roof and skirt, the racks, the door's rails, and a lined, floored and benched inside behind clear glass. A new lab, `make lift-path`, traces his path through both stations.

# Adding a real face: a real mountainside, named by its place

A REAL FACE is a 4×4 km stretch of a real ski mountain, from its summit
ridge down to its valley floor, turned so its fall line runs down the map
(`docs/real-faces.md` is the description; read it first). It carries two
GENERATED files of its own — its heights (`real-faces/face-<id>.ts`) and
its ski area's hints (`real-hints/hints-<id>.ts`), each listed in an index
beside them (`real-faces-index.ts`: its region and place;
`real-hints-index.ts`: the grades its pistes are signed and its woods by
height) — and both are
baked off ONE crop, so a face is first of all a row in
`scripts/lib/real-face-crops.mjs`. A face's files are FETCHED ONLY WHEN A
MAP IS RAISED ON IT (`loadRealFace`, a chunk of their own the service worker
keeps once fetched), so the bundle carries the indexes alone and a face
costs a player nothing until it is picked. This
skill is the list of what follows from that row, in order, with what each
step owes before the next. It routes to **`mapgen-improvement`** (how the
massif lays the relief, how the lifts and runs lean on the hints, the
rules a face is held to — `RR.massif.real`), **`buildings`** (the real
houses as stood, `engine/game/real-houses.ts`) and **`lift-areas`** (the
stations the leant lifts end in). Load **`write-code`** beside them and
**`skill-reflection`** at both ends.

**Read this skill's lessons first** —
`npx ogf-skill-lessons add-real-face --list`.

## The promises

- **NO DEALT MAP MOVES.** A face is read only after every draw off the
  stream, and the hints only where `level.face` is set, so a map with no
  face builds byte-identically. `make sim`'s digests are the proof — run it
  before and after and put both tables in the PR. A moved digest is a bug in
  the change, never a version row.
- **A FACE IS KEYED BY ITS REGION AND A NUMBER, AND NAMED BY ITS PLACE —
  NEVER BY A BRAND.** Its id (`<region>-<n>`) never moves. Its PLACE is
  three fields of its crop row: the `range` (a country's ISO code where the
  range's ski areas are all in one country, a key of its own across
  borders — named in `pwa/src/game/strings-ranges.ts`), the `area` (the
  town or mountain the ski area is known by) and the `part` of that area
  (the area itself where it is one part), written as the map writes the
  place, diacritics and all. Those two files and the generated face index
  are the ONLY places a real place is named: not a comment, a doc, a
  commit, a PR, a lesson or a lab picture's file name. No operator's or
  ski area's brand, lift, piste or race name enters the tree anywhere, and
  the bakes read no `name` tag — the place is looked up by hand (the map
  data's winter-sports area and the nearest village round the crop's
  middle) and written into the row. `tests/region_test.ts` scans every
  other file for place names.
- **A FACE READS ITS REAL ALTITUDE.** The map's heights are the face
  stretched to R25's vertical; every height above the sea the player reads
  is the elevation model's at that point instead (`pwa/src/game/altitude.ts`,
  BASE over the lowest lift's foot beside it). A face added needs nothing
  for it — the heights it is baked off are what is read — but a change to
  the face's window or its grid must keep a map point the real point.
- **NOTHING IS HAND-EDITED.** Every file under `real-faces/` and
  `real-hints/` and both indexes are generated by `--write`; a fix is a fix
  to the script and a re-bake. `--write` writes only the faces baked
  (`--only`) and keeps every other face's file byte-identical, so a new
  face never moves one already shipped — and the caches of the old ones
  need not be in hand.
- **THE LICENCES TRAVEL WITH THE DATA.** The heights carry the elevation
  model's notice, the hints the ODbL notice — both in the generated files'
  headers and `docs/real-faces.md`. Nothing fetched (`previews/.dem/`,
  `previews/.osm/`) is committed.
- **THE SIZE BUDGET.** Every ski area is to be imported in the end, each
  split into its parts, so a part must stay small. Measured over the forty:
  about 21.7 KB of heights (6.9 KB compressed) and 7.6 KB of hints (4.9 KB
  compressed) a part, fetched only when the part is picked; the bundle
  carries the INDEX alone — under 400 bytes of source a part (its place,
  its latitude and longitude, its grades, its nine bytes of woods and two
  loaders; under 100 bytes compressed). There is NO CAP on the count: the
  player downloads the index and the parts he picks, nothing more. A
  thousand parts is about 0.4 MB of index in the bundle and
  30 MB in the tree. If a new face's hints are far over the others', it is
  a crop holding a town — check the houses' count first: `real-houses.ts`
  stands EVERY one that fits (no tier is capped), so a big town is
  thousands of buildings, a few seconds of the placer's time and a valley
  of geometry the renderer must hold (measure them, below).
- **THE NEXT ONE IS THE NEXT ONE OVER.** The card lists a range's areas as
  a WALK (`face-picks.ts`'s `walk`): the most northerly area first, then
  always the nearest not yet listed, by the middle of its parts; an area's
  parts the same. The order is derived from the index's latitude and
  longitude, so a new face takes its place by itself — nothing to sort by
  hand. `tests/real_faces_test.ts` holds every hop to the nearest left and
  the walk no longer than the areas taken by name.

## The quality bar

A face is DONE when all of the following hold:

- **It is a skiing face.** The bake's table prints its `drop` (ridge row
  over the valley floor) and `falls` (the share of the playable face that
  falls down the map): the drop well over 250 m (`tests/real_faces_test.ts`
  holds it), the fall share near the other faces'. A crop that faces
  sideways reads as a traverse, never a mountain.
- **It builds, and builds quickly.** `make resort SEED=1 ARGS="--face=<id>
  --hints"` and a handful of other seeds: every one builds, in a couple of
  attempts (the twenty took 1.45 on average and ~8 s a face when this skill
  was written). A face that needs eight attempts is losing its hints
  (`real.hinted`) — find which rule refuses it (below), never widen a rule
  for every map.
- **The ski area reads like the real one.** In the resort lab's picture the
  generator's lifts sit on the magenta real lifts, its runs (thick) lie over
  the real pistes (thin, in their signed colour) and the houses stand where
  the grey ticks are. Measure it, do not eyeball it (below).
- **It skis.** `make build`, then a free ride on it looked at by hand
  (`?start=free&face=<id>`): the lift ridden up, a run skied down. The
  world and screenshot labs take no face yet — teaching them one is a
  `lab-tooling` change worth making the first time a face needs it.

## The loop

### 1. The crop

Add the row to `FACES` in `scripts/lib/real-face-crops.mjs`: the next free
`<region>-<n>` id, the region (R21 — pick the kind of country the mountain
IS, `add-region` describes each), its place — `range` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2,
or a key across borders such as `ALPS`, named in `strings-ranges.ts`),
`area` and `part` (another part of an area already listed takes that
area's spelling exactly; the start card groups by it) — the middle it is searched round (`lat`, `lon`
in degrees, the skiing face's middle — the lifts' middle, read off the map
data), and the crop: `east` / `north` (m, the window's middle off that
point), `bearing` (° clockwise from north the fall line runs down the map
on) and `scale` (real metres a map metre; 0.8–1.2 — under 1 the real face
is stretched, which steepens it). Keep a new face's middle 4 km or more
from every other's, or it is the same mountain twice.

Pick the bearing so the summit ridge is the map's TOP edge (z = 0) and the
valley floor its bottom, the lifts' bottoms near the bottom row: the
generator's village and lifts leave from the valley floor.
`make real-faces ARGS="--fetch --search --only <id>"` tries every bearing
(10° apart), offset (450 m apart, the middle kept well inside the window)
and scale (0.9, 1, 1.1) round the middle and prints the best four rows by
`drop` and `falls`; take the best whose window keeps the ski area on the
map (step 3's lifts and pistes say whether it does).

### 2. The heights

`make real-faces ARGS="--fetch --only <id>"` (fetches the tiles it lies in
into `previews/.dem/`, once) and read the table; iterate step 1 until it
reads like a mountain. Then `make real-faces ARGS="--only <id> --write"`
(the faces named, and the index; every other face's file is kept).
A change to a crop row's place alone (`range`, `area`, `part`) owes no
re-bake: `make real-faces ARGS=--index` rewrites the index from the rows
and the files already written, fetching nothing. Check the new face's grid decodes
(`npx vitest run tests/real_faces_test.ts`) and look at it: `make level
ARGS=--face=<id>` and `make resort ARGS=--face=<id>`.

### 3. The hints

`make real-hints ARGS="--fetch --only <id>"` (its heights must be written
first: the hints are laid on them; the map data into
`previews/.osm/`, a few tiles a face, politely — the editing API is shared;
never refetch what is kept), read its table (lifts, pistes, how many have
widths, houses, bytes) — a good face has three lifts and five pistes on the map or
more — then `make real-hints ARGS="--only <id> --write"`. What is kept
is `real-hints.ts`'s `RealHints`: every lift's two ends and kind, every
downhill piste's signed colour (orange for a freeride or extreme one, which
is kept but never laid — the game's own ski routes, R42, are laid on every
face whether the real area marks one or not), its line thinned to bends,
and its width at each bend where the map draws the piste's area; every
building's middle, size and bearing; and THE TOWN at the face's foot,
roughly (`scripts/lib/real-face-streets.mjs`: its car roads as a main road
or a street, joined where one street is mapped as many ways, thinned to
3 m, the most central first up to 90 streets or 1.8 KB, and its middle and
radius) — read the table's `streets` and `town b` columns: a face whose
town is a handful of streets is a crop with little town, which is fine; a
face at the 1.8 KB cap is a big town cut to its middle. Beside them, in the index, the WOODS
BY HEIGHT (`scripts/lib/real-face-forest.mjs`, `HINT_TREES`): the forest
the map draws rasterised onto the face's height grid, its cover in eight
bands and its tree line — read the table's `wooded`, `line m` and `bands`
columns against the real mountain's (a line far off the region's, or a
face hardly wooded where the real one is, is an unmapped forest: it is
kept as none under 1 % and the region's row stands in). The map call
leaves out a forest relation's members that lie outside the box; `--fetch`
fetches such a relation whole, once. The cache in `previews/.osm/` is
keyed by the face's id AND its crop, so a face re-cropped fetches afresh.
`--write --trees` writes the woods alone and keeps every hint file.
After the town, each hint file carries THE WATER
(`scripts/lib/real-face-water.mjs`): every lake, pond, reservoir and river
mapped as an area (a closed way or a multipolygon's rings, its islands as
holes, clipped to the window), thinned to 4 m on a 2 m grain, with the
lowest its real shore stands, at least 400 m² and the 40 largest; and the
30 longest rivers and streams mapped as lines, cut out of the bodies and
ordered downstream. Read the table's `lakes`, `streams`, `water b` and
`biggest m2` columns: a face is 0.1–2.1 KB of water, and a body of square
kilometres is a valley lake the window cut. A water relation the map call
left members of out is fetched whole with `--fetch`, as a forest's is.

### 4. The suite

`tests/real_faces_test.ts` counts the faces (`REAL_FACE_IDS` has the new
length), holds every face to a mountain and its hints to the map, every
face to a place (no two the same part) and every range to a name, the
woods to the relief, the RANGE, AREA and PART rows and the GRADE row's
filter; move its count, never loosen a bound. Then:
`npx vitest run tests/real_faces_test.ts tests/real_houses_test.ts
tests/resort_test.ts tests/region_test.ts tests/imports_test.ts
tests/file_size_test.ts` (and `tests/village_streets_test.ts
tests/traffic_test.ts` when the town's streets move). Look at the village
with `make resort ARGS="--face=<id> --hints --village"` (its
`-village.png`: the village's streets in dark grey under the real town's in
orange) and in the game's renderer with `make buildings
ARGS="--face=<id>"` or `npm run world -- --free --face <id>
--views=village-air,village-street,real-house,real-air` — `real-house` the
real building stood highest up the mountain, close, and `real-air` those
stood 60 m or more over the valley from the air: a face whose real huts
and hotels up the slope are missing from it is a placer refusing them
(`placeRealHouses`), not a bake.

### 5. Measure it, before and after

The numbers a PR owes, over the new face (and all faces when the change
touches how hints are used), on seed 1 and a few more:

- **attempts** and **seconds** to build (`generateLevel(seed, { face })`'s
  `level.attempt`, timed);
- **near**: the share of the generator's run points within ~40 m of a real
  piste (`resort.runs[].points` against `realHints(id).pistes`);
- **colour**: the share of runs laid on a real piste billed its signed
  colour;
- **refusals**: why attempts were refused. Set the framework's output sink
  with debug on (`@niclaslindstedt/oss-game-framework/core/output`) in a
  throwaway Node script under `scripts/` (run with `node
  --experimental-strip-types`, listed in `.git/info/exclude`, deleted when
  done) and count the `refused` lines by reason;
- **the water** (when the bake's water or how it is laid moves): the
  bodies and streams kept and their bytes, the largest body, how many lie
  on the mountain (60 m or more over the village) and how many below it,
  the bodies left out for want of flat ground, and the trees, towers,
  buildings and pads that stand in water with the guard off and on;
- **the town and the buildings** (when the village's place or the real
  houses move): the village's centre and the valley lift's foot against
  the real town's middle (`villageOf(level).centre`, `realHints(id).town`),
  and the share of the real buildings stood at their spot (a building
  within 8 m) in each of `realHouseTier`'s tiers — the mountain's, the
  town's and the valley's apart, since a town of a thousand houses hides
  a mountain whose dozen huts are all missing — and what standing them
  costs: `cabinsOf`'s seconds a face, the buildings and the walls' posts
  a map, and the world lab's draw calls and triangles (`npm run world --
  --free --face <id> --views=village-air --frames=10`) before and after.

Keep the before table, change one thing, re-measure. A rule that refuses
the real mountain over and over (a real face's pads are rougher, it has
fewer pistes than a dealt one, a real piste is signed by more than its
steepest pitch) is relaxed for faces ONLY, in `RR.massif.real.least`, with
the analyzer reading `level.face` and the generator `face` — never for a
dealt map.

### 6. The docs and the changeset

`docs/real-faces.md` — the face count by region and by range, the bake's sizes, any rule
relaxed and the measured numbers. A changeset fragment (`Added` for a new
face). The README and `AGENTS.md` only if a count they state moved.

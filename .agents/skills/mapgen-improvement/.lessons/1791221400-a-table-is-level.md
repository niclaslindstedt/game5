title: A built jump's table must be level in absolute terms, and its landing sized for the race's own air
date: 2026-10-05

A ski cross's jump written as `kickerProfile` over the course's line kept its table parallel to the falling slope, so the lip threw a racer almost flat: 0.2 s of air at 50 km/h, no flight off the lip at all. The jump is now its own profile — the ramp relative to the line, the table level, the landing dug back to the line. Then size it for the speed the bot carries and the race's 1.5 g flight gravity, not a real course's: a point mass flown over the profile (its impact against `harshSpeedOf`) found a short table and a 28–32 m landing that land 10–20 m/s clean; a real course's 15–35 m flights overshoot onto the flat in this air.

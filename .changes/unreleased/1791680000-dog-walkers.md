The village's households walk their dogs on the sidewalks of a free ride,
crossing a street only at a crossing with no car coming. Five kinds of dog,
built in the wildlife's faceted look and dressed in their own coats, collars
and sometimes a dog coat, walk, trot, sniff, mark, sit at the kerb and squat
to poop on a lead that sags when slack. Most piles are left on the sidewalk,
steaming for their first minute beside the yellow patches, and a few are
bagged by an owner who stoops for them.

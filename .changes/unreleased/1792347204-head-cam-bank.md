title: First-person views bank with the rider's head
---

The first-person views (the helmet and the look down past the skis) used to tip the horizon the opposite way to the rider's body. Under the paramotor's wing in a turn this swung your own arms and skis off across the screen, so the view did not feel like it was coming from your head. Now these views bank with your head: under the wing your skis hang straight below you through a turn, and on skis, the bike, the snowmobile and the piste machine the horizon leans the way you lean.

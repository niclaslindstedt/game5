type: Fixed
title: Smooth junctions
---

Where a transport lane leaves a piste or one run merges into another, the snow no longer breaks into a lip or a wall a skier is thrown off: a lane stays on the piste's surface while it runs on it and eases off it, and a wide run coming into a narrower one narrows to it and meets its surface as it falls (generator v8). The HUD's seed now carries where the skier stands on the map, X and Z in metres, so a screenshot names its spot.

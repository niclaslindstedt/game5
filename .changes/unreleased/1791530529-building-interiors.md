Every building in the ski area and the village is furnished inside: a restaurant's servery and long tables, the rental shop's ski and boot walls, a guest house's tiled stove and corner bench, the apartments' ski room, the church's pews, a hut's bunks and stove and more, each laid out to fit its own walls, windows and doors, lit warm inside and kept clear of the snow the building is dug into.

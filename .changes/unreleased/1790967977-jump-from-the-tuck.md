type: Fixed
title: Holding the jump in a tuck no longer stands you up
---

Holding the jump with the tuck held (W on the keys, the gas on a phone) no longer stands the skier up into a skid. A layout saved before the jump had its own key put Space on the skid too, so it did both. Now the jump takes its own keys back from older saved layouts. While a jump loads in the tuck he sinks lower on his knees, and he only rises into the jump when he isn't tucked.

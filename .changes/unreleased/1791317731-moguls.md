title: Moguls, the fourth freestyle contest, and the Ibex mogul ski
---

The tricks card now has MOGULS. The game builds a moguls course down the mountain: 235 m at 25°, a 10 m line of moguls three and a half metres apart between nine control gates, two air bumps and a finish area at the foot.

Turn on every mogul down the line and keep your speed to what your legs can take — they swallow the moguls up to about 35 km/h, and much faster the moguls throw you. Throw a trick off each air bump, a different one on each. A run is scored out of 100 the way the rules score it: the TURNS out of 60 by five judges, the AIR out of 20 (each jump times its degree of difficulty) and the SPEED out of 20 off your time against the pace. A qualification, then a final of sixteen and a final of six, nothing carried over. The HUD shows the phase, the pace time and the airs taken; the plate shows the three parts, each jump and the board.

A new pair joins the ski card: the IBEX, a mogul ski — short, narrow and nearly straight, soft at the tip — with a new BUMPS bar. Picking moguls opens the ski card on it at the medium build. `?start=moguls` boots straight into it.

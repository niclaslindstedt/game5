title: Skiing without poles
---

A hard mode on the DRESS card: POLES ▸ NONE sends the skier out with nothing in his hands, and the card says what it costs. With no poles there is no double pole — he skates at every speed on his legs alone, slower across the flat; a rise he would skate up on his poles is a crawl, and a steeper one beyond him; and with nothing to catch a lurch on he keeps less of his balance, so a hard landing, a caught edge or a trunk throws him sooner, and he rocks out of a bog in deep powder worse. He is posed for empty hands: his arms hang at his sides when he stands, his hands rest on his knees in the start gate, he holds them low and wide for balance as he rides and reaches for the snow inside a turn, swings them like a speed skater when he skates and like a walker when he sets off — and no pole plant anywhere, seen or heard. The time trial's ghost and the replay remember whether a run had poles. Every rival keeps his.

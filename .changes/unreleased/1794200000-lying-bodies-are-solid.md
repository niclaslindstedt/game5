title: People lying on the snow are solid
---

Someone already lying on the snow, knocked down, hurt or dead, is no longer something you ski or drive straight through. Your skis ride up over the body and lose some speed to it, and the body is pushed and dragged along. Catch a ski tip under an arm or a leg lying across your path at speed and you go over the front. The snowmobile rides over a body barely slowed. With INJURIES on, the weight of what goes over a body hurts it: a snowmobile breaks a person lying there and can kill someone already hurt at speed, and a piste machine or a car crushes them. With INJURIES off it is just the bump and the push.

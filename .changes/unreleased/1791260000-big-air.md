title: Big air

The front door's TRICKS tile now opens a tricks card: the park run as before, and BIG AIR, the first of the freestyle contests, with the others named as coming. A big air jump is cut into the mountain straight down the face — a start platform, a drop-in long enough to bring a tucked skier to the lip at about 60 km/h, a 26° kicker, a 16 m table and a landing shaped so a skier at that speed comes down softly. Each jump is one run: drop in, throw a trick off the kicker and land it. A panel of six judges scores it out of 100 on how hard the trick was, how clean the landing, the height and whether it cleared the table; the highest and lowest marks are dropped and a fall scores low whatever was thrown. A qualification of two jumps (the best counts) sends the best twelve of twenty-four to a final of three jumps, where the best two count and must be different tricks. The plate names the trick as a judge would call it, gives the score and shows the board. The contest is picked on any of the six trick maps, and `?start=bigair` boots straight into its first jump.

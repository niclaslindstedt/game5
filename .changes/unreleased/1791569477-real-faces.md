type: Added
title: Free rides on real mountainsides
---

A free ride can now be raised on one of twenty real mountainsides instead of a dealt one: eleven in the alpine, four continental, three maritime and two on the fells, each a 4×4 km stretch of a real ski mountain read off a satellite elevation survey. The start card's new SHAPE row picks one of the chosen country's faces (REAL 1, REAL 2, …), and a `?face=` link does too. The mountain keeps its real summit ridge, peak, spurs, gullies and the ground around the ski area. The lifts, the runs, the woods and the day are still the seed's own, built down it the same way as on any mountain.

title: The replay keeps you in the picture, close, with four more camera angles
---

The replay's cameras keep the skier in the picture. The broadcast camera no longer stands thirty metres off at the edge of the wood or hands over to a chase camera that stares into the slope with him somewhere behind it: between moments it cuts every few seconds between close lenses on him (low behind, alongside, ahead looking back, up over him), and for a jump, a crash, a pass or the finish it plants a lens a few metres beside where it happens and lets him arrive at it in slow motion. The camera button now walks those angles on their own (CLOSE, SIDE, FRONT, AERIAL) and the skier's own eyes. Every one of them aims at him, at his body once he is thrown off his skis, and stays close, lifted or swung to his other side so no slope or trunk stands between them.

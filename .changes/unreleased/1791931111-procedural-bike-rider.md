An optional new downhill bike and rider for summer rides, switched on with `?bikerig=ik` on the link. The bike is built in detail, with a dual-crown fork, a coil shock and knobbly tyres. The rider wears a full-face helmet, goggles, knee pads and baggy kit. Every frame his body is fitted to the bike as it moves, so his hands stay on the grips and his feet stay on the pedals. His hips move back and down when he shifts his weight or crouches, his legs fold on a landing, and he stays upright while the bike leans under him in a turn. Nothing is a canned animation. The old bike and rider stay the default.

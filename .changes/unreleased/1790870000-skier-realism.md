title: A more lifelike skier
---

The skier skis like a skier: his shins stay in his boots and lean with his skis, a carved turn is an inclined body hinged at the hips (no more sitting sideways over the outside ski) with his shoulders turned down the hill and his eyes held level, his back rounds in the tuck, he flies compact with his knees bent, he takes off and lands without a jerk, his hands close round the poles, and he breathes, shifts his weight and looks about while he waits at the start. His suit bends cleanly at the hips, knees, shoulders and elbows, carries its zips, pockets, seams and hem, his helmet's stripe has clean edges, his goggles a mirrored lens — and when he is thrown, his feet go with him instead of staying behind in the boots.

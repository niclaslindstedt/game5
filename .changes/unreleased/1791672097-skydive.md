title: Skydive out of the jump plane
---

Out of the jump plane's door a free ride becomes a skydive. ENTER (a double tap on touch) jumps, then opens the parachute, then cuts it away, and the HUD says which comes next. A `?chute=<m>` link starts you already out of the door that high.

- You leave the door laid onto the wind and fall belly to earth at about 200 km/h. The tuck dives head down, leaning forward tracks across the sky, leaning back slides you back and the edge turns you.
- The opening plays out over a few seconds: the pilot chute thrown, the bag lifted, line stretch, the slider coming down the lines and the cells filling from the middle out, with an opening shock of three to six g.
- The canopy is a nine-cell sport main flown on its toggles, brakes and risers. Flare it to land on your skis and it lets go by itself as you ski on.
- Cut away, the canopy crumples and streams down and you fall on; there is no reserve. Fly it into a tree or a lift's rope and you hang there under it until you start again in the door.
- The jumper wears a full harness and container and is posed through every stage with his skis on. In freefall a camera flyer follows him, HELMET is his own eyes and TIPS looks back past his skis; under the canopy the paramotor's views take over.
- It is heard: the roar of the freefall by body position, the opening's crack, the lines' hum, the brake lines and the canopy's flutter.

type: Changed
title: Snow lips over the rock
---

Where a face gets too steep for snow and the rock shows, the snow no longer fades into the stone in a soft smudge. At the top of a rock band the snow builds out over the edge in a ragged white lip, with the stone just under it in shade. At the bottom the snow banks up against the rock, the edge between snow and stone is crisp and broken, and snow lies in streaks down the rock. Your skis leave tracks on the lip and the banked snow, but still not on the bare stone.

**Smoother animation everywhere.** A wipeout no longer throws the skier's limbs half a metre in one frame as he goes from his skis onto the ragdoll and back. His fist no longer turns half round on the pole when his forearm swings through the pole's line. A snowboarder no longer hops on his board as he lands. A thrown skier's elbow no longer snaps across his arm. Getting on and off a snowmobile, a helicopter's skid, a balloon's basket and a lift no longer jumps his body. The free ride's crowd now blends between gliding, skating, stopping, queueing, a chair's load and unload, and a fall. The people on foot and their dogs blend between what they do. The grimbear and the animals blend between their moves, and the birds blend into a take-off. A new lab, `make jolt`, finds snaps like these in every animation.

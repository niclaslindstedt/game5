title: A landing no body could ride away now throws the skier
---

Landings are judged on how the skis come down, not only how hard. Bury the tips, land in the back seat, come down on one edge or sideways at speed and the skier goes over — the harder the landing, the truer it has to be, and a small hop forgives a little more. Landings taken anywhere near true, and the tips a little down onto a kicker's slope, are still ridden away.

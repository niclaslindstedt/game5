The jump plane's strip is laid properly on real mountains: it now keeps off every building, piste, lift, street and lake by their real size, clears the trees on its line instead of giving up on wooded valleys, and no longer falls back onto the town. Every real face gets a flat strip of its own beside the village, and finding it takes a fraction of a second instead of several. Crashing the plane no longer freezes the picture for seconds: the fire is made ready while the ride loads instead of at the moment of the crash, and a plane that lost a wing in the air no longer cuts its airframe up again as it goes in.

The camp fires and bonfires are drawn: a ring of stones with a teepee of burning logs, glowing embers and a sooty grill grate outside the huts, log benches and woodpiles round them, big log teepees burning on the valley floor, flickering flames, rising sparks and a thread of smoke drifting off on the wind over trampled, melted snow — and after dark each fire throws a warm, flickering light on the snow, the benches, the people and the trees round it.

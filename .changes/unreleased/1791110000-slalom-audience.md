title: A slalom watched the way a slalom is watched
---

A slalom's crowd now stands where a slalom's crowd stands: behind the spectator fence on both sides of the course, a couple of metres back from the safety nets, the whole way down — a thin line near the top, thickening toward the bottom, gathered at the hairpins and verticals and on the steepest pitch, and packed rows deep over the last gates above the finish arena's grandstands. The start is quiet: a few officials, coaches and fans beside and behind the start house, never in front of its door, and nobody stands in the run-out.

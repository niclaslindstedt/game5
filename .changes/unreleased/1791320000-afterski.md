title: Afterski lodges and the buzz
---

Every free ride's mountain now has an afterski lodge by the village on the valley floor, and most have a second part way down: a long log hall with a terrace of tables, parasols and string lights, and racks of skis and poles below it. Stop at its steps and press Enter (or double tap) to go in, where your skier dances and drinks a beer every so often among a crowd in their ski boots — the jump orders the next one sooner — and Enter takes you back out with your skis on. Every beer raises your BUZZ, shown as a filling beer glass on the HUD and wearing off over ten minutes: buzzed, the skis answer late, turn harder or softer than asked and drift off a straight line, and you fall far more easily. Through the first-person cameras the whole picture sways, swims and doubles. And a buzzed fall is not reset: you get up, walk to your skis, pick them up, step back in and ski on — steering yourself or letting him stagger there — with R still the way back to the piste.

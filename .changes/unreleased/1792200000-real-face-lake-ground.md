title: Real mountains' lakes lie flat on the snow
---

On the real mountains, a lake crossed by a run or lying beside a lift station could float metres above the snow, or have the snow poke up through it, because the runs and the stations were graded into the ground after the lake was laid. The ground under every lake is now levelled again once all the grading is done and eased into the shore, so a run crosses a frozen lake without a step. A lake that would have to move a station's pad, its ramps, or make a run steeper than it may be is left off the map instead. The village's streets, square and car park are now laid off the water wherever they fit.

title: A lighter frame on the snowmobile
---

Riding the snowmobile asks less of each frame. Its shadow is now seven draws instead of thirty-nine, so a frame on the ride makes about a tenth fewer draws in all. By day, the snow, the snow cloud it throws, the falling snow and every lit surface stop checking each of the night's six lamp slots. They stop at the first empty one. The picture is the same to the pixel.

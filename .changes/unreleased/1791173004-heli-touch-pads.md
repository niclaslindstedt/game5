title: The helicopter flies on two touch pads — the cyclic on the left, the collective and the pedals on the right
---

The helicopter's touch controls are two pads: the left thumb is the cyclic (drag up to tilt forward and fly ahead, down to tilt back, across to bank) and the right thumb works the collective (drag up to climb, down to sink, and it stays where you leave it) and the pedals (drag across to turn the nose), with a dead band so turning never creeps the height.

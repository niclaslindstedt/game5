title: A lit boarding ring at every lift's foot
---

On a free ride every lift now has a glowing amber circle on the snow where its queue starts, just past the open end of its roped corral. A column of light stands up out of it, so it can be spotted from up the hill. Ride into the ring, facing any way and at anything short of a schuss, and you are glided up the corral to the load line and put on the lift: sat on a chair, taken in at a gondola's door or handed a drag's bar. The old way still works too: ski slowly onto the load line facing up the lift.

title: The people on foot, their dogs and the skiing crowd use less memory
---
The people on foot and the dogs drew their figures from a copy each mesh kept, so the page held every figure twice. They now draw the one figure the game builds, which frees about 16 MB when a free ride starts and more as you come near people. Leaving a map, or the crowd changing at nightfall, also frees the GPU textures the skiing crowd's figures were leaving behind.

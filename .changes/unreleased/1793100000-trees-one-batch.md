The woods are drawn in one batch: every kind and cut of tree in a single draw for the picture and one for the shadows, culled a cell at a time. Near spruce and fir have jagged skirts with white shelves of snow on their boughs and green tips hanging below, every tree carries its own load of snow (more of it higher up the mountain) and its own shade of green, the crowns sway in the wind, and the forest past the draw distance is stippled into the snow as crowns rather than a flat tint.

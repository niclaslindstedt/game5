---
type: Removed
title: The campaign is gone
---

The CAMPAIGN is no longer part of the game: its tile, its shelves, its points, its table and its locks are gone, along with the finish plate's campaign lines and NEXT RUN, the `?menu=campaign` link, the developer page's UNLOCKS and the board the cloud save carried. Nothing locks any mode any more. RACE is now the front door's lit tile, and every race discipline is raced on its own nine race maps, and a tricks run on its trick maps, exactly as before.

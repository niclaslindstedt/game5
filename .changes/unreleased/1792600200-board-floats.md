title: A snowboard floats on deep powder instead of vanishing into it
---

In deep powder a snowboard is drawn riding at the snow's surface, at most a few centimetres under it at a crawl, and the trench it leaves is as shallow — where the board used to sink out of sight with the rider in snow to his shins. Hopping along at a crawl keeps it out of the snow too.

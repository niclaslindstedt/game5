# What is built

The catalog of every system the game has, one paragraph a system: what it is, the engine files and data it lives in, the app files that draw, hear and read it, its lab, and the skills that own it. [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) routes here instead of carrying it, because a session needs the paragraph for the system it is touching and none of the others. A system built adds its paragraph here; a system changed keeps its paragraph true in the same PR. What is NOT built (and the no-music rule) is stated in `AGENTS.md`.

The deeper page for a system, where one exists, is the `docs/<system>.md` its paragraph names.

**This repository is a VERTICAL SLICE.** The engine — the world generator, the skier on the snow, the course, the race, the bot — is built and green, and so is the first playable cut of the app around it: ONE generated MOUNTAIN per seed (in the alpine unless a REGION is asked for — below: a summit ridge along the top of the world, a valley floor along the bottom and a concave face between them standing 1420–1620 m over a valley floor 10–20 m above the sea (R25; the curated maps pinned to older generators keep their lower massif), ridged by spurs and gullies running down the fall line, rolled into rollers a skier takes air over and cut by headwalls and cliff bands, woods below a tree line that is an ALTITUDE with a band of krummholz under it, open bowls and powder above it), ONE PISTE graded down it — an open descent from a start gate near the summit to a finish straight on the valley floor, its heading wandering about the fall line with a few long traverses, never climbing, with crests on it that are jumps, drifts of fresh snow across it and the groomer's windrows along both edges, the start line standing four abreast behind the start gate — and GATES down it every hundred metres or so, red and blue alternating, the last of them the FINISH LINE under an arch in a fenced arena; SIX PAIRS OF SKIS in the engine's catalog — the Chamois (all-mountain), the Swift (slalom), the Chough (giant slalom), the Eagle (downhill), the Marmot (powder) and the Hare (a soft twin-tip for the park and the beginner), each a real class of ski with its numbers inside the class's measured bands and an answer to a kind of snow, each sold in ONE TOPSHEET of its own, the player's picked on the GEAR CARD after the RIDER card (the DRESS card: the body, the weight and the kit — the rider first, then what he rides): the skier stood on the pair on the snow of a mountain stage behind the card (the rider card stands him at ease in his boots on the same snow), drawn off its own spec and its class's traced look, with a spec sheet beside it and the POLES under it, and — where a run lets a free pick, a SKIS / SNOWBOARD switch over it — a rack of SIX SNOWBOARDS (`defs/boards.ts`: the Lynx all-mountain twin, the Pika beginner's board, the Stoat park twin, the Ptarmigan powder board, the Wolf big-mountain board and the Goshawk alpine carver, each a class's numbers and a topsheet of its own, `board-look.ts`), the free ride's START row's SLOPE ridden on whichever the gear card picks; and the slalom's field on the slalom pair — with the SKIER on them, dressed in an OUTFIT (below), standing on his bindings, knees bent by the legs, angulated inside a carve, tucked for speed and folded by a landing, poles in hand; FOUR MODES — the SLALOM (below: two runs against a field of thirty, one racer on the course at a time, the place on combined time), the DOWNHILL (below: a training run, then one race run down a black's whole course against a field of thirty, the place on time), the FREE RIDE (the whole mountain with the ski area's CROWD of amateurs on it — below — no lights, the gates counting nothing and no record kept, on a day and a depth of snow of the skier's own, begun seated on the chairlift up to the run nearest the spot picked and let go at its top, never led: every run off a top starts under it, a ramp falls from the pad's rim onto it and its sign stands at the ramp's head — and every lift on the mountain boarded by riding into its load zone: a chair's load line, a gondola station's door, a drag's track; the lifts always run) and TRICKS (below), every race filed in a RECORD BOOK — the best time per seed, skis and mode, with the split's gap to it at every gate; a winter sky whose sun starts at the seed's hour and moves with the run's own clock (five minutes on the snow an hour of sun — `SKY_CLOCK`, `?skyclock=`), on the seed's day, at its latitude, under one of eight WEATHERS the seed deals — the bright ones most days (R19: clear, fair cumulus, flurries out of a sunny sky, high cloud, the flat light of an overcast, a steady snowfall, a storm under black cloud that closes the view to tens of metres with spindrift off the crests, a valley fog) — whose fall comes and goes in squalls and LAYS NEW SNOW at a real fall's rate (a storm's hand an hour), burying the groomer and deepening the powder for the physics and the picture alike, and — on a free ride — a PISTE THROUGH THE DAY (`piste-day.ts`): the night's corduroy whole at the first chair, skied up into scrapes, streaks and heaps through the afternoon, the day's fall skied into it as soft heaps — never a grip lost to the hour, no slush and no evening freeze, so an evening run keeps its traction — and on a quarter of the maps from sunset into the NIGHT — the moon, the stars and the Milky Way, FLOODLIGHT MASTS down every run (about fifty metres apart, ten to eighteen metres tall, lighting the piste to some 30 lx and leaving the woods dark), the finish arena's FLOODLIGHTS on the snow and a HEADLAMP on every skier's helmet (a narrow spot and a wide flood round it, turned with his head, lighting the snow, the woods, the falling snow and the powder he skis into); a snow shader (the glitter, the blue in the shadows, the groomed piste's corduroy, the windrows' clods) and snow that DEFORMS — every skier's two skis stamped at three stations each into a world-space trail map, an edged ski cutting a narrower, deeper groove, so the tracks persist; the spray and the snow CLOUD a skier raises (the sheet of snow off a skidded turn, the powder off the tips and the tails, a rooster tail that stalls, swells, drifts and hangs, lit by the sky and shading itself, glowing against a low sun), both read off SIX KINDS OF SNOW — groomed, hard crust, soft powder, new snow, wet spring snow and ice — that also shape every groove and every print; a ladder of six cameras (TIPS, HELMET, CHASE, FAR, HIGH, ORBIT; the replay's own five beside them — the BROADCAST, CLOSE, SIDE, FRONT and AERIAL); the synthesized sound (the wind rising with speed and the tuck, the edges' hiss, chatter and scrape, the powder's whoosh, the poles' clicks at a crawl, every landing, the start wand, the finish horn and the cowbells); a HUD (speed, the height above the sea, position, gates taken of the total, the run clock, the split, the air clock, the EDGE bar with the skid's word on it, the missed-gate arrow, the news column, the finish plate, and a heading-up MINIMAP of the piste, the field and the gate owed), keyboard and touch; and the shell — an attract card, a front door with a RACE tile as its lit tile opening the race card, one box a discipline (each on a pinned map picked on a level card), each opening the ski card, and a FREE RIDE tile that opens the WHERE steps first — a WORLD MAP flown onto the range picked, a marker on every real ski area where it lies, then MOUNTAIN where an area has more than one (`menu-where.tsx`, `where-steps.ts`, `world-map.ts`, the generated outline `world-map-data.ts`) — and then a START CARD (the map's seed with a chart of it — the piste, the start line and every kicker — tapped to start anywhere, the date, the hour, the snow's depth, WHERE — GENERATED (its country and its number) or a real RANGE, AREA and PART — the RUN — any slope of any colour — and the START: on skis or on a machine, also on the pause card's PISTE MAP), OPTIONS (the picture as eight ladders applied live over the run, with a first-visit probe that picks the rung this machine can hold; three sound faders; the keys rebound; the thumbs' side, travel and lean; how much the skis help), a loading card, a pause card, and the REPLAY — every slalom, downhill and tricks run recorded as the controls that skied it, watched back from the finish plate or the pause card with the field skied again beside it, on a BROADCAST camera that cuts between close lenses on the skier and plants one beside each moment the run marked, running it in slow motion, or on CLOSE, SIDE, FRONT and AERIAL lenses picked on the bar — every one aimed at him (his body once he is thrown) and kept close, over the snow and with nothing between; and the SHUTTER — P (or the desktop menu bar's row, or the phone's own screenshot in the store app) photographs the run with the HUD rasterized in and the mark stamped in the corner, H takes the readouts down for a clean frame, and a GALLERY chip on the front door opens the roll of the last forty (IndexedDB) to flip through, share, copy, save or delete. Around THAT, both platform shells are built: `tauri/`, the desktop app, and `native/`, the store app.

**The slalom is built too:** the race is a real discipline now, the SLALOM (R31) — the old line-start race against three rivals is `fieldRules`, what a run naming no mode is dealt (the suite, the labs, the benchmark), and no mode the player picks. A slalom is SET over a built map (`engine/mapgen/slalom.ts`'s `setSlalom`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves): the steepest stretch of the piste that drops 140–220 m, netted, its kickers levelled and its trees cleared, a START HOUSE over its top with a START DROP out of the door, POLE GATES down it (open gates, hairpins, verticals, delays — flex poles a racer knocks down and that spring back, `gate-poles.ts`) and the finish arena on its run-out; a seed of its own is built to a red. ONE RACER IS EVER ON THE COURSE: the field of 29 is never skied but DEALT as a board off the seed about the course's par (`field.ts`, `par.ts`), and the player races its times. The start as television shows it (`camera-start.ts`: overhead, then behind him in the house), the starter's READY and GO, the start push — the tuck throws him out through the wand in one push (`start-push.ts`, the clip `slalom-start.ts`), and no skating after — the clock started at the wand and a ten-second window after GO; the STRICT gates (`strict.ts`): both feet through every gate, a miss, a straddle or a skipped gate a DSQ, a fall or a reset a DNF (`Progress.out`); the HUD's RUN and its splits against the leader at two timing points, the BOARD on the plate (`slalom-board.ts`, `hud-board.tsx`), and the SECOND RUN — set afresh, the first run's top thirty in reverse order, the final on combined time (`slalom-heat.ts`, `CreateGameOptions.heat`); the audience behind the nets on both sides, thickest at the finish (`spectator-slalom.ts`) (`engine-system`, `collision`, `hud-and-menus`, `pinned-maps`, `crowd`).

**The downhill is built too:** the second race discipline, the DOWNHILL (R32) — the fastest race there is, raced on the Eagle at well over a hundred kilometres an hour. A downhill is SET over a built map (`engine/mapgen/downhill.ts`'s `setDownhill`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves) on the WHOLE piste of the ski area's course with the most vertical (`downhillCourseOf`), over the preparation the slalom shares (`course-prep.ts`'s `prepareCourse`: the start drop, the stretch combed and groomed, the trees cleared — and the CRESTS SHAVED so a jump lands on the downslope); a RACING LINE relaxed to the least curvature inside the course's corridor (`speed-course.ts`'s `speedLineAt`, its signed bend), SPEED GATES on it every ~80 m (`Checkpoint.panels` — two poles and a panel, wide, never on a jump's lip) held by the same strict gates, A-NETS down both sides that catch a racer and put him out (`nets.ts`, a DNF `net`) — driven in hard, he goes down INTO them (the `net` wipeout): the mesh billows round him, drops him to its foot and keeps him, and his skis hook in it more often than not (`catchInNets`, the drawn net bulging round them — `net-bulge.ts`), a SPEED TRAP on the fastest long straight (`speed-trap.ts`, `Progress.trap`) and four intermediates. ONE RUN, after a TRAINING run that counts for nothing (`Field.training`, `CreateGameOptions.training`, `downhill-run.ts`); the field of 29 dealt about the course's par (`field.ts`'s `DOWNHILL_FIELD`, `par.ts`'s `downhillPar`: a forward pass of the speed down the line, capped by what the pair can hold round each bend), a speed through the trap dealt to each; the bot follows the line (`sim/downhill-steer.ts`) on the downhill technique, cut hard. The HUD's race chip, splits and plate are the slalom's, shared as `snapshot.ts`'s `RaceHud` (`raceOf`) with the trap beside them; `mapgen/race-course.ts`'s `raceCourseOf` is the one question "which race is set on this map" the nets, the start house and the audience ask (`collision`, `engine-system`, `hud-and-menus`, `pinned-maps`).

**The super-G is built too:** the third race discipline, the SUPER-G (R33) — the speed event whose gates TURN the racer, one run skied unseen (no training: the course is inspected, never skied), on the Falcon (the super-G pair, built to the class's rule: 210 cm, 45 m, 94/65/79 mm). A super-G is SET over a built map (`engine/mapgen/super-g.ts`'s `setSuperG`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves) on the downhill's hill — the ski area's course with the most vertical (`superGCourseOf`) — from a START LOWERED down it until the drop to the finish is 600 m at the most (`superGStart`), prepared as a downhill's (`prepareCourse`, its crests shaved for a super-G's speed); its JUMPS its drops and the crests a racer leaves the snow on; ~42 gates on 600 m (7 % of the vertical, ~49 m apart, a rhythm dealt off the seed), each two panelled pairs red and blue in turn (`Checkpoint.panels` with a `turn`), its turning pole inside the apex of a RACING LINE that is the downhill's least-bending line SWUNG to each gate's side in turn; the A-nets, a speed trap and three intermediates. What it shares with the downhill is one module: `mapgen/speed-course.ts` (`speedCourseOf` — the one question "which speed course is on this map" the nets, the trap, the bot and the par ask — `racingLine`, `speedLineAt`, `trapArc`, `gateArcs`). The field of 29 is dealt about par (`field.ts`'s `SUPER_G_FIELD`: wider apart and oftener out than a downhill's; `par.ts`'s `superGPar`, the downhill's forward pass under the super-G technique); the bot is the downhill's line-follower read closer (`SUPER_G_STEER`) on the super-G technique (`SUPER_G_TECHNIQUE`, its edge held at speed). Its NINE PINNED MAPS are its own (`pwa/src/game/race-maps.ts`'s `RACE_MAPS.superG`, all open, held by `tests/race_maps_superg_test.ts`), picked on its level card off the RACE CARD (`menu-races.tsx`) the front door's one RACE tile opens (`collision`, `engine-system`, `bot-improvement`, `hud-and-menus`, `menu-system`, `pinned-maps`).

**The giant slalom is built too:** the sixth race discipline, the GIANT SLALOM (R36) — the technical race between the slalom and the speed events, ~20 m turns at 60–80 km/h, TWO RUNS on two courses set on the same hill, raced on the Chough (the giant slalom pair, built to the class's rule: 1.93 m on a 30 m sidecut). A giant slalom is SET over a built map (`engine/mapgen/giant-slalom.ts`'s `setGiantSlalom(level, run)`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves) on the super-G's hill — the ski area's course with the most vertical (`giantSlalomCourseOf`) — from a START LOWERED down it until the drop to the finish is 400 m at the most, inside the top level's 250–450 m (`giantSlalomStart`), prepared as a super-G's; its gates ~13 % of the vertical in direction changes, 18–42 m apart down the hill (27 aimed at, a rhythm dealt off the seed), each two panelled pairs 6 m wide red and blue in turn, the line passing 3 m outside the turning pole and swung 2.5–4.5 m either side of the least-bending line, never tighter than a 22 m bend; nets, two intermediates and no speed trap. It is the super-G's course with a technical race's numbers: both are set by ONE module, `mapgen/turn-course.ts` (`mostVerticalCourseOf`, `loweredStart`, `turnCourse` — the start house, the preparation, the line swung half a cosine from apex to apex round the gates, the nets), a discipline's salt its only difference, and `super-g.ts` is thin over it; the giant slalom's row alone carries `towers`, which holds the line 4 m off any lift tower standing on the piste, eased over 30 m (so no super-G moved). The SECOND RUN is set afresh on the same stretch off a salt of the run's own (`Level.giantSlalom`'s `run`), skied by the first run's best thirty in reverse order, the final on combined time, as the slalom's (`slalom-heat.ts`'s `twoRunMode`). The field of 29 is dealt about par (`field.ts`'s `GIANT_SLALOM_FIELD`: closer than a slalom's, out of a run a tenth to a quarter of the time; `par.ts`'s `giantSlalomPar`, the downhill's forward pass down the swung line under the giant slalom technique, scaled 1.11 by the bot's own median); the bot is the super-G's line-follower read closer still (`GIANT_SLALOM_STEER`, `steerOf`) on `GIANT_SLALOM_TECHNIQUE`, taking each turn at 0.85 of the grip it could cut. Its NINE PINNED MAPS are its own (`RACE_MAPS.giantSlalom`, all open, held by `tests/race_maps_giantslalom_test.ts`), picked on its level card off the race card; `?start=gs` boots straight into its first run, `?run=2` its second; its words are `strings-giantslalom.ts` (`collision`, `engine-system`, `mapgen-improvement`, `bot-improvement`, `hud-and-menus`, `pinned-maps`).

**Speed skiing is built too:** the fourth race discipline, SPEED SKIING (R34) — straight down the fall line as fast as a body can go, the result a SPEED. Its course is not on the piste: a TRACK OF ITS OWN cut straight down the face (`engine/mapgen/speed-ski.ts`'s `setSpeedSki`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves) — a line searched over every column of the face and a few bearings either side of the fall line, its profile combed, never rising, its crests cut and its knees filled round so nothing at 200 km/h leaves the snow or is slammed into it, graded 30 m wide with a 25 m safety margin cleared either side; a launch of 300–900 m, a 100 m TIMING ZONE, a run-out sized to stop a racer stood up into the wind (its BRAKING LINE, then a skid) and a finish enclosure at its foot (`speedSkiLines`), the speed each track is built for dealt off its seed in the top class's band. Raced on the PEREGRINE (the speed ski: 2.40 m, under 10 cm wide, next to no sidecut, under a racer in an airtight suit and calf fairings — a tuck of 0.08 m², `defs/skis.ts`) with the speed skier's technique (`SPEED_SKI_TECHNIQUE`, 20° of edge at most). TWO RUNS: a QUALIFICATION from a start lowered 12 km/h down the track, then the FINAL from the top for the best 20, slowest first, ranked on the final alone (`field.ts`'s `SPEED_SKI_FIELD`, `slalom-heat.ts`'s `twoRunMode`). The clock runs between the zone's two lines only, each crossing read to the fraction of a step (`strict.ts`, `course.ts`'s `crossingShare`) — the speed is the zone over that time, and the HUD, the plate and the board read every time as a speed (`speed-ski-run.ts`); par is the clean run itself, SKIED in a full tuck in the race's weather (`par.ts`'s `speedSkiPar`); past the zone the racer UNTUCKS, rides the wind and skids only past the braking line (`run.ts`'s `runOut`); the bot holds the middle (`sim/speed-ski-steer.ts`); the jury's anemometer stands at the zone (`jury.ts`). Its NINE RACE MAPS (`RACE_MAPS.speedSki`, 187–225 km/h finals, `tests/race_maps_speedski_test.ts`) (`collision`, `engine-system`, `ski-physics`, `bot-improvement`, `hud-and-menus`, `pinned-maps`).

**The ski cross is built too:** the fifth race discipline, the SKI CROSS (R35) — four racers on the course at once, out of one start gate and down a course BUILT in the snow. Its course is its own line weaving down the gentlest long stretch of the piste (`engine/mapgen/ski-cross.ts`'s `setSkiCross`, its rule in `discipline-rules.ts`, so no digest moves; a seed of its own takes the ski area's course `skiCrossCourseOf` picks): a start straight, legs swung either side of the piste's line, every corner rounded into a turn and the big ones BANKED into BERMS, ROLLERS, table JUMPS and STEP-DOWNS built on the straights, a start platform with a START RAMP below four DOORS (`Level.grid` the lanes, the first turn's inside chosen first), a FENCE along both edges (`netsOf`, caught like a downhill's A-nets) and triangular GATE FLAGS — a turning gate on every berm's inside, a corridor gate before every feature (`Checkpoint.flags`, judged by `strict.ts`, a gate missed a DNF). Raced on the WOLVERINE (the ski-cross pair: a giant-slalom-type ski cut down, 1.88 m on a 24 m sidecut, `defs/skis.ts`) with the ski-cross technique (`SKI_CROSS_TECHNIQUE`). THE FORMAT (`engine/game/cross-bracket.ts`, pure): a timed QUALIFICATION alone against a board dealt about par (`field.ts`'s `SKI_CROSS_FIELD`, `par.ts`'s `skiCrossPar` — the bot's own clean run), then a BRACKET of heats of four — quarter-finals seeded 1-8-9-16, semi-finals, the small final and the big final — the first two of each heat through, a heat the player is not in dealt off the race's seed, the final ranking the bracket's. A HEAT is SKIED (`engine/game/cross-heat.ts`): "skiers ready", "attention", the doors dropping at a moment dealt 1–4 s later (`crossCountdown`, `RunRules.start` "gate"), the pull out of the gate (`start-push.ts`), three of the start list beside the player in their lanes, their paces their skills, each holding and passing in a lane of his own (`crossLane`), a share of his drag taken off tucked in behind a rival (`stepDrafts`, `SkierState.draft`, read by `air.ts`); CONTACT is judged (`RunRules.knock`, `judgeContact`): a shoulder hard enough puts a racer down, and a knock-down from behind is a RED CARD for the racer behind (`RunOut.why` "contact"). The race rides the run as `GameState.bracket` (`CreateGameOptions.bracket`); the app's side is `ski-cross-run.ts` (the HUD's round and the gate's commands, the plate's next heat, `nextBracket`), `hud-cross.tsx` (the plate) and `strings-skicross.ts` (`collision`, `engine-system`, `mapgen-improvement`, `bot-improvement`, `hud-and-menus`, `pinned-maps`, `crowd`).

**Piste grades are built too:** every map the current generator (v2) builds is built to a PISTE GRADE (R23, `engine/mapgen/grades.ts`) — GREEN, BLUE, RED or BLACK, asked for (`?grade=`, a pinned map's own — the free ride's start card asks none: its RUN row picks any slope of any colour) or dealt off the seed — its steepest hundred metres held inside the colour's band (a green's 16 %, a blue's 27 %, a red's 47 %, a black past that), the mountain's vertical, fall line, width, kickers, cliffs and grooming the grade's own; a BLACK drops off the start hut onto its pitch and carries DROPS across the piste (R24, `drops.ts`) and cliffs beside it; the face is turned to the sun (R15). Past black is ORANGE (R42, `engine/mapgen/ski-routes.ts`, generator v9): the SKI ROUTE, never a piste — up to two lines a map FINDS on the finished mountain off a chair's or gondola's top, steeper than a groomer works (past R8's 78 %, to 110 %), never over a cliff, open of trees and down onto a run, moving no ground and packing no snow, so never groomed (`Resort.routes`, kept out of `resort.runs` so no machine, gun or amateur touches one); marked in orange stakes down both sides, signed SKI ROUTE · NOT GROOMED at its head with an orange double diamond, pointed at from the pad by the locals' homemade board (`route-sign-plan.ts`, `route-sign.ts`: a scrap plank hacked to a point and nailed crooked to a stick, painted by hand, `route-amateur` its world-lab view), dashed orange on the start card, the board and the minimap, and started on by asking for ORANGE (`RunGrade`, `courseGrade`); `make world ARGS=--views=route-head,route-in,route-steep,route-side,route-air` is its lab. The grade is signed the way a ski area signs it (a green circle, a blue square, a red rectangle, a black diamond — `grade-look.ts`, `grade-mark.tsx`) on the cards, the HUD and the piste's own edge poles (`mapgen-improvement`, `pinned-maps`).

**Pinned maps are built too:** nothing is locked — every race discipline is raced on its own NINE RACE MAPS (`race-maps.ts`) a TRICKS run on its trick maps (`trick-maps.ts`) and a ski jumping contest on its own hills (`jump-hills.ts`), each a seed on a generator VERSION with a digest the suite holds it to (`pinned-levels.ts`, `pinned.ts`), chosen because its course makes a good race of it, all open — so a record is a record down a piste anyone can ski; a seed of one's own is the free ride's and a link's, and the pause card names a measured run's seed, country and grade so its mountain can be free-ridden (`pinned-maps`, `level-rating`).

**Crashes are built too:** a trunk met hard, a landing over the tips, a fall at speed or an EDGE CAUGHT (the sideways slip at a contact past what an edge that far over can hold — the high-side) throws the skier off onto a body of his own — a ragdoll, jointed like a body, that goes over, is dragged flat by the snow (at once in deep powder) and lies in it, the skis carrying on down the slope without him — until the reset stands him back up; a skier stopped in deep powder sinks to the knees, BOGGED, and is poled and rocked back out (the tuck, the lean and the edge); in deep powder every trunk stands in a TREE WELL — a funnel of loose snow, deepest at the trunk and opening downhill — that a skier who comes too close slides into and is held in; and, behind an OPTIONS switch that is off by default, a blow dulls an edge or hurts the legs and the skier rides it for the rest of the run, read off a HUD instrument (`crash`).

**Gear is built too:** the skier is DRESSED — an OUTFIT off a catalog (`outfit.ts`): a BODY (a man or a woman, each the anthropometric survey's mean measure on the one skeleton every pose and the ragdoll share, a woman's braid out from under her helmet), a WEIGHT (four builds — light, medium, solid, heavy — the one row the ENGINE skis too, `engine/game/defs/riders.ts`'s `withRider`: a heavier skier faster in a tuck, slower to skate up to speed, landing harder and shouldering harder, cut to the build by the dress — broader, a solid one through the chest and a heavy one with a stomach; the medium build is every pair's own row, handed back to the bit), a JACKET (a race shell, a down puffer, a freeride shell cut long, an anorak, a retro one), PANTS (insulated, a race softshell, baggy, cargo), a HELMET (race, freeride, visor, slalom with its chin guard), GLOVES (gauntlet, mittens, undercuff, race) and POLES (alloy, carbon, a downhill racer's bent pair, powder baskets), every piece SOLD IN ITS OWN COLOURS and picked on the DRESS card behind the ski card's CUSTOMIZE SKIER press; each rival wears his own, his jacket in his slot's colour. Every piece is CUT IN CODE onto the rig in its bind pose and weighted across its bones (`dress-loft.ts`), so the cloth bends with every pose as one skin and nothing is drawn under it; `make gear` is the lab (`skier-gear`).

**The body is built too:** every blow a skier takes — a landing, the hips, the shoulders or the helmet on the snow, every point of the ragdoll meeting the snow or a trunk, a rival's shoulder — is a STOP over the body's own give and the snow's (ice, the groomer, loose snow by its depth — fitted to measured head drops on snow), its peak in g; twenty parts each carry a LADDER of injuries ranked on the Abbreviated Injury Scale (a sprained thumb, a torn cruciate, a bruised kidney, a broken collarbone, a concussion…), each an even chance at a dose of its own mechanism (a blow, a landing's load, the back seat's drawer, a caught edge's twist, the boot-top lever) on a log-logistic risk curve drawn off a hash, never the stream; the whole body summed as an INJURY SEVERITY SCORE; every fracture names the BONES it cracks (a HAIRLINE) or breaks, thirty of them from the skull to the feet. A readout — nothing in the physics reads it — and a RESET MENDS IT, except with the INJURIES switch on, where he is stood back up as hurt as he lay, where he left the piste and facing down it, and SKIS HURT (`hurt.ts`, `defs/hurt.ts`: the leg on the outside of a turn standing the skis over less and tipping them slower, each ski's edge holding by its own leg, the turn-in slowed by the head, the drive by the arms, the tuck and the landings by the legs and the trunk — each a share of a part's worst AIS, never below a floor) until the injuries he keeps adding up kill him and the run starts again from the top; an ARM BROKEN through DROPS ITS POLE (one broken, he rides with the other; both, with none) and hangs from the break — the upper arm's lower half, the elbow and the forearm off a broken humerus, the forearm's far half off both its bones, the hand off a broken wrist — a pendulum swung by his own acceleration, the rig cut at the break (`skier-broken.ts`). Drawn as an ANATOMY PLATE at the HUD's left edge, MADE FROM A WHOLE 3D BODY — one man's CT, every bone and the skin a mesh of its own (BodyParts3D, fetched by `make hud-body` and never committed), the feet turned so the toes show, the trunk's bones cut along the spine — seen from the FRONT (a BACK view beside it, generated and off by default) and traced into the generated `body-model.ts`: the skin's silhouette the outline, the twenty parts cut at the seams read off his bones, each part's flesh painted by its worst injury that is not a fracture or an organ's, and over it every BONE and every ORGAN as it lies, lit and shaded, in the order its depth says (`body-figure.ts`) — the brain, the heart, the lungs, the liver, the spleen, the stomach, the bowel, the kidneys and the bladder traced off the same body INSIDE the bones that hold them (the skull's cap lifted off the brain, the ribs over the lungs), each in its own soft colour while sound and painted yellow, orange or red by its worst injury (`organsOf`: an injury names its ORGANS, a paired one on the side the blow came from — bruised, torn and ruptured, from a blow or a landing's deceleration), a hurt one lying mostly behind another dashed in over it — the bone itself fractured, never a mark drawn on it: bone grey and whole when sound, yellow with a fissure cut into it on a hairline, red and broken through on a break — a long bone BETWEEN ITS JOINTS, each piece turned about its own joint end so the joints stay where they are and the ends at the break are shoved past each other (`fractureOf`), the pieces SNAPPING apart and the bone flashing white as it breaks (`body.css`'s `.hud-bone-move`) — and a break BREAKS BY THE ENERGY THAT DID IT (`Injury.energy`, raised by every harder blow after it): simple, a WEDGE with a butterfly fragment knocked out, or SHATTERED into pieces past 2.3 times the energy that breaks it (`injury.comminute`, the pendulum study's), every piece thrown the further the harder he was struck; the lines under it name only what the bones cannot show (the organs, the ligaments, the sprains); the plate is HALF ITS SIZE while he is sound and grows with his injury severity score to its full size (`body-tile.ts`' `scaleOf`), and it SHAKES with every blow he goes down on, as a shaking G METER over the skier does; **Gore and death are built too, behind the same INJURIES switch:** a run with it on (`injuriesShown` → `CreateGameOptions.gore`; off, `GameState.gore` does not exist and no digest moves) carries the MORTAL WOUNDS (`engine/game/gore.ts`, its numbers `defs/gore.ts`): a blunt dose far past a fracture's TEARS A PIECE OFF (the head, an arm or a forearm, a leg or a shin, the body torn in two at the waist — the grimbear's catch always that), CRUSHES THE SKULL, OPENS THE CHEST or the belly, and a body coming down onto a tree's bare top or a steel post's is RUN THROUGH and held on it (`Thrown.pin`) — only his trunk is (a limb or the head is torn open on the tip and bleeds, `Gash`), and down a tree's stem he SINKS until the trunk holds him, the tip he was dragged down red with his blood (`gore-tree.ts`); a working PISTE MACHINE that runs over him tears off every piece its belts and tiller pass over and spits it out behind (the dead as well), and the BLAST of a helicopter he rides into the snow BLOWS HIM APART, limbs, head or lower half dealt off a hash and flung off his middle; a body SMASHED on a rigid solid — a trunk, a boulder, steel (a lamp's column, a floodlight mast, a lift's tower), a wall or a crag's bare rock, a lift tower's pad giving only its foam's 11 m/s, never the snow — while going faster than 100 km/h is CRUSHED where it hit (`gore-smash.ts`, `GORE.smash`, the speed from forensic series of falls and collisions): the head bursts off the neck, a shoulder stoves in the chest, a hip bursts the belly, a hand, elbow, knee or foot is PULPED to the joint above (`GoreState.pulped`, `TornPiece.pulped` — meat and splinters, never a limb flown whole), met SQUARE ON the MIDDLE is crushed — the chest and the belly burst together, some two litres of the blood in them out at once (`GoreState.burst`, three to four of his 5.6 litres all told) and flung on past the solid onto the snow, the arms and the legs torn off at the shoulders and the hips and gone on the way he was going (`GoreState.middle`) — and each is SMEARED on the solid's face (`GoreState.smears`, drawn by `gore-smear.ts`: a ragged blot of blood with the brain, the meat, the bowel or the bone pressed into it and runs down the bark under it); The HEART races as the blood goes and pumps a torn artery out in beats (`GoreState.pulse`) only until the artery's SPASM closes it — a spurt halved in seconds to an ooze that clots (`woundFlow`, `GORE.blood.spasm`) — on a clock shortened for play; a PART HIT HARD bleeds without a mortal wound (`bleedsOf`: a blunt blow far past an injury's even chance splits the skin and the part bleeds OUT, a torn organ or a closed break bleeds INSIDE, each clotting, a reset's mending stopping it), and only what leaves through the skin reaches the snow (`GoreState.out`, `.shed`) — a body torn open goes on emptying onto it after the heart stops; he DIES at once of the worst, or bleeds out, or lies still — and is never stood up: the HUD's glass, jolted by every blow, fades its readouts slowly away, DIED comes up with the cause, the picture goes dark and the run starts again from the top, a new rider (`hud-wreck.ts`, `hud-glass.tsx`, `wreck.css`, `App.tsx`'s `deathOver`). DRAWN all in code (`gore-view.ts`): the torn piece a second skin of the same outfit cut at the joint (`gore-cut.ts`) — only the triangles its own bones move, cut ahead at load — and flown as a stick on the snow (`gore-gibs.ts`), and what is thrown out of him instanced by shape and baked into one mesh once it lies still (`gore-lumps.ts`), every program a hit draws with compiled behind the loading card, raw stumps with the bone in them, the organs, ribs and bone shards out of an opened trunk and the bowel on a rope, a crushed skull's brain, the spike through him, long bones broken out through the skin; the blood POURS as a jet only while it flows fast (a spurting artery, a couple of millilitres a second and up), and the jet holds together only a hand's breadth out of the wound before it breaks into DROPS strung along its way (`gore-blood.ts`'s `JET`, a spurt's arc a line of drops, never a thread), the air past a body thrown through the air breaking it sooner and carrying the drops out BEHIND him (`gore-gibs.ts`'s `streamPath`); below that it leaves a wound or a sodden gap as drops alone (`gore-flow.ts`'s `STREAM`), never thrown far, as the bowel streams behind him on its rope and tears when jerked — out of a torn wound where it is, pumped on the beat, and from a part hit hard soaked into the clothes round it and down them until they hold no more, then out of the lowest GAP in them as he lies (the collar, the jacket's hem, a cuff, a trouser leg over the boot — `gore-leaks.ts`), while a head split open bleeds straight off his BARE FACE, nothing over it, run red from under the nose and off the chin or the cheek he lies on as a stream and drops — and POOLS under him, all of it in one spreading round his body, a litre spreading wide on the packed groomer and on thin snow and little in deep loose snow, which drinks it (`gore-blood.ts`, `gore-shapes.ts`); **The skydive is built too:** out of the jump plane's door a free ride becomes a SKYDIVE (`CreateGameOptions.chute`, `engine/game/chute.ts`, `docs/skydive.md`; `?chute=<m>` starts out of the door that high) on the press sequence JUMP, OPEN, CUT AWAY, RESERVE (ENTER, a double tap). Each face also carries the real ski area's HINTS off OpenStreetMap (`real-hints.ts`, baked by `make real-hints` into a generated file a face (`real-hints/hints-<id>.ts`, ~7 KB, ODbL, fetched with the heights): every real lift's ends and kind, every real piste's colour, line and width, every real building's middle, size and bearing, the town's streets roughly (its car roads a few bends each, a main road or a street, and its middle and radius) — the lifts are laid along the real lifts' lines and onto their tops, each run laid on a real piste and billed its signed colour at its real width, the village stood below the real town and every real building small enough to be a house stood at its real spot as the face's own — the mountain's huts, hotels and chalets first, then the town's, nudged off a run rather than dropped (`real-houses.ts`), the village's streets laid along the real town's and its other streets laid as town streets where they clear the ski area (`real-streets.ts`), on a face's first attempts (`RR.massif.real.hinted`), and the rules a dealt massif is held to fitted to a real one where reality differs (`RR.massif.real.least`). Its REAL WOODS come with it: the forest the real map draws, rasterised onto the face's height grid into a tree line and its cover in eight bands by height (`HINT_TREES` in `real-hints-index.ts`, nine bytes a face, `realFaceTrees`), stand the map's tree line where the real one is and thin R14's woods where the real face is bare (`resort-woods.ts`'s `faceWoods`); a face whose map draws no forest keeps its region's row. Its REAL WATER comes too: the lakes, ponds, reservoirs and rivers the real map draws as areas and its rivers and streams as lines (`HintWater`, `HintStream`, baked by `scripts/lib/real-face-water.mjs` after the town), laid as `Level.water` and `Level.streams` (`real-water.ts`'s `layWater`) — each body's ground flattened to its surface (the median of the ground under it) and eased into the shore over 10–20 m, and flattened again once every run, pad and ramp is graded (`relayWater`) or the body left off where that would move a pad or steepen a run, the valley floor's hub rows kept dry and, where the water would leave the village none, the village's room below the hub cut out of it (`keepVillageDry` — the village stands on the shore, never on the ice), a body the dealt ground under it stands more than 15 m off left out, and no tree, tower, building or pad standing in one (`inWater`). Chosen on the start card's RANGE row or a `?face=` link; a map with no face is untouched, so no digest moves.

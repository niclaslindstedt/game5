title: A smoother frame at the foot of the mountain
---

The village at the bottom of a free ride is no longer drawn whole around the lens: the people on foot, the dogs, the amateurs, the cars, the ski bus and the lifts' moving chairs are left out when they are behind the camera and their shadows cannot reach the snow in front of it, and the village's buildings are cut into tiles so the ones out of sight are skipped. Looking round the village square, about two fifths fewer triangles are drawn a frame, with the same picture.

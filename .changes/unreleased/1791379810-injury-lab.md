title: Injuries that match the moment
---

The skier's injuries now follow what he hits and how. A lift tower's bare steel hurts far more than a trunk, and its foam pad far less. A cabin's log wall sits between the two. A fall on his side breaks the ribs over the spleen or the liver, a dive head first breaks the neck, and a hard fall on his seat crushes a vertebra. Slid feet first into a wall, his legs break along their length. The collarbone breaks from a blow on the point of the shoulder, the shoulder blade from behind, and the kneecap from in front. A new injury lab (`make injuries`) stages some forty moments and checks each one does the injuries it should.

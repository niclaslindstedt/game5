title: Wind tunnels along the valley floor
---

Every ski area now has two wind tunnels along the valley floor, one blowing each way, that carry a skier across the bottom of the mountain without skiing. Each is a run of white arches under a tinted canopy, amber one way and cyan the other, with rings of light and chevrons on the snow chasing down it the way the air goes, streaks of blown snow overtaking the skier, and a big fan turning in a cowl at the entrance under a sign. The minimap draws them in their colours with arrows down them. Inside one you hear a roaring gale and the fan's motor, with a whoosh going in and coming out, and the news column says so.

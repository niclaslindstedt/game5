title: Sidestep up steep faces
---

Stood still across a slope too steep to walk, the steer toward the hill sidesteps the skier up it a ski at a time: the uphill ski lifted and stamped down on a ledge a step up the hill, his weight moved onto it, then the downhill ski brought up beside it, the poles planted with each step. The ledges hold him on faces up to about 48°; past 35° each step is shorter and slower, and past 50° the snow will not hold a platform. Each stamp leaves its own ledge in the snow, a ladder up the face. The steer away from the hill still steps him round to face down it.

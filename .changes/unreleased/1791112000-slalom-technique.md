type: Changed
title: A slalom skied like a slalom — the racer's technique, real turns at real speed
---

A slalom is now skied with a racer's TECHNIQUE: the edge rolled quickly from side to side and stood at up to 70°, held there at a slalom's pace, and the skier standing on the shelf his edge cuts — so the slalom skis carve a real slalom turn, some 4–6 m round a pole at well over 2 g, a turn every second. Runs that took one and a half to two minutes now take 50–70 s at about 40 km/h on the average and 50 at the most, and the field's times and the course's par moved with them. The slalom course is groomed hard from the start hut to the finish, whatever drift the wind laid across the piste. Every other mode skis exactly as before.

---
type: Changed
title: The free ride is leisure — no gates, no run figures, and reset and restart that know where you have skied
---

A free ride no longer draws a race's figures at the top left of the screen: the clock, the best air and the distance skied are gone from over the snow, and the map's seed is the one number left there, so a picture still says which mountain it is (the best air and the distance are still on the pause card). The course's red and blue gates are no longer set on the mountain either; the start, the finish, the edge poles, the signs and the lights stay.

A free ride owes no gate, so its reset and restart no longer think in gates. The ride remembers every run you have had your skis on, starting with the one you were put on. The HUD's reset (R) stands you back on the nearest of those runs, never on one you have not been near, and no longer sends you back up to the start line from the foot of the mountain. Restart (B, or RESTART on the pause card) takes you to the top of the slope again: you stand at the head of the last piste you skied, facing down it.

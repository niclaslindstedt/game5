On a free ride and in the tricks park the back key held on its own is the whole stop: a skidded turn or two across the hill at speed, then a hockey stop, and the skier stands. Pressed again once he has stopped, it takes him back — walking up a gentle hill, sidestepping up a steep one, turning round to face down a hill he faces up, or turning round on the flat — and the tuck held stood facing a hill climbs it the same way.

type: Added
title: Statistics
---

A STATISTICS tile on the front door opens a card that counts every run you ride on this device: runs, time on the snow, distance skied and height come down; personal bests (top speed, longest air, hardest landing, biggest combo, best trick score, hardest blow in g, most bones broken in one run, worst injury score), each with the run that set it; wins, podiums and gates; airtime and trick points; wipeouts by cause, trees, injuries, bones broken and deaths by cause; lift rides and time in the helicopter, under a wing, on the snowmobile and in a piste machine; time by mode and distance by skis; every map's own runs, top speed, best trick score, best time per mode and tricks landed; and the last twelve runs. It is kept in the browser and can be cleared from the card.

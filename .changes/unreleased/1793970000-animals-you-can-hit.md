title: Animals off the piste you can run into
---

On a free ride the animals of the mountain now live beside every run of the ski area, off the piste and in sight of it, so you meet about one animal or herd on each run down. They are bodies now: some bolt across your line when you come close, and you can ride into them. A hare or a fox is run through and you ski on. A deer or anything bigger knocks you over it, and a moose stops you dead. With injuries on, an animal hit hard is killed and flung across the snow, and a hard enough hit into a big one hurts you as badly as a tree would: past about 75 km/h a leg is torn off, and a little faster it opens your chest and takes your head. In safe-for-work mode nothing dies: the animal is knocked over, gets up and runs back to its herd.

**The piste is full of other skiers' tracks.** A groomed run is the night's corduroy whole at the first chair; through the morning it fills with crisp lines — a carver's two thin grooves, deepest at the turn with a little snow thrown out on the outside, a skidder's broad smear, a snowboard's single band — winding down and crossing one another, and by the afternoon they have blurred into each other as churned, scraped snow. It follows the map's own hour on every mode.

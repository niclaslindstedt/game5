title: Speed reads as speed
---

The chase camera rides lower and closer, a little under head height, and its lens opens wider with speed while it pulls in, so the snow at the edges of the frame rushes past while the skier keeps his size. It now tips only about half way with the slope on an ordinary piste, so the drop under the skis reads as a descent rather than a flat plain (on the steepest faces it still tips further, so the piste ahead stays in view). The groomed corduroy is worn in patches along its length, and the snow has a finer grain close in, so the surface visibly streams under the skis. At pace the ice crystals in the air streak past the lens. The edge poles stand every 25 m instead of every 50, and the chatter in the lens starts at 36 km/h instead of 40.

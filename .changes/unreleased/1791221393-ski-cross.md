title: Ski cross — four out of one start gate down a course built in the snow, a qualification then heats to the final, on nine courses
---

A fifth race discipline, and the first where you race the field ON the course: four racers out of one start gate, side by side, the first two over the line through. Its course is BUILT in the snow down a gentle stretch of the ski area — a fenced lane weaving down the mountain, its turns banked into berms, its straights carrying rollers, table jumps and step-downs, triangular flags on the inside of every berm and either side of every feature, a start ramp below four doors and a finish jump into the arena. First a QUALIFYING run alone against a board of thirty; the best sixteen go into the heats — quarter-final, semi-final, then the small final or the BIG FINAL — and every heat you are not in is raced the same way every time. In a heat it is "skiers ready", "attention", and the doors drop when nobody expects them: hold the tuck to pull out of the gate. Your three rivals are the start list's own racers, holding their lanes and passing where there is room; lean on one and he leans back, but put a racer down from behind and the jury shows you the red card. The HUD carries the round and your place in the pack, the plate the heat's order with who goes through and the press on to the next round — or, once you are out, where you finished and who won. Raced on the new ski-cross ski, the Wolverine. Nine courses of its own, from a gentle fell blue to a continental black of seven tight berms, one under the floodlights, all open from the first visit.

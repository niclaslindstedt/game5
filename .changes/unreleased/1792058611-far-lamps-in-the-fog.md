type: Fixed
title: Far lamps fade into the weather
---

Far-off lights no longer flicker through a storm or fog as a row of coloured dots. The airstrip's edge lamps, the helipad's paint and lamps, the lifts' amber boarding rings, the cars' lamps, the groomers' lamps and the finish floods now fade into the air the way the snow around them does. A glow point is never drawn thinner than a steady dot, and the boarding rings stop at 1.5 km, where they would only be a flickering sliver.

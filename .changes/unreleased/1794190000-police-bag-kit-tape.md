title: The police keep the skis off the body bag, and the bag and the tape read from afar
---

After a death, the skis and poles lying where the police lay the body bag no longer end up under it: once the last photograph is taken, the photographer gathers up whatever lies in the bag's way and carries it aboard as evidence. The body bag is cut to a body's outline and rounded over him, so it no longer looks like a box from a distance, and the cordon's tape stays readable from the helicopter or a high camera without looking any wider up close.

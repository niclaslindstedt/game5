**A downhill bike in the engine, for the summer.** A summer free ride can begin on a downhill mountain bike at the head of a bike park trail. It has long-travel suspension at both ends, tyres that grip each kind of summer ground differently, and a rider who shifts his weight, leans into turns, pumps and pops. Brakes can lock and skid, and the front alone can pitch you over the bars. Landings are judged as clean, cased, nosed or overshot. A crash throws the rider, leaves the bike lying on its side, and stands him back on it on his trail. The machine key steps off to walk and back on, and the chairs and gondolas carry rider and bike up together. Where two park trails cross, both are now graded to one height. The bike is not drawn yet: its picture, camera, readouts and start-card stop come next.

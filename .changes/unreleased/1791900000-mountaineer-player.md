title: The mountain guide on the rider card
---

The rider card has a third body, GUIDE: the player as a mountain guide on a glacier tour, modelled in Blender. He wears a red shell, a harness with its rack, and a black pack with yellow trim, crampons and a shovel strapped to its back. His rope is coiled over the shoulder and across the chest, and he carries a radio on the strap, a climbing helmet with goggles round its back, and glacier glasses. Every animation the game already had poses him. His pack, the coil and the rope's loose tail swing on springs as he turns, lands and tucks, and the loose cloth flaps harder the faster he goes, on the run's own clock so it slows with the replay. A broken arm hangs from the break as on every other skier. With GUIDE picked the card offers only the body and the weight; every other skier is still dressed in code.

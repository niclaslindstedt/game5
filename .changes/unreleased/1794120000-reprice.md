type: Changed
title: The automatic picture keeps more on a fast machine
---

The automatic picture setting chooses each machine's picture from what every option costs, and those costs were measured again after the HIGH picture got faster. The deformable snow, the shadows and the spray now cost about half what they did. A machine that held MEDIUM before now gets more of the picture, and on a fast laptop at 1080p every option is at its highest.

title: A helicopter crash hurts the skier on its skid
---

Crashing the helicopter now hurts the skier riding its skid at the moment it goes down, not only when he lands after being thrown. The machine's fall, stopped by the snow, goes up the seat into his spine, pelvis and legs, as in a real vertical crash: a hard touchdown is a jolt, and one at a survivable crash's limit breaks his back. The fireball burns him by how long and how close he is to it, through what his clothes let through. Flung clear, he is burnt on the face and hands. Caught in it, his burns are deep and his airway is burnt.

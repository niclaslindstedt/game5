title: Blood pours in streams out of the clothes and pools round the body
---

With INJURIES on, blood no longer sprays far in drops. It pours in unbroken streams: out of a torn wound where it is, pumped on the heartbeat, and from a part hit hard enough to split the skin, soaked into the clothes round it first and then running out of the lowest gap in them (the collar, the jacket's hem, a cuff, a trouser leg over the boot, the back of the helmet). A head split open bleeds straight off the bare face instead, nothing over it to hold it: it runs red down from under the nose, streams off the chin or the cheek he lies on and drips as it goes. A torn organ or a closed break bleeds inside and is never seen. What comes out pools in the snow round him, spreading wide on the packed piste and on thin snow and soaking away in deep powder, and a body torn open goes on emptying onto the snow after the heart stops.

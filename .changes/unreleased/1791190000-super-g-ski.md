title: A super-G ski joins the catalog
---

A seventh pair of skis, the Falcon, a super-G ski built to the men's top-level rule: 210 cm on a 65 mm waist with a 45 m sidecut, a 94 mm shoulder over a short, low shovel, stiff and on a race plate, carried with the speed events' bent poles. It holds the hardest bend of any pair at a super-G's hundred-odd km/h, where the giant slalom ski's tighter arc asks more than its edge holds and the downhill ski's longer one will not bend, and it sits between the two on top speed and quickness. The super-G's field now races it, and the ski card bills every pair on a seventh bar, SPEED CARVE.

The sky now lights the snow. Snow in shade takes the colour of the sky above it: deep blue under a clear sky with a low sun, and pink from the band of light opposite the sunset once the sun is down. Under a cloud deck it goes a soft grey. A low morning or evening sun lays golden light across the snow, and every swell, drift and track throws a long soft shadow. Seen against the sun, the walls of a deep track glow a faint ice blue. At sunset the peaks keep a red light for some minutes after the valley has gone blue. Through dusk the floodlights fade in gradually instead of washing the slopes out while the sky is still bright.

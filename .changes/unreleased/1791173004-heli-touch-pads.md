title: The helicopter flies on two touch pads — the cyclic on the left, the collective and the pedals on the right
---

The helicopter's touch controls are two pads: the left thumb is the cyclic (drag up to tilt forward and fly ahead, down to tilt back, across to bank) and the right thumb works the collective (drag up to climb, down to sink, and it stays where you leave it) and the pedals (drag across to turn the nose), with a dead band so turning never creeps the height. Each pad is drawn as a d-pad under the thumb, its arrows lighting the way it is pushed: the left pad's knob is the helicopter seen from behind, banking with your thumb; the right pad's is its rotor, turning with the pedals, beside turn arrows and a gauge of the collective.

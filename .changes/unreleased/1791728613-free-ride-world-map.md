title: The free ride's world map
---

FREE RIDE now opens on WHERE: a world map that flies onto the range your ride stands in, with a marker on every real ski area where it really lies — near enough to tell two towns ten kilometres apart. Tap a marker to pick it, drag to pan, pinch, scroll or press + and − to zoom; areas too close together to read gather into a numbered bubble that flies in when tapped, and the RANGE and AREA rows beside the map step the same picks from the keys (GENERATED, the seed's own mountain, is still RANGE's last stop). A ski area with more than one mountain then shows MOUNTAIN, its mountainsides on a closer map, before the start card; the start card's new WORLD MAP chip and its BACK walk the steps again. Every real mountain is offered, whatever colours its slopes are. The map is a small download of its own, and no mountain is built until you have picked it.

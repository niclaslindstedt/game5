---
type: Changed
title: Getting going on a snowboard is quicker
---

The snowboard's one-foot skate pushes harder: from a stand, a rider now reaches 10 km/h in about 3 s on the flat, and in under 2 s down a gentle grade. Before, he never quite reached it on the flat.

Strapped in, a rider can hop the board forward with the jump.

On a free ride, a rider standing still on his board is told how to get going: hold the tuck to skate, tap the jump to hop.

Skiers are unchanged.

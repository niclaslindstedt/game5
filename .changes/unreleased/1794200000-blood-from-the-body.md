---
type: Fixed
title: A person run into is struck as the bodies meet, and bleeds from his body, not the air
---

Skiing into someone at speed now lands the blow the moment your body meets his, not a metre and a half out where your skis first came near him: the impact camera's slow motion shows the two of you touching as he is hit, and skis passing beside or under his with the bodies apart are no blow at all. With INJURIES on, the blood comes out of the struck side of his body and goes with him as he is thrown, pouring out over the first moments of the blow, instead of a cloud hanging in the empty air between the two of you.

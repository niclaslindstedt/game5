With INJURIES on, a skier who hits a lift tower faster than 100 km/h is now crushed on it just as on a tree or a lamp pole: the tower's pad takes the edge off a slow hit but nothing at that speed. The pad visibly caves in where he hits and slowly comes back, and nothing flung off him — a limb, a gib, the blood spray — flies through a tower, a post or a trunk any more.

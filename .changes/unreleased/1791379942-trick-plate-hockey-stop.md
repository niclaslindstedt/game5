title: The freestyle contest plate, and a hockey stop after the run
---

The plate after a big air jump, a slopestyle, halfpipe or moguls run or an
aerials jump now reads cleanly on every screen: the run's HUD steps aside
while it is up, the board shows the leaders and the rivals either side of
you with a mark where the places skip, and every press stays on screen — on
a phone on its side the board takes the right-hand column. And once the run
is over the skier no longer coasts on down the mountain behind the plate:
he throws his skis across in a hockey stop and stands there.

title: The wingsuit takes to the air
---

A free ride can now be flown in a wingsuit, with no skis on and no reserve. Stand high on the mountain, or on the skid of a helicopter holding still over its steepest high edge, and jump. The suit fills with air and flies a glide of about three to one at 150 km/h. Sweep your arms back and lean forward to dive past 300 km/h. Lean back and spread your wings to flare, which climbs before it mushes. The edge banks you into a turn. One press pulls a single BASE canopy, which opens in about four seconds. You land on your boots and walk. You can also leave the jump plane's door in the suit. Fly into the snow or a tree and you start again at the exit. This release is the flight itself. The suit, the picture and the start card follow.

The snow a turn throws now follows what real riders throw. On the groomer a hockey stop throws a low, flat fan of chips about knee high, and the fine cloud stays small and low. A board's stop throws a taller fan than a ski's. A slash in powder raises a taller wall. A new lab, `make snow-ref`, lays each manoeuvre's measured envelope over the game's own plume and scores them against each other.

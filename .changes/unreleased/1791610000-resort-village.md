title: A resort village at the foot of the lifts, and a restaurant and patrol hut up the mountain
---

A free ride's ski area now has a base that reads as a real one: round the plaza at the foot of the lifts stand a base lodge with its glazed dining hall over a terrace of tables, parasols and full ski racks, a ticket kiosk with its serving windows, sign band and queue rails, a rental shop with a glazed front and a rack of hire skis, a red ski school hut with its meeting flags, a first-aid station marked with the red cross, a row of hotels with a balcony on every floor, a groomer garage with its roller doors and a snowmaking pump house. Up the mountain a low stone restaurant sits under its slab of snow beside a top station, with a deck, deck chairs and feather flags, and a red patrol hut on posts stands by the highest top. After dark their windows light up.

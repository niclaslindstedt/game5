title: Hands and poles riding switch
---

Riding switch, the skier now drops his hands low by his sides and points his poles low and back, trailing toward his tips with the baskets clear of the snow, the way switch riding is taught. Tucked switch at speed, his fists go down beside his knees. Before, his hands were held out wide and swung round with his shoulders, and in a tuck one arm stuck out with the pole pointing up.

type: Changed
title: Real mountainsides laid like the real ski areas
---

A free ride on a real mountainside now lays its ski area the way the real one is laid, off map data of the real ski area (© OpenStreetMap contributors): its lifts run along the real lifts' lines onto their tops, each run is laid on a real piste in the colour that piste is signed and at its real width, and the real buildings stand where they are, with the village's streets laid through them. The rules a generated mountain is held to give way to the real one's where it differs (fewer runs, rougher station pads), so a real mountainside also builds faster.

title: A free ride starts without crashing a phone
---

Starting a free ride holds less memory while the mountain is built — the title picture, the ski card's 3D view and the start card's map worker are let go before the load, the picture's programs are compiled a part at a time, a town's buildings no longer keep a second copy of themselves in the page (some 400 MB less on a real face), and the plane's crash is made ready with the plane rather than at the moment it burns — so a phone is less likely to crash and reload the page, and the RIDE press now shows a spinner the moment it is pressed.

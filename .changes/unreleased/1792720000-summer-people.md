**Summer people at the ski area.** In summer the people on foot are about a quarter as many as on a winter's day. They are dressed for the season: t-shirts and shorts, sun hats and caps, sunglasses and day packs. Hikers carry walking poles and rest at the tops or walk the cat tracks. Riders in full-face helmets, long jerseys and knee pads wait at the lifts' feet. Picnics sit on blankets on the grass. The lift crew work only at the lifts that run. Nobody carries skis, there is no ski school and nobody builds a snowman. Fewer dogs are out, and none wears a coat. The village's streets, square and car park are bare asphalt and stone, with painted lines. The snow poles are gone and the parked cars carry no snow.

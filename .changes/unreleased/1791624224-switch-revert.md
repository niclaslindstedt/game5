A skier riding switch on a free ride or a tricks run who slows under 15 km/h now turns round to ride forward — the skis laid flat and pivoted round under him, his head leading the way he travels — so he can pole and skate again. Under 40 km/h, a jump popped riding switch hops him round the same way in the air. Hold the edge to choose which way he turns. `make revert` is its lab.

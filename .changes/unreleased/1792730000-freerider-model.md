The player now rides as a modelled backcountry freerider made in Blender: a long baggy shell over bunched bibs, gauntlets, a helmet and goggles and an avalanche airbag pack, posed by every one of the game's own animations, with the shell's hem, sleeves and cuffs flapping in the wind harder the faster he goes. `VITE_MODEL_RIDER=0` builds the code-dressed skier instead.

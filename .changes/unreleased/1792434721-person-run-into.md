title: Running into a skier at speed is a real collision
---

On a free ride, hitting one of the amateur skiers faster than a brisk cruise is now a collision between two bodies of about the same weight, not a shoulder bump. Both of you are thrown. Your body takes the blow on every part the other person's body hits: bruises at 30 km/h, a cracked neck and broken bones at 45, and at 60 km/h and over, crushed ribs, a broken pelvis and femurs, torn organs and usually a broken neck. With INJURIES on, the skier you hit stays down hurt or is killed and crushed. A child weighs half as much as you and takes far more of the blow than you do. With INJURIES off, nobody is killed and the skier gets back up. A new lab, `make body-hits`, prints both sides of every collision.

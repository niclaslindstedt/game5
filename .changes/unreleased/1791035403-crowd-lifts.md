title: The amateur skiers queue for the lifts and ride them
---

The free ride's amateurs queue in the corral at the foot of a lift, shuffle up as the line moves, and ride it up — four to a chair, two behind a T-bar, eight to a gondola cabin — then ski off the top onto the run their group picked, the leader waiting for the rest. The chairs are full of people from the first frame.

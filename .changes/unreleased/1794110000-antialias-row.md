---
type: Changed
title: ANTIALIASING is OFF, 2× or 4× and takes effect at once; RESOLUTION is HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW; AUTO gives up both first
---

The antialiasing setting under OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE, now called ANTIALIASING, has three stops instead of a switch: OFF, 2× and 4× (what ON was). 2× smooths most of the steps along edges for less of the frame than 4×. On a high-density laptop screen at the HIGH preset it is the difference between about 57 and 60 frames a second. The change shows at once, over the live run, instead of the next time the game is opened. A stored ON reads as 4× and OFF as OFF.

RESOLUTION has three stops instead of six percentages: HIGH draws all of the screen's pixels, MEDIUM half of them and LOW a quarter. A stored percentage becomes the nearest of them.

The automatic picture now gives up the antialiasing first and the resolution next, each all the way down, before it lowers anything that changes what is in the picture (the trails, the shadows, the woods, how far you can see). The LOW preset uses 2× antialiasing and MEDIUM resolution.

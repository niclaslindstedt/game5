type: Changed
title: The lifts' towers, chairs, gondola cabins and T-bars rebuilt to look like the real thing
---

The lifts' hardware is rebuilt from what real lifts are made of. A tower is now a round tapered steel column with a ladder up its downhill face, and at its head a box-girder crossarm carrying a train of rubber-lined sheave wheels at each rope, a railed catwalk, lifting frames and a lightning rod. A chair is a detachable quad on a swept hanger, with four padded seats and backrests, armrests, and its safety bar lowered over the footrests. A gondola cabin is rounded at its corners, with a window band right round it, a domed roof, door seams on its platform side and ski racks on its ends. A drag's T-bars hang from spring boxes, their bars padded. Far from the lens each part is drawn at a cheaper cut, so the mountain's lifts cost no more to draw than before.

A free ride is far quicker onto the snow: while the rider and gear cards are up, the mountain picked on the start card is already being stood up behind them — the run laid on it and every mesh of its world built — so pressing SKI leaves the loading card only the picture's shaders to compile. Changing the mountain, the hour or the craft, or leaving the free ride's cards, throws the half-built world away.

title: The snowboarder carves with bent knees and a quiet upper body
---

On a toeside carve the rider now bends his knees and flexes his ankles into the hill, his hips over the toe edge and his upper body near upright. Before, his legs came out nearly straight and he leaned far over at the waist. Cut hard, he leans out and his front hand reaches the snow.

On a heelside carve he sits lower over the heel edge, his chest up.

Hopping while holding the tuck at a crawl now keeps him skating through the hop, so he gets going about twice as fast that way.

On village streets at a crawl the board is no longer drawn under the street's surface.

Skiers are unchanged.

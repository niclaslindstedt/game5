type: Changed
title: A camera of its own for the machines
---

The camera rung picked aboard a machine — the jump plane, the helicopter, the snowmobile, the balloon, the paramotor, a piste machine — is kept apart from the skier's own, so a cockpit view in the plane is never his own eyes once he jumps out, and the skier's view is never the machine's when he climbs aboard. The skier's camera starts on the close chase; a camera stored before this goes to the machines.

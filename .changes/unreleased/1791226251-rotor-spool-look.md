title: The helicopter's rotor spools up the way a real one looks
---

The helicopter's rotors no longer jump about as they turn. Spooling up, the blades turn sharp at first. Then they smear into pale wedges and thin into a haze, with the painted tips showing as a ring at the rim. At full speed a ghost of the blades creeps slowly backwards, as a rotor does on film. The tail rotor is a faint disc at speed.

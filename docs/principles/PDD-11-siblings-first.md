## Rule

Before building a system, read how game4, game3 or game2 solved it, and port the DESIGN retyped in this game's vocabulary. Nothing imports from them.

## Why

They share the spec, the shell, the labs and most of the plumbing; their answers are tested.

## Prevents

A second, worse invention of something that already works next door.

## Exceptions

What is this game's alone: the skier's answer to the snow, the mountain and its piste, the skis and the skier as drawn.

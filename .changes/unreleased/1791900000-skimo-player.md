title: A skimo racer on the rider card
---

The rider card offers a third body, a SKIMO RACER modelled in Blender after the ski mountaineering stage races: a skin-tight one-piece race suit in acid green and black piped in blue, his number on his thigh, the suit's hood stowed down his back, a neck gaiter, a slim race pack worn as a vest with a flask on its strap, a shovel up its side and bungee across its back, a yellow race helmet and wrap glasses with a mirror lens. He is chosen on the card like any body and only by the player, posed by every animation the game already had, and a broken arm hangs from its break as the code's skier's does. The pack, the hood and the ski strap's loop swing on springs as he turns, lands and tucks, and the loose cloth flaps harder the faster he goes, all on the run's own clock, so a paused run holds them still.

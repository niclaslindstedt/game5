---
type: Changed
title: Falls that look like a person falling, at full speed, with the reset lit
---

A thrown skier now falls like a person instead of a rag. In the air he throws his hands out ahead of him to meet the snow and spreads his legs. Once he hits, his body goes slack. His knees and elbows bend only the ways real ones do, and his arms and legs no longer pass through his chest. Each kind of fall sends him over its own way: on over a tree or the ski tips, over onto his side when an edge catches, and back and down when a landing is too hard for his legs. He no longer cartwheels down a steep face or gets kicked back up off the snow once he has stopped.

The slow motion when you fall is gone, so a crash plays at full speed. The reset button on the HUD now lights up the moment you are thrown, so you can be back on your skis at once.

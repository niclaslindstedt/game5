---
type: Changed
title: The slalom and the downhill each have nine maps of their own, picked because they make a good race — and the pause card tells you how to free-ride the mountain
---

The level card behind the SLALOM and DOWNHILL tiles no longer borrows the campaign's maps. Each discipline has nine of its own, all open from the start, ordered from the gentlest to the hardest. They were chosen out of a sweep of the generator's mountains for the race they make. The slaloms are 140–190 m hills of 36–63 % in all three race countries, two of them blacks. The downhills run 550–1090 m of vertical and 2.0–3.0 km down the alpine and continental blacks, with jumps, gliders' pitches and a long morning course among them. Each ladder has one race under the floodlights at night. Every box shows the course's drop and length beside your best time. The time trial still picks from the campaign's maps, and the campaign itself is unchanged. On a slalom, a downhill or a time trial, the pause card now shows a second line, **FREE RIDE IT · SEED · COUNTRY · GRADE**: the three answers that raise the same mountain on the free ride's start card.

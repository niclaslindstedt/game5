title: Avalanches as drawn
---

A slab avalanche on a free ride is now seen as well as felt. The crack runs out across the slope from where the slab gave way, and the slab breaks into irregular plates of every size that heave, tip and pile into each other. A clean crown wall stands over the bare, greyer bed, with ragged flanks down its sides. The moving snow is one churning white tongue, roughest at its front, and its blocks round off into snowballs down the track. A powder cloud tens of metres tall billows over and ahead of the front, bright on its sunlit side and blue-grey in its own shade, and hangs on the wind after the flow stops. Where the flow stops it lays a rough, shadowed tongue of debris that stays for the rest of the ride and is lit by the headlamp at night. The ground shakes under the camera as the flow runs near, and the release and being caught are felt in the hands. The free ride's start card has a new AVALANCHES row (on by default) to turn them off, and `make avalanche` photographs a slide sheet by sheet.

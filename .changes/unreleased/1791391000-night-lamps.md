title: Faster nights, and two more picture steps
---

After dark the snow's sparkle under the lamps is worked out once for all of them instead of once per lamp, which makes night runs lighter on every machine without changing the look. OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE has a new LAMPS row (how many lamps light the snow at once: two, four or six — your own headlamp always one of them) and RESOLUTION has a new lowest stop, MIN, for phones.

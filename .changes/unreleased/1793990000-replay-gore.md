type: Fixed
title: Replays show the gore again
---

Watching a replay of a run that ended in gore now shows it: a rider cut by a lift's rope, crushed on a tree or torn apart is cut, crushed and torn again at the same moment in the replay. A replay had dropped a few of the controls it was ridden on (the flick to jump on touch, the jump plane's controls, the transceiver and the rescue key), so it could ski a different line and miss what killed you. A dead rider's body is also no longer left lying in the snow ahead of the replay, or laid down a second time when you leave it.

---
type: Removed
title: The knuckle huck, the rail jam and dual moguls
---

Three freestyle formats are gone from the tricks card: the knuckle huck (big
air's jump without its kicker), the rail jam (slopestyle's rails on their
own) and dual moguls (moguls side by side). Big air, slopestyle, the
halfpipe, moguls and aerials are unchanged; a record, ghost, replay or link
that named a removed format is skipped or falls back to the front door.

title: The snowboarder carves low on both edges and absorbs in the air
---

On his toe edge the snowboarder now flexes both ankles into his boots, with both knees bent deep and pointing down the toe edge and his hips low over it. Cut hard, he folds right down with a hand near the snow. On his heel edge his shins go over with the board and he sits low behind his heels, as if on a chair. In the air he pulls his knees up with the board under him instead of hanging on stiff legs. On a street, the board is now lifted onto the snow as fast as he is riding, so it no longer sinks into the street or floats off its edge.

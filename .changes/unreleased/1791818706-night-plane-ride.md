title: A night plane ride runs at the right speed, and its parachute draws right
---

After dark, every keen skier lapping the runs was stepping the player's own jump plane, skydive, balloon and paramotor again, so a night ride ran several times too fast and the parachute was flown off another skier. Each now leaves the player's ride alone. The parachute's lines and bridle are lit by the sky like the paramotor's, so they no longer glow white at night.

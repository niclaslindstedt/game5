type: Changed
title: Each racer carries himself the way his discipline does, and a slalom racer cross-blocks the turning pole
---

The skier's figure now stands in his discipline's own way. A slalom racer skis square to the fall line with his shoulders countered against his skis, his hands up, forward and wide, a short pole touch by the boot on every turn, a narrower stance, and both legs pulled up together under a level body as the skis cross from one edge to the next — and as he reaches each turning pole his outside hand punches forward across his chest to knock it away, drawn back once he is past. The giant slalom, super-G and downhill racers (ready for when their disciplines arrive) bend further forward, hold the outside leg long through the turn and cross their bodies over into the next one; the speed racers carry their poles under their arms and fold flat in a low tuck on the straights, rising into a higher one in the turns. A free ride, a time trial and the crowd look exactly as before.

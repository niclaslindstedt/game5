---
type: Changed
title: Three bars on the ski card
---

The ski card bills a pair on three bars instead of eleven: SPEED (how fast it runs), CARVE (how hard and how quickly it turns on the groomer — the old edge hold, berm, speed carve, fast bend and quickness merged) and FREESTYLE (the old float, forgiveness, landings, bumps and spin merged). The figures above them — length, waist, sidecut, weight and top speed — are unchanged.

title: A hot air balloon on the free ride
---

The start card's RUN row has a BALLOON stop: you start in the basket of a hot air balloon tethered on the valley floor. Hold the tuck to fire the burner; once the envelope is light the tether lets go, and the day's wind carries you up the mountain toward the summit — the higher you fly, the faster, and turning a little with height, which is the only steering you have. A burn takes tens of seconds to become a climb, the skid pulls the valve in the crown to come down, and the edge and the lean walk you about the basket. Let it cool and it sinks; meet the snow, the rising slope or a tree too fast and the basket goes over and throws you out. Burning in a strong wind, or until the fabric cooks, sets the envelope alight and it falls. Enter takes you over the side — the only way out in the air — or, stood on the snow, steps you out. The balloon is drawn as a simple stand-in for now.

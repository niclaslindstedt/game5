title: A remodelled skier
---

The skier is rebuilt: an alpine kit in place of the old padded racer's — a fitted, quilted jacket with a zip and stripes down the sleeves, softshell pants over the boots, gloves closed round the pole grips, an open helmet with goggles on the face — with real cloth textures, and legs that bend at the knee above the boot, so he stands in a true athletic stance. He skates, double-poles, sinks and springs for a jump, angulates harder when he cuts and sits into a hockey stop.

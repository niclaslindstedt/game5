**Flips and spins are held, not snapped.** In the air on a free ride or a tricks run, the lean held now turns the skier head over heels smoothly for as long as you hold it — a backflip, a double, or round past his feet — and let go he opens out and slows, so landing it is down to when you let go; nothing stands him back up square any more. Spins work the same way: a tap of the edge is still a 180, but held (or dragged further across on the touch thumb, for a faster spin) he keeps spinning and settles on the next half turn when you let go.

---
type: Changed
title: Flips on big jumps look like flips
---

Leaning hard back or forward in the air on a big jump now throws a backflip or a front flip on any flight with a second of air ahead, not only off a kicker or your own pop, and the loop comes round a little quicker (about 0.6 s). The skier turns it tucked, knees to his chest and hands on his shins, and opens out to spot the landing; a snowboarder tucks with a hand on the board.

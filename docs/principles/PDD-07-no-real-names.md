## Rule

Nothing in the tree names another game, a console, a ski or boot maker or model, a resort, a named piste, mountain or race, or any other brand. Keep a real class's NUMBER and the band it sits in; drop the badge. The feel reference is a genre, never a title.

## Why

The game must be publishable without anyone's permission, and a number generalises where a model name dates.

## Prevents

Trademark trouble, and design anchored on one product instead of the class it belongs to.

## Exceptions

A real face's range, area and part, held in its crop row, the generated face index and `strings-ranges.ts`. Cited scientific models (Coulomb, Bekker) and the sibling repos are not brands.

title: A ski-cross ski joins the catalog
---

A ninth pair of skis, the Wolverine, a ski-cross ski: a giant slalom ski cut down for a course of berms, rollers and jumps — 188 cm on a 68 mm waist with a 24 m sidecut, a softer, slightly rockered shovel for the landings, on a race plate as high as the rule's 50 mm — in a teal topsheet of its own. It lands softer than any other race ski, and round a banked turn at 65 km/h it holds the hardest bend of the nine. The ski card bills every pair on a ninth bar, BERM — the bend a pair holds at a ski cross's pace, where the ski-cross ski is the best of them.

title: A wanted level for the army hunting a helicopter thief
---

A free ride now has a wanted level. Five stars sit under the buttons in the top right. A star is solid while the army can see you and flashes while it is searching. Stay out of sight long enough and the stars are gone. The longer you hold out, the higher the stars climb. A survival timer runs beside the stars, and the longest you have lasted is kept as your best and shown on the card at the end of a run. With INJURIES off the army arrests you instead of killing you, and the run ends with ARRESTED. A link with `?wanted=1` to `?wanted=5` starts a free ride already wanted. Stealing a helicopter will raise the wanted level once that is in the game. The army's helicopters, soldiers and vehicles will follow in later updates.

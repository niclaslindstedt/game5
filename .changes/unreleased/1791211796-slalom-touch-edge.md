type: Changed
title: The touch edge control is quicker on a slalom and never runs away from the thumb
---

On a slalom the edge control's throw is just over half as wide, so swinging the skis from full edge one way to full edge the other between gates is a short flick of the thumb instead of a long sweep; the lean keeps its full travel, so a quick swing does not lean the skier or throw the brake by accident. On every run, a thumb swept past the control's reach ring now drags the control along with it, so coming back is never further than one throw — before, an overshoot had to be undone before the edge began to come off, and the swing the other way fell short by it.

title: A modelled Nordic ski tourer on the rider card
---

The rider card's BODY row has a new pick, TOURER: a Nordic ski tourer modelled in Blender, in a deep red pullover anorak patched in olive, slim touring pants with knee patches and gaiters, a cream wool beanie with a pompom, sunglasses, leather work gloves and a touring pack with an avalanche shovel and a rolled sit pad lashed on it. He comes in his own kit, so only the BODY and WEIGHT rows are offered, and the weight reshapes him. He is posed by every animation the game already had, a broken arm hangs from its break, and his pack, rolled-down hood and pompom swing on springs while his anorak's hem, drawcords and strap tails flap in the wind. All of it runs on the run's own clock, so it stills on pause and slows in a slow-motion replay. The man and the woman are still dressed in code, and nobody else on the mountain wears the tourer's kit.

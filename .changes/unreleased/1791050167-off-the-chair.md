Getting off a chairlift on a free ride now plays out like the real thing, and the camera stays with you all the way. The camera rides close behind you on the chair, with the rope running on up to the top station ahead, and you sit back in the seat rather than through its backrest. At the top you stand up and slide straight down the unload ramp into a lane, with the station house on your left. Across the end of the lane, run signs point left or right, one arrow per run in its grade's colour, and you're led off the way your run's sign points and around the house if the run lies behind it. The camera holds close through the stand-up, the ramp and the lane, then opens out to the chase view as you ski off. The chair you got off carries on empty round the wheel instead of popping out of sight.

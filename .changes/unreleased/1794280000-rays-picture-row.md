title: A RAYS row in OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE
---

A new picture row, RAYS, works out light by marching rays through each frame on the WebGPU renderer. LOW darkens the snow where things crowd round it: at a trunk's foot, under the boughs, along a wall and in a furrow. HIGH adds the short shadows a boot, a ski or a gate's foot casts where it meets the snow. MAX also makes lakes and ice mirror the slopes and the sky around them. RAYS is on at HIGH in the HIGH preset and off in LOW and MEDIUM. PRESET ▸ AUTO weighs it like every other row, and the classic WebGL renderer never draws it.

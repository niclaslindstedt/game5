type: Fixed
title: The skier no longer bows his face down to his skis when riding fast
---

Riding fast in a tuck, a compression, a roller or a landing no longer folds the skier over at the waist until his head is below his hips and down by his skis: his knees are already as bent as knees go in a tuck, so the jolt is now taken in his hips while his back stays level and his head stays up. Stood up, a landing still bends him forward, but only as far as his shins lean. And braking down a pitch, or simply gathering speed, no longer sinks him slowly into a deep squat.

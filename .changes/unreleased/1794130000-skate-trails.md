title: Skating leaves the tracks its skis make
---

The marks a skier leaves in the snow now follow each ski where it is drawn. Skating and poling, each ski leaves one clean straight stroke angled along its arm of the V, from when his weight comes onto it to the end of its push, and the next ski's stroke runs the other way. The ski swung back in for the next stride leaves no mark. Before, a skater left two straight parallel lines, or a smeared band in powder. A skid's tracks now also lie across the way the skis do.

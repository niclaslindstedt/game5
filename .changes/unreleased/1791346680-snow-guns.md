title: Snow guns in thin seasons
---

Early and late in the season, on a day it is not snowing, a ski area's runs carry snow guns at their hydrants: fan guns on two-wheeled carriages and up on padded columns, and lances leaned over the run, on the windward edge past the groomed width and aimed so their cone lands on the piste. A skier meets one as he meets a floodlight mast. On a free ride, whenever the air is cold enough, they run: each fan gun sweeps its drum side to side and throws a dense white jet that arcs across the run and settles as a drifting veil of ice, the lances mist from their heads, the floodlights light the plumes after dark, and under each one lies a whale of heavy machine snow a skier ploughs into.

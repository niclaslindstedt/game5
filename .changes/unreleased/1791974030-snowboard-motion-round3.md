The snowboarder moves more smoothly through tricks and landings. A method grab no longer swings his head round at take-off, the boots are no longer thrown up and back at the top of a spin, a landing is taken in the knees over a moment instead of in one frame, his hips settle into a landing rather than stopping dead on it, and his elbows and knees swing round instead of flipping when a grab is caught or let go.

type: Fixed
title: Run signs stand at the piste's edge
---

A run's sign no longer stands out in the middle of a wide slope: it stands at the piste's edge, just inside the stakes, on the side the lift (or the ramp off its top) arrives from, so a skier coming down the unload ramp sees it as he comes onto the run. A lane's junction sign stands at the edge of the run it leaves, too.

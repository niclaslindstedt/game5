The summer's downhill bike and its rider are now modelled. The bike has a long, low frame with a coil shock in the front triangle, a dual-crown fork on gold stanchions, a box swingarm, riser bars with levers and hoses, a chain guide, pinned flat pedals, big disc brakes, and knobbed tyres on spoked wheels. Its suspension, steering, wheels and cranks move with the ride, and only the coil stretches as the shock works. The rider wears downhill kit: a full-face helmet with a peak and goggles, a loose jersey, baggy shorts over knee and shin pads, flat shoes and gloves wrapped round the grips. He moves with every pose of the rider, including riding, pushing the bike and falling.

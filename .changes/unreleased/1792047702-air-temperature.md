The air has a temperature, and the snow answers to it. A free ride's start card has a TEMPERATURE row beside WEATHER: AUTO shows the air the mountain's own day and sky give at the valley floor, or pick −20 to +10 °C. The day's air follows it — colder up the mountain, coldest at dawn, warmest in the afternoon. Warm air wets the snow under any sky, a spring sun wets it only a few degrees under zero and never in deep cold, the piste goes to slush on a warm afternoon and freezes hard only once the air falls below zero, a snowfall through warm air lands heavy and wet, and the snow guns run only when it is cold enough. QUICK START rolls an air that fits the season, hour and sky it deals. The jump plane's outside-air gauge reads the real air at its height, and a link's `?temp=` sets the air for a lab.

type: Added
title: The jump out of a craft shot like a film
---

Jumping out of the plane, off the helicopter's skid, over the balloon's rim or out of the paramotor's rig now plays as a short film: the camera starts right in the skier's face as he leaves and pulls back to show the craft behind him, switches to his own eyes for the dive and the look back up at the craft, and ends on a long lens from the craft down at him falling away. The leap runs in slow motion, the screen is letterboxed and the readouts step aside; a pull, a fall or a change of camera ends it at once.

type: Fixed
title: Trees no longer switch their detail in front of you
---

Trees used to swap to a simpler or more detailed shape in a single frame as you skied toward or away from them, so you could see a wood change as you watched. Now a tree that crosses one of those distances fades smoothly into its other shape over a moment. Trees at the far edge of the woods fade in instead of appearing suddenly. A tree near the edge no longer flickers back and forth while you stand still or the camera bobs.

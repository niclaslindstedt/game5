The snowmobile's rider's-eye camera is now a cockpit view. Stood over the
bars, the rider sees his own gloved hands on the grips, the thumb throttle
that moves with the throttle, the brake lever his fingers pull, the red
emergency stop, the switch cluster for the warmers, reverse and the
headlamp, the mountain strap and the hand guards, the tether clipped in,
and a live display reading the speed, the engine's revs against the red
line, the coolant, the fuel, the altitude and the clock. The bars turn with
the skis.

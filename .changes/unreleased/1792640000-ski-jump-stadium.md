A ski jump's out-run is now a stadium: grandstands packed down both sides, terraces across its foot on a counter-slope behind the back boards and their exit gate, the leader's box, banners down the walls, flags streaming in the hill's wind, and the crowd standing up the landing hill behind nets. The video wall shows the jump as it happens — the gate and the distance to beat on the bar, the speed in the in-run and the air, the distance landed — and tall floodlight masts round the arena light it for a night contest.

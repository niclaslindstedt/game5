title: The snowmobile rider leans smoothly and is thrown off properly
---

On the snowmobile, the rider now hangs his weight into a turn smoothly — hips across, knees bent, trunk leant in — instead of sinking low and then jumping upright at the end of the lean; and an amateur shouldered hard enough to throw him now throws him off the machine properly, so he tumbles, is stood up and is put back on it, rather than being left frozen standing beside it with the reset doing nothing.

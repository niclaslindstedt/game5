title: A ski jumping hill, first on the tricks card
---

Ski jumping is on its way. The tricks card now lists SKI JUMPING first, and choosing it puts you at the top gate of a large jumping hill (HS 140, K 126) cut straight down the mountain. The hill is laid out to the construction standard: a 35° in-run in tracks, a table at 11° ending at the edge, the knoll, P, K and the landing radius down to the hill size, and a level out-run. You ride it on the Albatross, a new pair of jumping skis 2.54 m long. For now the in-run holds you in the crouch and you roll off the edge with no take-off of your own. The take-off, the flight, the telemark and the scoring come next.

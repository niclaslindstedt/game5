title: The army's waves bring carriers, trucks and tanks
---

From three stars the ground waves now come up the mountain with their vehicles. Tracked over-snow carriers drive their section up close and put the men down out of the back. Trucks come up the village's road and stop where it gets too steep or where you could see them, and their men climb on from there. Tanks in winter white and grey climb wherever their tracks hold, halt, turn the turret and fire the main gun at you. The shell's burst throws you off your skis, throws up the snow, leaves a crater and damages any machine near it. With INJURIES on a tank that gets close will run you down. With SAFE FOR WORK on nothing they do wounds you: the tanks fire only warning shots well wide of you and never run you down, and their men arrest you. You hear each vehicle's diesel, and the tracks clank and squeal as they come.

**Summer on the free ride.** The start card's SEASON row now ends in SUMMER, mid-July. The mountain's snow is gone: hay meadows and grazed pasture below the tree line, turf, heath, scree and rock above it, and a few grey snow patches left in the high hollows. The ski runs are short-grass corridors with bare scars and gravel cat tracks, the lakes are open, and grass and flowers sway in the wind round you. The trees are bare of snow and in leaf, and the roofs are bare too. In summer the sky never snows, the drag lifts and wind tunnels stand still, and the start is an aircraft: the paramotor, the balloon, the helicopter, the jump plane or the wingsuit. Links take `?summer=1`.

title: The snow gives way under you
---

The snow round you now gives way as a volume at TRAILS MEDIUM and HIGH: a
skidded turn or a stop digs a real hole and banks its berm ahead of the
edge, a fall leaves a ragged crater, and the walls slump to the snow's
angle of repose. The hole is made of exactly the snow the spray threw, lit
by the same sky as the rest of the slope and self-shadowed under a low sun.

title: The free ride's panorama hangs under a blue sky, and its runs are numbered without gaps
---

The painted mountain on the free ride's start card now fills its frame edge to edge, cut off cleanly at all four sides. A clear blue sky with a few fair-weather clouds sits behind the summit ridge, and a cloud can drift behind a peak. The valley floor runs on to the bottom of the picture.

The runs are now numbered 1, 2, 3 and on with no numbers missing. The number on the map is the same one the sign at the head of the run and the news line show. Before, the numbers skipped, for example 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12.

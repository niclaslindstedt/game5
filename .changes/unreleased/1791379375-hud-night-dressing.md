title: The HUD and the minimap dip at night
---

After dark the HUD dips with the floodlights, as a dashboard does when its
headlamps come on: the white ink goes to a soft grey-blue, the plates and the
shadows go nearly black, the presses' rims and highlights dim, and the
minimap's ground goes from bright snow to a moonlit dark with the runs, the
piste and the lifts left light over it. It ramps with the lamps through the
dusk rather than switching, and the warnings, the flag's red and the gap's
green never dim.

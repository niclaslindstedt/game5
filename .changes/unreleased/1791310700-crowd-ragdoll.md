title: The crowd falls like a body
---

On a free ride, an amateur who goes down — shouldered by you, landing a kicker on his back, or losing it on his own — now falls on a ragdoll of his own: he goes over, slides and tumbles to a stop in the snow, lies there a moment, and gets back up on his skis across the slope before turning them down the hill again. Before, a fallen amateur just tipped over stiffly.

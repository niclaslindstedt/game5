Standing still, holding only left or right now turns you on the spot. The skier steps his skis round one at a time, the way you would in real life. The inside ski's tip is lifted and set down further round, then the other ski is brought alongside, about 30° a second, until you face where you want to go. He doesn't slide forward or roll onto an edge while he does it. Let go and he finishes the step he is in, then stands with his skis together. Without poles, he holds his arms out for balance while he steps. Hold the tuck as well and he skates off round the turn instead.

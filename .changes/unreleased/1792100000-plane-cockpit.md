title: The jump plane's cockpit
---

The jump plane's cockpit: from the HELMET rung the pilot's own eye in the left seat looks out over the long nose past the propeller's disc, through faintly tinted glass, the lens rolled with the airframe and turned a little into a turn. The panel under its glareshield carries two live glass displays (the flight display and the engine page with its terrain map), the standbys and the warning lights, all read off the plane, its lettering backlit after dark; the sticks, pedals, engine levers, flap lever and trim wheel move with the controls, and the pilot sits with his hands on the stick and the power lever, seen through the glass from outside.

type: Fixed
title: Free ride skiers no longer freeze in a stop
---

Skiers on a free ride no longer get stuck mid-stop. One who stopped on a steep pitch could slide on slowly for minutes, frozen in the pose of a skidded stop, and the rest of his group would stand waiting for him. A stop is now braked all the way to a standstill, and he stands still on his edges. It also always ends on time, however steep the slope.

title: A fall from a height landed on the feet breaks the legs
---

Landing on your feet from a big fall, such as off the helicopter, now breaks the leg bones the way a real fall from a height does, from the bottom up. That means the heel bone, the ankle end of the shin bone, the top of the shin bone in the knee, the thigh bone and the hip socket, each at its measured breaking load. Before, a landing hurt only the spine, the knees' ligaments and the neck, so even a 130 g landing left the legs whole. A hard enough landing now breaks both legs at once instead of stopping at three injuries, and a heavier skier breaks sooner. Ordinary jumps and clean landings still hurt nothing.

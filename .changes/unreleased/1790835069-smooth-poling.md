type: Fixed
title: The skier poles and skates away smoothly instead of twitching
---

Pushing off and gaining speed no longer jerks the skier about. When skating, his weight now moves across from one ski to the other during each push, instead of snapping from side to side every stride. His poles swing through a real plant, push and recovery as rigid poles, where before the baskets jumped between the snow and his hands. The double pole looks calmer and more like an alpine skier pushing out of a start. He bows less deeply, drives his arms back until they are straight, and keeps his elbows in, where before he looked like he had his hands on his hips and his elbows out like wings. In the diagonal stride his arms now swing opposite each other like a walker's.

title: Dive, swing up and loop the paramotor
---

Under the paramotor's wing, the lean forward now pulls the front risers and the wing pitches over into a steep, fast dive. The lean back pulls the rear risers and swings you out in front of the wing and up. How hard the rears bite depends on how fast you were going when you pulled, so from a cruise you only climb a little. Pull out of a dive of about five seconds and you swing up, over the top and round in a loop at around ten g, read off a new G cell on the HUD. Pull without the speed for it, or hold the lean back too long, and the lines go slack and you fall into your own wing, tangled and tumbling. Press the machine key to cut it away, or ride it into the snow.

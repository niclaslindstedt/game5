---
type: Changed
title: The helicopter rolls and loops, reads its altitude and climbs harder
---

The helicopter no longer stops at a steep lean. Hold the cyclic over and the rotor carries on past the vertical and round, so with height under you it can be ROLLED and LOOPED; the horizon on the HUD turns over with it.

The HUD reads the machine's ALTITUDE over the pad it took off from beside the drop under its skids.

It climbs harder: the rotor has more power (nearly twice its weight at the hover, its weight alone at about 70 km/h of climb), and the collective moves faster on the keys and the power pad.

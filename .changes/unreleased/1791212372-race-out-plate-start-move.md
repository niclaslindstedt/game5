---
type: Changed
title: A plain disqualification plate and a start shot that moves instead of cutting
---

A slalom, downhill or super-G run that goes out now ends on a plate that says only DISQUALIFIED or DID NOT FINISH, why under it, and a TRY AGAIN press that skis the run again — no board, no course name, no other presses. And in the start house the television's lens no longer cuts from over the door to behind the racer: it moves down off the roof and settles behind him before GO.

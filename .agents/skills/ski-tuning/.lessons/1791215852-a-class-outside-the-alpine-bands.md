The Peregrine (speed skiing's pair) is 2.40 m, a ~285 m sidecut, 27 kg of kit and a 0.08 m² tuck — past every alpine band `catalog_test.ts` held the catalog to. The bands now hold the alpine pairs and a test of its own holds it to its sport's rule. Its `topSpeed` (316 km/h on the 20° pitch) is the terminal speed: no strip in the suite is long enough to reach it in the 30 s the others settle in, so the test holds it to `terminalSpeed` and the physics to three quarters of it, still gathering. Its racer is the catalog's 80 kg skier like every pair's (`riders_test.ts` holds `withRider`'s medium build to the row itself) — the class's heavy men are the HEAVY build. And a pair that outruns the downhill ski leaves the Eagle best at nothing on the ski card: the card got the axis that IS the downhill class (FAST BEND, the bend held at 130 km/h, `fastCarveOf`), never a nudged number.

The free ride's start card picks a generated mountain the way it picks a real one: the RANGE row lists the real ranges by name and ends on GENERATED, whose AREA is the kind of snow country (alpine, fell, continental or maritime) and whose MOUNTAIN is its number, typed or stepped, with ANOTHER MOUNTAIN still dealing the next; a real range's AREA and PART are its ski areas and their parts as before. The separate map and climate rows are gone, and stored rides and links read as before.

type: Added
title: Every mountain is a whole ski area
---

Each map is now a ski area rather than a single piste. Lifts climb to the bench, the peak and the shoulder. Green, blue, red and black pistes come down the mountain and merge into one another. Cat tracks cross the face between the sectors and run down to the lifts' bottom stations. The pistes are broad, open out above the tree line and close to a neck through the woods every few hundred metres. A race or a time trial is run on one course down the area; the rest of the mountain is there to ski. The gates now alternate left and right as a slalom, and a missed gate costs three seconds instead of the run. The woods grow thicker and taller lower down the mountain. The campaign's four shelves are four ski areas, raced down their red and black courses — one blue to warm up on at the very start — turning blacker and steeper shelf by shelf; the greens and blues are the free ride's. Campaign progress from the graded ladder does not carry over.

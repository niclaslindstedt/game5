---
type: Changed
title: Enter gets you on and off the helicopter and the snowmobile; P takes a picture
---

On a free ride, Enter is now the key for the machines: stop beside the helicopter's right skid or the snowmobile and press Enter to get on, and press it again to get off — to jump off the helicopter's skid, step off where it has landed, or ski off the snowmobile. Skiing past or into a machine no longer puts you on it, and the HUD says ENTER TO FLY or ENTER TO RIDE once you are close enough. On touch, a double tap on either thumb does the same. Space no longer takes you off the helicopter. The screenshot key moves from Enter to P; a layout you saved with the shutter on Enter moves to P on its own. Both keys can be rebound on OPTIONS ▸ KEYS (the GET ON / OFF and SHOT rows).

title: Slopestyle, the second freestyle contest, and the first rails
---

The tricks card now has SLOPESTYLE. The game builds a whole course down the mountain: three rail sections, each with a rail and a box side by side on two lines, then three jumps that grow from the first to the last, and the finish. The course is shaped so the slope alone gives you the right speed at every feature: slow through the rails, fast off the jumps.

RAILS AND BOXES are new. Meet one at its end, going along it, and you slide down it. On it, tap the edge to swap between a 50-50 and a slide across it, hold the lean to press the nose or the tail, and jump to pop off early. Let it run out and you are turned square and ride away.

The judges mark each run section by section, as the top level does: two judges on each of the six sections and three on the whole run's flow and variety, weighted 60/40. A fall ends the run. The contest is a qualification of two runs and a final of three for the best twelve of thirty, the best run counting each time. The HUD shows your run and the section you are in; the plate shows each section's mark, the two panels and the board.

Picking slopestyle opens the ski card on the Raven at the medium build. `?start=slopestyle` boots straight into the first run.

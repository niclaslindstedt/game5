title: The sun and the moon stand where they really stand
---

The sky is reckoned for a real date and place — the sun's declination and the equation of time, the air lifting the low sun, sunrise at its upper limb, the midnight sun and the polar night, and a moon on its real orbit and phase — and a real face is skied at its own latitude, longitude and true north, in its own winter south of the equator.

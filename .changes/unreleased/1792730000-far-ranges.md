The horizon is no longer empty: real ranges now stand round every map out to the horizon, 60 to 100 km by the DISTANCE row. On a generated map the country is dealt in its region's character, with valleys leading out and no step at the map's edge. On a real face it is the real country round it to 60 km, fetched with the face. The ranges are lit by the moving sun, so the peaks keep their alpenglow after the valleys fall into shade and the shadow line climbs them at dusk. They carry snow above the tree line, woods below it and rock on the steep faces, and they fade into the air layer by layer.

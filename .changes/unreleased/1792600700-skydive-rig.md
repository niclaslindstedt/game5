The parachute rig a skydiver wears now looks like a real sport rig, on skis and on a snowboard. It is a slim, rounded container on the upper back with coloured side walls, bound edges, a reserve pin cover, a main pin cover and the pilot chute's pouch. Padded riser covers run over the shoulders to the three rings, and the harness has lift webs, a chest strap, laterals and padded leg straps. The old version was a box standing off his back with straps floating over his head.

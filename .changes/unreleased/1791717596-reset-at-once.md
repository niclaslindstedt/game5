---
type: Changed
title: Restart straight away after a fall
---

The reset lights red the moment a fall is final — thrown off the skis, the snowmobile, a crashed helicopter, plane or balloon, a skydive that ends in the snow or caught in a tree — and answers at once instead of after a few seconds. Down too hurt to rise or dying, it starts the run again straight away; the RESTART AFTER setting still says when that happens on its own.

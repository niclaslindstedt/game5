title: Look round from the lift
---

Riding a lift you can look round: drag a finger anywhere on the screen on a phone, or hold the mouse button and drag on a desktop, and the camera swings about you — back down the valley, down onto the run under the chair, up the rope to the top station. It swings home behind you as the lift lets you go at the top, or when you jump off. A finger dragging to look is never taken as the tuck held to skip the lift.

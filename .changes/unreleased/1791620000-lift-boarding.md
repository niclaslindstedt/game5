type: Changed
title: Turning into a lift queue, a gondola boarded on its platform, and no thumb pads while looking round
---

Riding into a lift's boarding ring facing the wrong way no longer swivels the skier round while he slides along the queue's lane: coming in fast and turned far off it he stops first, the skis thrown across; at a crawl he steps his skis round on the spot a pair at a time; rolling and turned less he skates round to it, a step turned into each push — and then skates up the lane. A gondola is boarded as a real one is: through the door at the back of its hall you come out on the platform beside the wheel, your cabin comes round the wheel to you at a crawl with its doors sliding open, you rack your skis on its door, step in and sit down on the back bench while it creeps on, its doors shut and it is taken back onto the rope and away. And on touch, while a lift carries you, the thumbs' pads are no longer drawn under a finger dragged to look round.

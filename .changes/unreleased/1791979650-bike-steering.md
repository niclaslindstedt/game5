title: The bike steers at once, and too tight a turn throws you
---

The downhill bike turns the moment you steer: the rider lays the bike over with his body as well as the bars, so the turn bites in about a quarter of a second instead of swinging the wrong way first. A turn held tighter than the tyres can take now slides them out and throws you, while a quick stab of full bars is still a turn at the grip.

title: The wind never blows up the mountain
---

The wind no longer blows up the mountain into a skier's face. A storm straight up the fall line made the run a crawl, and every sky picked on the free ride's WEATHER row, or with a `?weather=` link, used to blow that way. The wind now comes across the slope or down it, from any direction in that half and with straight down as likely as any other. It helps a run along rather than stopping it. A picked sky now blows from the direction that map's own wind was dealt, so it is not always the same. No map has moved.

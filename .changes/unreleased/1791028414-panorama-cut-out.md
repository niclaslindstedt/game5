type: Changed
title: The free ride's panorama shows only the mountain, with lanes drawn as cat tracks
---

The painted mountain on the free ride's start card is now cut out against the card. You no longer see the map's icy rim down the sides, the made-up far ranges or the sky, or a pale band of snow behind the summit ridge. The ridge is the skyline, and the mountain fades out softly at its sides and along the valley floor.

The transport lanes are now drawn as thin dashed cat tracks instead of bands as heavy as a piste. The woods no longer bury them, so every run and lane shows its cut through the trees.

title: Dual moguls, the seventh freestyle contest
---

The tricks card now has DUAL MOGULS: moguls head to head. The game lays a moguls course twice side by side — a blue lane and a red one 6.5 m apart on one rhythm, the air bumps level across both — down the mountain.

First a qualification, one moguls run alone in the blue lane; the best sixteen go into a ladder. Then the duals: "blue course ready… red course ready", both gates drop together and a rival skis the other lane beside you. Both skis over the centre line, or ten seconds stopped, and you are out. Once both lanes are down, seven judges split five votes each between you — four on the turns, two on the air, one on the speed off the gap at the line — and the majority goes through, from the eighth-finals to the small and the big final. The HUD shows the round, your lane and your rival; the plate shows the votes and how they split, the board and the next dual.

Picking dual moguls opens the ski card on the Ibex at the medium build. `?start=dual` boots straight into it.

Two new bodies on the rider card: the HIKER, as a man or a woman, each modelled whole in a touring guide's kit. The kit is a red hardshell, charcoal pants and a grey helmet with a lamp and glacier glasses. The pants have dark reinforced knee panels, stitched and darted the way touring pants are, and kick patches inside the lower legs. Over that go a lime harness and an orange-and-white rope coiled round the chest. A graphite pack with a pale face panel and an ice axe rides on the back. The woman is cut to the women's measures and wears her braid out from under the helmet. With a hiker picked, the card asks only the WEIGHT, and the weight now reshapes the model too: a heavy hiker is broader and a light one narrower. No rival is ever dealt a hiker. Both ride every animation the code's skier does. A broken arm hangs from the break inside the sleeve. The pack, the coil and the rope's tail swing on springs.

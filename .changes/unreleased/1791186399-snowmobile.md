title: A mountain snowmobile waits at the bottom of every free ride — ski into it to ride it up the mountain
---

Every free ride has a deep-snow mountain sled parked at the bottom of the mountain beside the village, and the start card's RUN row has a SNOWMOBILE stop that begins the ride stood on its boards (`?sled=1` from a link). Ski into it slowly to take it: your skis and poles go onto the rack on its tunnel and you stand on its running boards in your boots. The tuck is the throttle, the back key the brake, the edge the bars and the leans your weight forward and back. Press the jump twice, or double tap on touch, to step off onto your skis again. The machine stays where you left it and idles a while before it shuts off.

It rides like the long-track machines built for climbing: it sits down into powder at rest and climbs out onto it as it gathers speed. It climbs faces no lift reaches, and it bogs into a high-mark when the slope is too steep. In powder it carves onto its belt's edge under your weight. The belt cuts a trench and the skis cut grooves beside it, and both stay in the snow. A spinning belt throws a roost of powder that hangs in the air. Run it into a trunk, roll it or land it too hard and it throws you; when you stand up again you are back on it.

It is a Blender model posed off the physics every frame: the bars, the skis on their springs, the rear suspension and the paddles running round the belt. It is heard as a two-stroke twin, with the expansion chamber's rasp on the pipe, the belt's whine and the paddles churning powder. The HUD reads its revs on a tachometer.

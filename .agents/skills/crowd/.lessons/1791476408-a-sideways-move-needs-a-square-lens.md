The second dance was rebuilt as a step-touch side to side, and on the moves
sheet (strobed three-quarter to side-on) it still looked like the first: a
step across the body is foreshortened to nothing. The sheet turns a dance
square to its lens now; judge any lateral move that way before changing the
move again. The same trap hid the shouldered skis: built flat across the
shoulder, the pair is seen edge-on from a standing eye a few metres off, so
two skis with a gap read as one plank in the game though the sheet's close
cell showed both. Turning the pair half onto its edges reads from behind and
from the side. Look at the GAME's view (`--sheet=resort --views=walker`), not
only the sheet's cell.

title: Cars, the ski bus and bicycles on the village's streets
---

The village at the bottom of the mountain now has traffic. Cars of four kinds — small hatchbacks, estates, SUVs and vans — drive round its streets on the right, turn at its junctions with their indicators flashing, come in off the road to park in the car park and leave again. A ski bus pulls in to its stop and stands there a while, and a few cyclists in winter clothes pedal along near the kerb. The kerbs and the car park are lined with parked cars, fuller by day, most of them under the night's snow; some cars carry a roof box or skis on a rack. After dark the head, tail and brake lights glow, the nearest cars light the snow ahead of them and the bus's windows are lit. On a free ride a skier who runs into a vehicle is knocked down.

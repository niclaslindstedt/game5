The modelled racer on the rider card now wears a national team's race kit: a red suit fading through coral to white below the knee, fluorescent shin guards edged in black, black arm pads, knuckle shields and a red helmet with a white crown band. His bib floats off him in the air and slaps back down on a landing, and his suit ripples on its lee side at speed, both on the run's own clock so a replay shows them too. The WEIGHT row now builds him heavier or lighter, and on a giant slalom or ski cross pair he leaves the gate guards off, on a speed pair the arm pads as well.

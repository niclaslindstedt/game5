The skier riding the helicopter's skid now has to hold on. Rolled or looped
past what his hands can carry, his grip gives out and he falls off. If the
machine is upside down over him he falls through the main rotor, which
strikes every part of him it passes. With injuries on, it tears him apart in
a fan of blood, flesh and bone, smears the rotor disc red, and he dies of the
rotor, shown in the X-ray cam.

Sat on the skid, he now sways with the machine's lurches, rights his head
against the bank and braces his hands on the tube when it drops or banks
hard. As it turns over he slides off the seat and hangs from both hands,
swinging and kicking, before he lets go.

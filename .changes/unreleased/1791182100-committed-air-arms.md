type: Changed
title: A skier leaning in the air is committed, and the windmill's poles turn with his fists
---

Holding a lean in the air now reads as a skier committed to his line: the arms set forward and still, and he never windmills while he leans — circling arms are kept for a skier dropping a cliff without a lean, fighting a rotation he did not ask for. When he does windmill, each pole turns with its fist at the grip it hung in, sweeping past his hip, up behind his shoulder and forward over his head, instead of sticking straight out of the hand.

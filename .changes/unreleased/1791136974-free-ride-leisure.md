---
type: Changed
title: The free ride is leisure — no run figures, and reset and restart that know where you have skied
---

A free ride no longer draws a race's figures at the top left of the screen: the clock, the best air, the distance skied and the map's seed are gone from over the snow (the best air and the distance are still on the pause card). The speed, the edge bar, the air clock and the minimap stay.

A free ride owes no gate, so its reset and restart no longer think in gates. The ride remembers every run you have had your skis on, starting with the one you were put on. The HUD's reset (R) stands you back on the nearest of those runs, never on one you have not been near, and no longer sends you back up to the start line from the foot of the mountain. Restart (B, or RESTART on the pause card) takes you to the top of the slope again: you stand at the head of the last piste you skied, facing down it.

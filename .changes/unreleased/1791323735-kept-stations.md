title: Lift stations and cat tracks stay rideable in a snowy free ride
---

On a free ride under a snowing sky, the day's new snow no longer lies on the ground a ski area keeps clear: the pads and decks at the top of every lift, the ramps down onto the runs, the load zones at the bottom and the transport lanes between runs. A rider stepping off a gondola or a chair in a storm used to sink into the flat deck, crawl to a stop and be reset; now that ground stays packed, carrying only the snow that has fallen since the ride began, while the runs themselves still carry the day's fall.

title: A busier base area — people going round between the lifts, children at play, guests skating across the bottom
---

The base of a free ride's ski area is now a crossroads at peak hours. About three times as many people stand and walk about the village and the lifts' feet by day, and far more of them are on the move. Guests go round the base from one lift's foot to the next and on to the village, skis on a shoulder, families with the children at their side. Friends wait at meeting points with their skis. A few guests still on their skis skate slowly across the valley floor between the slopes. Children throw snowballs that fly across the snow, and build snowmen that stand at different stages, one of them rolling the next ball over, growing as it goes. It thins out through the afternoon, and after dark only a handful are left.

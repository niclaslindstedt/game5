title: The police come after a death
---

With INJURIES on, a run that ends with the skier dead no longer gets the air ambulance. A police helicopter comes instead, in a dark blue livery with a blue and yellow chequer. Its three officers work the place as the scene of a fatal accident: they walk it from where he struck to where he lies, run red-and-white tape round all of it, stand a numbered yellow marker at every piece of evidence (the impact, blood, anything torn off, his skis, the body), and photograph it all with flashes. Then two patrollers carry a body bag in down the hill, the officers roll him into it just as he lay, zip it shut over him and carry him off; the machine is on the snow for two and a half to four minutes, landing as near him as the cordon and the slope allow. The landed machine is solid, and the officers can be knocked over. Like the air ambulance, the police helicopter can be stolen off the snow and flown away. It is a model of its own: POLICE on its sides and belly, a camera turret under the nose, a searchlight, a loudspeaker and blue beacons that flash while its rotor turns.

title: Hop round to ride backwards
---

On a free ride and a tricks run, jumping with left or right held now hops the skier half round in the air so he lands riding backwards (switch). It works under 40 km/h, and the direction can also be pressed just after the jump. Off a kicker the same press is still a 180 trick. As he loads the jump his shoulders wind up against the turn. At the jump his head leads round over the shoulder he turns to, and he lands looking back down the hill over it. After the hop he stays backwards for two seconds however slowly he is going, before the automatic turn back to forwards can take over.

On the HIGH forest setting the nearest trees, out to about thirty metres, are drawn with their needles on cards: layered boughs carrying pillows of snow over dark gaps, bare trunks under old spruce, larch and birch as a haze of twigs, and a glow round a crown against a low sun. A depth pre-pass draws only the needles in front, the cards fade into the rest of the woods without a pop, and LOW and MEDIUM are unchanged.

title: Crashing into the A-nets
---

A downhiller or super-G racer who drives into the tall orange safety nets now goes down in them instead of being stopped flat against them. The mesh billows back as much as two metres round him and drags him to a stop along it. He drops to its foot and stays there, tangled in the pocket his weight leaves, as racers do in a real net crash. His skis come off and three times in four each one hooks in the mesh by its tip or its tail and is left hanging there. The drawn net bulges round whatever it holds. A light brush along the net is still skied out. The world lab photographs the crash frame by frame with `make world ARGS="--downhill --views=net-0.4,net-1,net-3"`.

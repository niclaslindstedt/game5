title: The halfpipe, the fifth freestyle contest
---

The tricks card now has the HALFPIPE. The game cuts a 22-foot pipe into the mountain: two walls 6.7 m high and 20 m apart, their tops standing nearly vertical, 170 m long down an 18° pitch, with a start platform above it and a finish line below.

You ride it wall to wall: across the flat at an angle, up a wall and straight out over its top, and back down onto the same wall, five or six hits a run. Tuck on the flat and stand up through the curve of the wall to pump your speed back. In the air spin, flip and grab as anywhere; a spin turned uphill is an ALLEY-OOP. Each run is judged as one — the height over the top of the wall, the difficulty, the landings, the variety and how well you used the pipe — with a landing on the flat or the deck taken off and a fall scored low. Two qualification runs with the best one counting, then the best twelve of twenty-five to a final of three. The HUD counts your hits and the last one's height, and the plate names every hit's trick.

Picking the halfpipe opens the ski card on the Raven at the medium build. `?start=halfpipe` boots straight into it.

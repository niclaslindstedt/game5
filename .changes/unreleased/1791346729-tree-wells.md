title: Tree wells in deep powder
---

In deep powder every trunk now stands in a TREE WELL, as real ones do: a
funnel of loose snow round the trunk, deepest beside it and opening
further on the downhill side, shadowed under the boughs, the trunk going
straight down into it. Ski too close and you slide in; the loose walls fall in
on you and you sink — rock out of it or reset. Skiing past at speed you
dip through and ride on. None at the ordinary snow, so races are
unchanged; the deeper the snow dial, the deeper the wells.

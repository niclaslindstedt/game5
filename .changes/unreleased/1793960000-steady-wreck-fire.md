type: Fixed
title: Steadier fire on a crashed helicopter or plane
---

The fire on a burning helicopter or plane wreck no longer flickers. The texture inside the flames now flows upward with the fire instead of churning from frame to frame. The pool of burning fuel has a steady, glowing base, with flames puffing and tearing off above it as a real pool fire does. Tongues of flame fade in and out instead of popping, and they no longer jump in front of one another. The fire's glow on the snow breathes slowly instead of strobing. The flames also stay as smooth after a long ride as on the first crash.

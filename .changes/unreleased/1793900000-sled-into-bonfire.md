Riding the snowmobile into a bonfire or a camp fire sets it alight. A moment later its tank explodes in a fireball that blows you off it, your clothes burning, and the wreck burns black for a minute and a half before a fresh machine waits at the bottom. If you were watching from the cockpit, the view stays in your head as you are thrown, through the flash, the heat and the smoke. With safe for work on, the machine is held off the fire.

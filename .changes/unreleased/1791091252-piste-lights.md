Floodlight masts down every run that light up as evening falls: a mast about every fifty metres on one side of the piste (both sides on a very wide one), ten to eighteen metres tall by the piste's width, carrying one to three neutral-white LED floods aimed down and across the run. They light the piste to some 30 lux, inside the recreational class for night skiing, and their beams are cut off at the edge, so the woods and the snow between the runs stay dark under the moon. The lamps glow on their masts, so a lit run reads as a string of lights from across the valley, and the falling snow and the snow a skier throws up catch their light.

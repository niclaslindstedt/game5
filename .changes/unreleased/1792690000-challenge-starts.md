**Timed lines start where the slope dips.** A free ride challenge's start gate no longer stands on the flat just under the lift's top, where you crossed it at about 30 km/h; it stands down the run where the slope has dropped away and a skier coming off the top is already moving at around 50–55 km/h, so the clock starts with speed under you. Par and the medals are re-measured for the new starts.

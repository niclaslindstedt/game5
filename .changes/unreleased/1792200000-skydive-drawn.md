title: The skydive as drawn
---

The skydive is drawn whole. The jumper wears a harness and container with its leg straps, the pilot chute's handle at the foot of the container, the red cut-away pad and the reserve's metal handle. He is posed through every stage, his skis on: poised in the door, the exit, the box, the track and head down, turns, the reach and the throw, the opening shock, hanging on the toggles, the flare, the cut-away and hanging snagged.

The opening plays frame by frame: the pilot chute thrown, the bridle, the bag lifted and tumbling, line stretch, the snivel, the slider coming down the lines and the cells filling from the middle out. The canopy is a nine-cell sport main built on the paramotor's wing, flown on its four risers and two steering lines. A canopy cut away crumples and streams down, then lies on the snow. One caught in a crown or over a lift's rope hangs there with him beneath it.

New lenses: a camera flyer beside him in freefall, his own eyes ahead and down (HELMET) or back past his skis (TIPS), and a lens that pulls back to keep the whole opening in frame. The skydive bot now flies a downwind, base and final pattern to its landing. The skier's big speed number gives way while a plane or a canopy shows its own readout.

type: Added
title: Watch the run so far — from the pause card, with V, or right after a crash
---

Every run you ride is now recorded, a free ride too, and can be watched back before it is over. WATCH REPLAY on the pause card plays the run so far from its start; V mid-run plays the last stretch; and after a wipeout, an injury or a death a WATCH THAT AGAIN press comes up for a few seconds that opens just before the fall. The replay is a video player's: a scrubber with the run's moments marked on it, play and pause, five seconds back and forward, a frame at a time, four speeds (¼× to 2×) and the cameras, with the bar stepping out of the picture while it plays. BACK hands you the run exactly where you left it.

Sunrise is warm again. The low sun now tints the distant air gold instead of lavender. Snow in the mountain's shadow under a low sun stays blue but is no longer close to black. The tall flurry clouds billow instead of streaking at their sides. On the higher picture settings, overcast, snow and storm cloud decks are as bright as on the medium setting. The edge of a thin high cloud on the low setting fades into the blue instead of ending sharply. Seen from above at golden hour, the low cloud deck is a sea of soft lumps with warm tops and cool troughs instead of a flat magenta sheet.

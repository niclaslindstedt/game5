title: An even skate off a standstill, and pole plants you see and hear together
---

Skating off a standstill across a slope no longer has the skier sit deep on one stride and stand tall on the next. His legs had been taking the climb of his own uphill glide as a bump. After a fall and a reset he now stands up straight, instead of skating his first strides still folded from the crash. A pole plant now sounds only when the figure actually plants a pole. It no longer clicks on after his arms have given the poles up for the tuck. It also sounds like the snow it goes into: a tick on the groomer, a soft pat in loose snow, and nothing at all in deep powder.

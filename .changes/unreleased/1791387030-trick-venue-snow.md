title: The halfpipe, moguls and aerials snow no longer floats
---

The halfpipe's walls, the moguls course and the aerials site are drawn as a snow surface of their own over the mountain, and that surface was facing down: from above it was invisible, so the rough ground cut under it showed instead, and its underside hung in the air as a grey sheet wherever that ground dipped below it — across the halfpipe's mouth, along its copings and over the moguls. It faces up now, so the pipe's transitions and verts and every mogul are drawn as built, with nothing hovering over them.

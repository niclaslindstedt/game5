# The pinned maps: one map for every player

A measured run is raced on a PINNED MAP — a SEED on a generator VERSION,
one of its ski area's COURSES (R28) of a GRADE (R23) in a kind of country
(R21), ridden on the day its course was dealt (or a `sky` laid over it),
with the DIGEST of the map that came out written beside it. The same
generator builds it on the version it was curated under, so it comes out
identical for every player and every build, and two times in the record
book are two times down the same piste.

Every built discipline is raced on its own NINE RACE MAPS (`race-maps.ts`,
`docs/specs/RACE_MAPS.md`), all open, chosen because their course makes a
good race of it; a TRICKS run on one of the TRICK MAPS (`trick-maps.ts`);
the benchmark on its own pinned race (`benchmark-plan.ts`). A seed of one's
own is the free ride's, and a link's `?seed=` takes the pinned map off for
that visit (`pinnedFor`). Nothing is locked: there is no ladder and no
points.

**Read this skill's lessons first** — `npx ogf-skill-lessons
pinned-maps --list`. Load **`skill-reflection`** at both ends,
**`write-code`** beside this one for any code change, **`level-rating`**
whenever a map is being chosen or judged, **`mapgen-improvement`** when a
generator change is what moved a map, and **`menu-system`** for anything
about the cards themselves.

## The curation loop — adding or moving a map

```
   1. make rate COUNT=96 ARGS=--sim                    the pool, the bot's run as the time axis
   2. shortlist on the AXES, not the index            level-rating's brief
   3. make difficulty SEED=<seed> ARGS=--sim          LOOK at it
   4. build the seed COLD on its version, course and grade — a loading card
      nobody waits through is no map (the lessons)
   5. edit race-maps.ts (or trick-maps.ts): seed, version, course, name, blurb
   6. make rate RACE=<discipline>     the nine audited: digest, rating, the bot against par
      — write the digest and the day (`day`) into the row
   7. make routes                     the piste behind the box
   8. make difficulty RACE=<discipline>   the sheets, in the PR
   9. npx vitest run tests/race_maps_test.ts tests/generator_version_test.ts
```

**The day is the cheapest lever.** Every map rides the day its seed dealt
(R15, R19) unless its row lays a `sky` over it; a weather and a start hour
cost nothing that has to be re-verified, so when two maps sit too close,
try the day before the seed: a seed change re-rolls the map, the sheet, the
bot's time, the route and the name. The box bills the day it is ridden in
(`day`), and the tests hold that to the built map, so a `sky` added to a
row owes its `day` too.

**A map is named for what it is like, never for where it is** — the snow,
the light, the shape of the ask; a ski area's name is invented and generic.

## The version contract, and the digest

Every map names the generator version it was curated under and carries the
digest of the map that came out. The tests rebuild every one and compare.
When they go red there are exactly two cases, with opposite fixes:

- **You moved the map** (a new seed, a deliberate move onto a newer
  version): run steps 6 and 7 above and write the new digest down. The
  picture was meant to change.
- **The rules moved under a map that did not**: NEVER regenerate the digest.
  Add a row to `GENERATOR_VERSIONS`, keep the old behaviour on the old row
  as an optional trait read through `generatorTraits(opts.version)` at the
  one place it differs, and leave the map where it stands — or move it onto
  the new version as a curation, one map at a time. Bumping every map
  because the suite went red is the implicit re-roll the scheme exists to
  prevent, and it puts every player's record book on snow nobody rode.

And **delete a version nothing names any more**, row and trait branches
together — `tests/generator_version_test.ts` refuses a museum.

## What a change here obliges

- `make rate RACE=…` and `make difficulty RACE=…` in the PR, and
  `make routes` for any map that moved.
- A new word on the cards goes in `strings-maps.ts`; a new mark in
  `menu-glyphs.tsx`.
- Anything the player sees → a `.changes/unreleased/` fragment.

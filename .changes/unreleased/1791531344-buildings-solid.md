Every building is solid as it is drawn. A skier meets its walls where they
stand — the church's nave and tower, a porch's posts, a woodpile, a chimney,
a terrace's rail — and no speed, from a crawl to a straight run, carries him
or his tumbling body through one. Each building has a doorway where its door
is drawn, shut by the door.

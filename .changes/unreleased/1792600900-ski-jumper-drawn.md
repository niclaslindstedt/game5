The ski jumper now moves like one: sat on the bar, folded flat over his thighs down the in-run with his arms straight back, springing off the table, laid out over his skis in the V-style with his heels off them, readying the landing, coming down in a telemark (or on both feet) and rising on the out-run with his arms raised after a long jump. He jumps in a one-piece suit in your jacket's colours and a matching helmet, and never carries poles.

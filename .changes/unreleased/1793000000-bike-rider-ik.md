The downhill bike's rider is now posed from where he touches the bike. His hands stay on the grips and his feet on the pedals, and his hips sit back and low on steep ground and forward on climbs. His elbows and knees bend the way the bike asks, and his head looks down the trail. A whip, a hard brake or a foot put out in a slide now eases in instead of snapping.

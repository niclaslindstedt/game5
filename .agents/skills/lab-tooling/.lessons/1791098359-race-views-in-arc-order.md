---
title: A lab that photographs moments of one bot-skied race must shoot its views in the order the run reaches them, from a few metres off what it frames
date: 2026-10-04
scope: pwa/src/tools/audience-harness.ts
concepts: harness, framing, labs
---

The audience lab's race sheet rides ONE run forward to each view's moment (the racer's arc down the piste), so a view asked for after the run has passed its moment is shot late — the harness answers `__aud.order(views)` and the driver shoots in that order. And a bank of spectators photographed from the far edge of a 60 m piste is a strip of specks: stand the lens 9–11 m in from the bank's fence at head height (`across`), looking a few metres up the piste so the racer arrives in frame.

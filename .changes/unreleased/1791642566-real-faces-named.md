The free ride's real mountainsides are named by place: the start card's RANGE row picks where they lie (Sweden, Norway and Finland first, then the Alps and the rest), the AREA row the town or mountain the ski area is known by, and a PART row, where an area has more than one, which side of it. Each real mountainside also brings its real woods: the trees reach as high as the real tree line and thin out where the real mountainside is bare.

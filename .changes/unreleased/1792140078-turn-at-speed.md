Every pair of skis turns on a free ride. The long race pairs, the downhill and super-G skis above all, now carve as tight as the all-mountain pair instead of barely leaving the fall line. Past a crawl the edges grip harder the faster you go, so a carve bites hard at 50 km/h and still bends your line at 100 km/h and more. On touch the skis under your thumb now slide sideways inside the ring as you edge, and the ring follows only once your thumb reaches its side. Skiing hurt with injuries on also costs far less turn: even badly hurt legs still steer you.

title: Slalom
---

The race is now a real ski racing discipline, starting with SLALOM, set to the international rules on the steepest stretch of each piste: pole gates in red and blue, a turning pole and an outside pole 4–6 m apart, hairpins, verticals and delays, as many direction changes as a third of the vertical. A missed gate or a straddled pole disqualifies. The other disciplines — giant slalom, super-G, downhill, ski cross and speed ski — are named and coming.

---
title: A faceted animal's spine facets take the whole sky and read as a white stripe in the game (never on the lab sheet) — paint them a shade darker
date: 2026-10-03
scope: pwa/src/game/beast-shapes.ts
concepts: [wildlife, colour, faceting, lighting]
---

Smooth normals spread the sky's light thinly over an animal's back; a
faceted ring puts it all on the two facets either side of the spine, and in
the world's light (the hemisphere sky over snow, a low sun) a dark brown coat
came out as a pale stripe along the back — while `make birds`' studio light
showed the same animal brown. Coats are countershaded anyway, so the facets
along the spine are the coat times `DORSAL` (0.78). A pale patch the species
really has (a reindeer's neck, a roe's rump) needs toning down too, for the
same reason: judge colours in `make world ARGS=--views=herd`, not the sheet.

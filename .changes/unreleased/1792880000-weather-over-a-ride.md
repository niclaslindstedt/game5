The weather now changes over a free ride. As the hours go by on the sky's clock, the sky you start under can turn into another. High cloud can stream in and thicken to a grey lid before the snow starts. A storm can ease to showers and break up. A valley fog can sink and burn off. A clear morning can grow its afternoon cumulus. Each change comes in over an hour or two of game time. The clouds morph from one kind into the next, and the falling snow, the new snow it lays and the wind follow along. Races and trick runs keep the weather they were dealt. Add `?front=0` to a free-ride link to hold the weather still.

The downhill rider's knees and elbows stay in a rider's bend through landings, pumps and tables: his hips rise where a knee would fold shut over its pedal and his trunk lifts where an elbow would close. A whip or a table now unwinds smoothly on touchdown instead of snapping straight, the outside pedal drops through a turn only as far as the bike is laid over and only once a landing has settled, and his head no longer throws him sideways when the bike goes down flat.

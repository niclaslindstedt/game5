title: The army's soldiers come after you on skis
---

From two stars the army's helicopters now put soldiers down. A machine finds a flat, open place near you and lands on it, or, where the woods leave nowhere to land, its men come down a fast rope. The squad runs out of the doors and kneels facing out until the machine lifts, then chases you on skis: gliding down, skating and herringboning up, and stopping to fire off a knee. From four stars a soldier with a launcher comes along. A squad left behind is picked up and set down again ahead of you, and from three stars squads ride the lifts up after you. Ski into one and you knock him down. Every soldier is looking for you, and the woods now hide you from anyone on the snow. With SAFE FOR WORK on they never hit you: they run you down and arrest you, face down in the snow with your hands behind your back.

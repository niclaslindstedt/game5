---
type: Changed
title: The minimap shows the helicopter you fly
---

Flying the helicopter, the minimap now follows the machine rather than the skier sat on its skid: it is laid on the helicopter and turned by its nose, and the mark at its middle is the helicopter seen from above with its rotor turning, faster as it spools up. A helicopter on its pad or flying home is drawn the same way, pointing where its nose points.

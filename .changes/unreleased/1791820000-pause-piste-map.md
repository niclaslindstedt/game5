type: Added
title: Pick another run from the pause card's PISTE MAP
---

The pause card has a PISTE MAP press beside OPTIONS. On a free ride it opens the start card's chart and RUN row for the mountain you are on: step to another run, or tap the chart to start anywhere, and RIDE takes you there by its lift (or on the machine you picked), keeping the day and the snow. On a race it takes you to that discipline's level card to pick another of its maps.

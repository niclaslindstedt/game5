The skier's elbows stay tucked in close to his body when he works the poles. Double poling, skating and striding no longer hold the arms out like wings: the fists come in to about shoulder width and the elbows bend back past the ribs, as a skier poles. Going fast, the double pole's arms no longer stop at the hips: once the poles let go they carry on long and high behind him, the faster he goes the further, and fall back in short again at a crawl.

title: The grimbear
---

Something lives in the woods: about one free ride in three, the GRIMBEAR is out on the mountain — a bear's bulk on a man's stride. A while into the ride he waits behind a tree beside the run ahead, out of sight, and breaks out of the trees as you come, running you down however fast you are going. He takes you once — you go down, he stands over you and walks back into the woods, and you are stood up again at the top of the slope — and every time he comes out of the trees after that he only runs after you, roaring, and pulls up short. Only on a free ride; `?start=free&grimbear=1` sends him after you on a link.

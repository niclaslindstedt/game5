title: Things go wrong in the air — canopy malfunctions and engine failures
---

A free ride can now go wrong in the air, now and then. A parachute's main may open with its lines twisted (kick them out on the edge before you can steer), with a line over the top that pinches a tip off and spirals you down (cut it away and pull the reserve), or hard enough to hurt your neck. A paramotor's wing may launch with twists in its lines, and a deep collapse may leave a tip caught in them, which a sharp pump of the brakes frees. The jump plane's engine may cough and stop, leaving a glide of about nine to one, and the helicopter's turbine may quit: hold the collective up and the rotor slows into a stall, lower it at once and it autorotates down. OPTIONS ▸ THINGS GO WRONG sets how often: never, rare (the default) or often.

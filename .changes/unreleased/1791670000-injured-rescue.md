title: An air ambulance takes the injured skier off the mountain on the next run
---

With INJURIES on, the run after one that ended injured shows how he left the mountain. As the player comes near the spot, an air ambulance has landed beside it, across the slope and clear of the trees and the lifts. He lies where he fell, face up, in the player's own colours with his helmet on. Two of its crew and two patrollers walk up with a stretcher, kneel beside him, lay it alongside, log-roll him onto his side, slide it under him, lay him back onto it and strap him in. Then they take its corners, lift him, carry him to the machine at a walk with the stretcher held level, slide him in through its side door and climb in after him. The rotor turns all the while; the machine lifts off in a cloud of snow and flies down the valley. After dark its landing lamp lights the snow. The scene shows on that one run only.

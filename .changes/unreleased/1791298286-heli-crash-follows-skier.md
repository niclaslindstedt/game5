type: Changed
title: The camera rides along with the skier the helicopter's blast throws
---

When the helicopter crashes and explodes, the camera still opens on the fireball. Now, as the skier's flight nears its highest point, it swings in behind him and rides his path. It is a low tracking shot carried along at his speed, looking where he is going, so the snow and trees rush past. It widens and tilts the faster he goes, and follows him through the fall and his tumble along the snow. It never goes into the fireball.

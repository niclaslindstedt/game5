title: The army comes up the mountain in waves
---

From two stars the army sends troops up the mountain from the valley in waves, and every wave is bigger than the last the longer you survive. At five stars the slopes below you fill with soldiers. Each wave gets down out of your sight and climbs the ski area's runs and cat tracks toward you in a column: most of the men skin up on skis, and every third man slogs up on foot. Once they see you, they come after you like any other soldier: they stop to fire, and with SAFE FOR WORK on they run you down and arrest you, then close round you. Each wave also sends out more of the army's snowmobiles.

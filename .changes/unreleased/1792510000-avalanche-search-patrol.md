title: The avalanche search and the ski patrol
---

An avalanche on a free ride now buries the amateurs and the night's keen skiers in its path too, and the SKI PATROL comes out for anyone it buries: patrollers ski in, search with their transceivers, probe, dig and bring the victim out alive or dead. When someone else is buried, the player's transceiver switches itself to search (T switches it either way; BEACON on touch). The HUD's transceiver reads SIGNAL until it picks one up. Then it shows the distance and an arrow along the field line, with the beeps quickening, and under about three metres the distance alone. G pushes the probe in until it strikes, and G held then digs a hole down to him, longer the deeper he lies (PROBE / DIG on touch). A buried player sees the dark snow and his air clock while the patrol works, and is either dug out and stood up or dies when his air runs out. `make avalanche ARGS=--sheet=search,hole,patrol,buried` photographs it.

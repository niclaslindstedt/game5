A ski jump is now heard as one: the start signal as the light goes green, the knock off the bar, the skis hissing and rumbling in the in-run's tracks and rising with the speed, the thump and crack of the take-off, the wind in flight swelling with the speed and opening as the skis spread into the V, the slap of the landing and the telemark's split slap — and the stadium's crowd, a murmur while the jumper is on the bar, rising as he flies and roaring as he lands, louder past K and loudest past HS, a groan at a fall. The trick chimes and the finish horn no longer sound on a jump.

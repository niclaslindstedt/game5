Falling snow now streams past you as fast as you ski. Each flake moves with the wind and its own fall, and the game takes the camera's own speed off that, so at 100 km/h into still air every flake comes at you at 100 km/h. Each flake is drawn as the streak that motion leaves over a frame, rushing out of the point you're heading for and past the edges of the screen. You can no longer pick out one flake and follow it at racing speed, as if the snow were riding along with you. In a crosswind the snow drives sideways across the run. Riding downwind at the wind's own speed, it just falls. Watching a replay from the broadcast camera, the snow only falls and drifts, as it should seen from a camera standing still.

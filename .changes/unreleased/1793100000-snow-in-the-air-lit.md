The spray and the snow cloud are lit by the sky itself: snow thrown in the mountain's shade is blue rather than glowing white, a low sun behind it turns it into a silver or gold sheet, and after dark only the lamps and headlamps light it. The spray's grains are little clusters of crystals in a haze of fines with ragged clumps among them, the cloud is softer and torn into wisps, and a powder plume now lays a soft shadow on the slope beside it so it reads against the snow by day.

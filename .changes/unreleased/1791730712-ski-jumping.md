title: Ski jumping replaces big air on the tricks card
---

Ski jumping on the tricks card, first and lit: sit on the bar of a jumping hill under the start light, take off at the edge on the jump press (early, perfect or late), fly the polar, telemark the landing and read the distance, the judges' marks and the gate and wind points on the plate, through a trial round, round 1 of fifty and the final of thirty — on keys and on touch (a JUMP and a TELEMARK press), with `?start=skijump`, `round=1`, `gate=<n>` and `hill=normal` links.

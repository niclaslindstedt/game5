The rider card has a PARK rider as a man and a woman, modelled whole in a park kit: a tall baggy jacket to mid-thigh, loose pants stacked over the boots, a soft helmet and big goggles — his hood up over the helmet and a gaiter, hers down on her back. Its skirt, hood and stacked cuffs stream and flap in the air. The card's BODY row now starts with AUTO MAN and AUTO WOMAN, the default, which wear the kit of the run you ski: the hiker on a free ride, the racer in a race and on the ski jump, the park rider on a tricks run and in every freestyle contest. A man or a woman dressed piece by piece is still one press away on the same row.

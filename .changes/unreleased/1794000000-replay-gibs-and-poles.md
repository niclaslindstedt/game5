title: Replays of a death no longer show his body twice, and the poles stay clear of the skier
---

Watching the replay of a crash you died in no longer shows your own body, its pieces and its blood already lying where you died, so the rider in the replay no longer flies through his own remains. The wounds of the run you died on are no longer left on the rider in a replay of it either. Earlier deaths are still lying where they were. And the poles no longer run through the skier: tucked into a turn, leaning into one, or folded up in the air, the inside pole used to pass straight through his hips and back, and now it swings out past them.

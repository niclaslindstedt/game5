type: Changed
title: Off a lift you ski down to your run, following its sign
---

Getting off a lift no longer takes the controls from you: the moment you stand up off the chair the skis are yours. Every run off a chair's or a gondola's top now starts below it, and a wide groomed ramp falls from the edge of the station's pad down onto it — never a climb, never a flat to pole across, and never steeper than a green or a blue for those colours. The chair's unload ramp now slopes away ahead of you, so you slide off the chair onto the pad instead of rolling back under it. Each run's sign stands at the head of its ramp, down the pad's slope below where you got off, pointing down the ramp, so you follow the one you want straight onto its run. The piste map board at the top of every chair and gondola shows the same painted panorama as the free ride's start card, with a YOU ARE HERE mark, and the patrol hut beside it is gone. Transport lanes off a lift's top start below it too. The nursery drag's top is not covered yet: its runs get a ramp only where one fits.

title: A reserve parachute for the skydive
---

After cutting away the main, press again (Enter, or a double tap) to pull the reserve. The left hand pulls its metal handle, a spring throws the small pilot chute clear, and a free bag lets out a smaller white and red seven-cell square that opens in under three seconds. The reserve flies like the main, a little faster and steeper. It steers, flares, lets go by itself when your skis meet the snow and can be caught in a tree or on a lift, but it cannot be cut away. The HUD calls the press, it has a sound of its own, and the bot pulls it straight away after a cut-away.

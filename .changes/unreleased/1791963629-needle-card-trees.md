The woods are drawn as needle cards: every spruce, fir, pine and larch a dark core under drooping boughs of painted needle sprays, each laden with lumps of snow that lie where the bough faces the sky, the birches' twigs grown spray by spray, and the far trees thinned to a few standing cards that keep their outline — the forest drawn in fewer calls and fewer triangles, its edges smoothed where the picture is antialiased.

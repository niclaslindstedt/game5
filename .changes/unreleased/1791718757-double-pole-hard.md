type: Changed
title: Double-poling hard for speed
---

A skier who has stopped skating keeps DOUBLE-POLING instead of standing still: both poles planted together, the body folded over them and the arms working faster the faster he goes, the stroke shortening into a hard jab. He wins a lot more speed on the flat (37 km/h hands off, from 24) and down a gentle run-out (some 45 km/h), and gives the poles up for the tuck only where the hill pulls him on harder than his poles push. Down a steep face he never poles at all.

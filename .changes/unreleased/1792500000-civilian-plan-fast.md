title: A free ride loads faster and lighter: the people on foot are placed in a tenth of the time
---

A free ride loads several seconds faster: placing the people on foot round the ski area took 3–5 s on a desktop and far longer on a phone, and now takes a tenth of that, with everyone standing exactly where they did. Their close-up figures are also made only when someone first comes near, so a ride starts with about a quarter of the memory they took.

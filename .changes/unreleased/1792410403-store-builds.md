title: The phone and desktop apps no longer link to the source, and OPTIONS links the privacy policy
---

The version in the menu and the build label on the HUD name the build as plain text in the phone and desktop apps, which leave out the website's privacy and support pages. The desktop Help menu and About panel open the game's page on apps.agilator.se, which carries its privacy policy and support page, and a PRIVACY POLICY link at the foot of OPTIONS opens the same policy in every build. The website keeps its links.

**The free ride's cards fill the screen.** On a phone's browser the first touch asks for fullscreen. The world map stretches over the whole card. The free ride's piste map is the start card now, with its START and RUN rows beside it, and the season, time, weather, snow and avalanches are tucked behind one sky button in the card's head, with no hint line under them. The gear card's camera looks down at the skis or the board from close above. A generated mountain shows its number and its drop, steepest and average pitch on the world map, and a real mountain's slope count is the runs the game actually lays rather than every piece of its piste map.

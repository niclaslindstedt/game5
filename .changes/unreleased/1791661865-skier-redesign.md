type: Changed
title: The skier redesigned
---

The skier and every piece of his kit are recut against photographs of real skiers: jackets hang straight from a broader chest instead of bulging at the hips (a race shell stays fitted), collars stand up to the chin, gloves are fists with fingers, a thumb and a wrist strap, a woman's braid lies down her back, and the pants hang over the boots. The splash screen's skier is the game's own.

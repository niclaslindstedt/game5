---
type: Changed
title: Steadier landings
---

A skier now rides away the landings a mountain hands him: off a crest at speed, off a jump sprung from the slope and onto any slope a skier lands on, his legs sink deeper and softer into the landing and come back up slowly instead of springing him off the snow, his trunk stays square while the skis pivot flat under him, skis met tails first snap down flat about his boots instead of bouncing him off their tails, and a hard one rocks him in a short wobble rather than throwing him. A big drop onto the flat, a landing on his side or over his tips still puts him down and still hurts. Holding the tuck key into a jump no longer tips him forward in the air; letting it go and pressing it again in the air still leans him forward, as before.

title: An INJURIES switch, and a child's iPhone or iPad hides the injuries on its own
---

OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE has a new INJURIES switch: off, the HUD no longer draws the body plate with its bones and organs or the g meter, so a younger player can ski and crash without being shown what a fall would break. In the store app on iOS 17 and later the phone's own content restrictions are read at launch: with Communication Safety on a child's account the injuries are hidden and the switch is locked off, and with an adult's Sensitive Content Warning on they start hidden until switched back on. In a browser, on the desktop app and on Android, which offers no such signal to an app, the switch alone decides and the injuries are shown unless it is turned off.

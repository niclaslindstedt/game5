title: The army flies paramotors and balloons after you
---

From four stars the army also comes for you by air. Riflemen fly paramotors in olive wings. They circle you, keep clear of the snow, the trees, the lifts and each other, and fire at you from the harness. Army hot-air balloons in olive and khaki drift in on the wind. Soldiers in the basket fire shoulder rockets that you can watch fly in on a trail of smoke and burst in the snow. A burst near you throws you off your skis, and one near an army carrier, truck or tank knocks it out and leaves it burning. More of both come the longer you last. In a stolen helicopter you can bring a paramotor down with your blades or your downwash. Its soldier rides his reserve down and keeps chasing you on foot. You hear the paramotors buzzing round you and the balloons' burners roar. With SAFE FOR WORK on nothing they do wounds you: the rockets land well wide of you as warning fire, and the soldiers on the ground arrest you.

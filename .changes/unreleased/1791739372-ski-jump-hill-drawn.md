title: The ski jumping hill is drawn as a jumping hill
---
The ski jumping hill now looks like one. The in-run is a concrete trough with guard rails, a stair, lamp posts and two glazed ice tracks lying under the skis. A start tower stands over it with the numbered gates, the starting bar and a start light. The landing hill carries distance boards every metre, numbered every five, between board walls banded blue to K and red to HS. The P, K and HS lines and the dyed and branch lines cross the slope, and a green line marks the distance to beat. There are wind flags that stream with the wind, wind meters, a stepped judges' tower and coaches' platforms. Floodlight masts light the whole hill for an evening contest. The race-course arch, start hut and course markers no longer stand on the hill.

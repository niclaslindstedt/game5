title: Boarding a lift
---

Riding into a lift's boarding ring now skates you up the corral beside the queue, shouldering aside anyone in your way, instead of gliding you through the people and the fence. A chair or the gondola takes you behind a short fade to black and brings you back seated on the chair or in a glazed cabin with your skis in its rack; the gondola's queue walks in through the door. At a drag you wait on the track for a T-bar to come round and sit back onto it as it passes. While carried, ENTER (a double tap on touch) lets go of the lift, and holding the tuck for three seconds skips you to the top.

type: Changed
title: Every race map and trick map is re-picked on today's mountain
---

All fifty-four race maps (nine each for the slalom, giant slalom, super-G, downhill, speed skiing and ski cross) and the six trick maps now stand on the current map generator: the tall mountain over a valley floor by the sea, with the next lift's queue ahead of a rider off the gondola and smooth junctions where runs meet. Each was picked from a sweep of forty seeds in every country, skied by the bot, and chosen for a map that builds quickly; every ladder still runs from the gentlest to the hardest across the countries and skies, with one slalom raced at night. The trick maps now ride a terrain park laid down a ski area's course, the course's gates set round its kickers. Times set on the old maps stay in the record book under their own rows and no longer meet the new maps.

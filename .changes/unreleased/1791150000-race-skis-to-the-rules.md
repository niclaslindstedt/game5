type: Changed
title: The giant slalom and downhill skis are built to their discipline's competition rules
---

The CHOUGH, the giant slalom ski, is now 193 cm on a 65 mm waist with a 30 m sidecut, and the EAGLE, the downhill ski, keeps its 218 cm on a 65 mm waist with a 50 m sidecut — the least length and sidecut and the most waist a top-level men's race allows, with narrower shoulders and tails to match. Both carve a longer turn than before and take a tight bend less kindly: the Chough a little slower down a twisting piste, the Eagle readier still to run wide of a gate. The ski card's figures and the modelled skis follow.

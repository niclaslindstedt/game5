type: Changed
title: The camera sways round the trees off the piste
---

Off the piste, the trees no longer vanish just before the camera reaches them: a tree stays in the picture as the camera passes through it, boughs across the frame and all. The chase, far and high cameras now swing round the trunks ahead of time to keep a clear view of the skier, who stays in the middle of the frame. They swing back once the tree is past. In a thicket with no way round, the woods close in.

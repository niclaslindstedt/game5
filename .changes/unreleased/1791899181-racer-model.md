A modelled speed RACER on the rider card: a wine-red one-piece race suit printed with torn red shards, white upper-arm panels and bright knee bands, a white number bib, race gloves with hard knuckle shells and a speed helmet with gold goggles, made in Blender on the skier's own skeleton so every move drives him. In the air he rides through, his bib streams back and flutters in a wave down from his shoulders, the suit's slack shakes and fine ripples run down its lee side, and it creases behind the knees, at the hips and in the elbows as he folds. He is the default rider, and picks only his body and weight.

title: The skier's pole touch and head move like a skier's
---

The pole touch is timed as a skier times it: the downhill pole swung forward with the wrist through the end of a turn and touched at the edge change, carried just clear of the snow beside the boot rather than swung round wide. The head no longer snaps round on landing a hop into switch, and turning round to the other shoulder swings the head across smoothly. The animation lab reads a joint passing through straight as the smooth motion it is, and prints any stretch of a scene frame by frame with `--trace`.

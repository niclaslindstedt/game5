Ride a downhill bike in summer. BIKE is now the first stop and the default on a summer free ride's START row (and `?bike=1` on a link). You start at the head of the bike park trail nearest the spot you tap on the chart. Your own keys ride it: the tuck pedals, the skid brakes, the edge steers, the lean moves your weight, and the jump pops off a lip. The rider stands on the pedals in an attack stance with his hands on the grips. The camera follows the bike, close behind by default, with a bar-cam on TIPS. The HUD names the trail, shows how far is left and counts your falls, and a landing's verdict comes up in the news. You hear the tyres on every kind of ground, the hub ticking as you coast, the chain, the brakes and every landing and crash. A fall throws you clear while the bike slides on. Enter steps you off to walk and puts you back on. A chair lift carries the bike up with you, hung off the back of the chair.

title: The player as a ski mountaineer
---

The player now rides as a ski mountaineer modelled in Blender: an orange guide's softshell with a dropped tail, a harness with its rack, a light pack with an ice axe and a rope coil lashed on it, a climbing helmet with a headlamp, and glacier glasses. He is posed by every animation the game already had. His pack, the coil and the rope's loose tail swing on springs as he turns, lands and tucks, and his jacket's hem and the straps' tails flap harder the faster he goes. Every other skier is still dressed in code.

type: Changed
title: The menus fit a phone held sideways
---

Every card now fits a phone on its side, notch and browser bar included. BACK, and the button that moves you on (SKI, NEXT), stay pinned in each card's head. Only the lists under the head scroll: the options, the keys, the campaign's maps. The front door puts the name and the OPTIONS and GALLERY chips on one line, so all five ways onto the snow show without scrolling. On the ski card, SKI sits in the head and the pair fills the height beside its sheet. The free ride's chart gets a column of its own with every row beside it. The gallery shows the picture beside its buttons and filmstrip. A row's explanation now sits at the foot of its own card on one line.

title: Real buildings at the lifts and the races
---

The lift stations are real buildings now instead of plain boxes. A chair's foot has a timber drive house with a roller door for the chairs. Its top has an operator's house beside the way off, and the white terminal hood with its red band stands over the bullwheel. A gondola stands in a glazed hall with a platform roof over its wheel. The drags have timber huts, and the operators sit in red glazed booths. Every wall shows what it is made of (weathered boards, ribbed steel, cast concrete, standing-seam roofs, windows, glass), every roof has snow on it, and the panes light up after dark. The slalom's start house is a boarded house with referee windows behind a billboard braced on scaffold, the start hut beside every other course is a timber hut with a lit window and a stovepipe, and the finish arena's stands, leader's platform and video wall stand on real scaffold. The wind tunnels are built as galleries: their arches stand on concrete footings, snow lies along their crowns, a steel portal in the tunnel's colour frames each end, and the fan turns in a panelled drum with a flared intake, its switchgear cabinet beside it and the sign on a gantry over it.

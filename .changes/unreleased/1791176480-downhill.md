title: The downhill — a training run, then one race run down a black's whole course at well over a hundred kilometres an hour
---

A second race discipline beside the slalom. A downhill is set on the whole piste of the ski area's biggest black, from a start house under the summit to the finish arena: the crests shaved round so a jump lands on the downslope, the trees cut back, tall safety nets down both sides that catch a racer who runs wide and put him out, and wide speed gates — two poles and a red panel — every eighty metres or so on the racer's line. You ski a TRAINING run first, its times counting for nothing, then the RACE: one run against a field of thirty, the place on time, split at four intermediates, and a SPEED TRAP on the fastest straight that shows your speed against the field's. The DOWNHILL tile stands beside SLALOM on the front door and races a black off the level card; two of the campaign's blacks, First Black and Basin Wall, are downhills now, booked on the race's podium (a medal won there as a time trial still counts as the rung cleared).

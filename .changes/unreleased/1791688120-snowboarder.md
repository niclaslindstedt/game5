title: Ride a snowboard on a free ride and the park run
---

The ski card now has a snowboard at the end of its rack on a free ride and the park run: the Lynx, an all-mountain twin, with its own sheet (length, waist, sidecut, stance and both binding angles) and a dress card with no poles row. On a board you ride every lift the way snowboarders do: up a chair with your rear foot out of its binding, the board hanging off your front foot and your free foot resting on it, strapping back in once you are going again; towed side-on up a T-bar with the bar behind your front thigh; and through a gondola's station and in town with the board under your arm, racked on the cabin's door on the way up. A link's `?skis=lynx` picks the board for the visit. Races and freestyle contests keep their own pairs.

---
type: Changed
title: The skier keeps his hands forward and still when he nearly falls
---

A skier who rides out a near fall no longer throws his arms about. Off a big cliff he spots the landing with his hands forward and quiet instead of circling them, and when a hard landing, a trunk or a biting edge nearly takes him down he braces his hands forward and lets his legs take it, rocked once and brought back rather than wobbled from side to side with his arms flung up.

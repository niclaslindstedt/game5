title: The slopes empty out at night
---

A free ride after dark no longer has the daytime crowd on it. The families, ski schools and lunchtime groups have gone in, and the mountain is left to you, the snow guns and the piste machines, plus three keen skiers lapping the lit runs. Each of them is a full skier like you, on a pair of skis and in a kit of their own, wearing a headlamp, carving, falling and riding the chairlifts back up. By day the crowd is out as before.

AERIALS off the tricks card: single jumps off steep kickers — a single, a
double and a triple — on a jump declared on the trick map card's JUMP row,
twisting flips flown with taps (a lean back a flip, an edge a full twist
inside the flip it was asked in, the trick key the tuck), a steep landing
hill, and a formal score — five judges' air, form and landing, the middle
three summed and times the jump's degree of difficulty — over a
qualification, a final of twelve and a final of six. Raced on the Kestrel,
the new aerials pair (short, narrow and stiff), at the light build; the ski
card gains a SPIN bar.

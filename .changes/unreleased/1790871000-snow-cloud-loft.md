type: Changed
title: The snow cloud grows with speed
---

The cloud of powder behind a skier no longer hangs round him like a ball at a crawl: at walking pace the snow is shoved aside and falls back, and the fine rooster tail fills in as he picks up speed — a wisp at 10 km/h, a plume at 60. Landings and wipeouts still throw their full burst, and the chase camera now sees the skier clearly through the powder a carve throws beside his boots.

title: Skating leaves the tracks its skis make
---

The marks a skier leaves in the snow now follow each ski where it is drawn. Skating and poling, each ski opened into the V leaves its own short diagonal stroke and the ski swung back in for the next stride leaves none, so a skater's track is the herringbone of strokes a real skater leaves rather than two straight lines. A skid's tracks lie across the way the skis do.

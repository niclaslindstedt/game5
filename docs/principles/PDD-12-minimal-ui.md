## Rule

A card offers as few choices as the task needs. A title or label says what a thing is; no tooltip, hint line or caption restates it.

## Why

The game is played on a phone between other things; every extra row and line is read every time.

## Prevents

Cards that grow a row per feature and explanations nobody reads.

## Exceptions

The developer page, and settings whose effect cannot be named in a word.

- v1 (2026-10-17): adopted from repeated corrections on the menus.

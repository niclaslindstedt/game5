---
type: Fixed
title: Flying the paramotor no longer racks up a trick score
---

Flying under the paramotor's wing, falling or flying out of it in the skydive or the wingsuit, and riding in the balloon's basket no longer count as air: no score climbs while you fly, and touching down under the wing no longer calls a PERFECT LANDING. Jumps you make on your own skis score as before.

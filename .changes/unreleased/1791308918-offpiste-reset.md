---
type: Changed
title: A fall off the piste sends you back to where you left it
---

On a free ride, a reset off every run (after a fall in the woods or the powder, or pressing R out there) now stands you back on the run where you left it, at the spot you went off, facing down it. It used to put you on whichever run you had skied passed nearest where you lay, which could be far down the mountain. Riding a lift, the helicopter or the snowmobile clears that spot, so a reset never sends you back to where you were before you were carried.

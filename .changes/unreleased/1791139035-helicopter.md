THE HELICOPTER on the free ride. Pick the RUN row's last stop, HELICOPTER, or ride in beside the helicopter waiting on its pad in the valley — it lights up as you come near — to sit on its skid with your skis on. Then fly it yourself, anywhere on the mountain and as high as you like:

- ↑ / ↓ (or Shift / Z) work the collective, W / S / A / D the cyclic, and Q / E (or ← / →) the pedals.
- On touch, the left thumb works the collective and the pedals, and the right thumb is the cyclic stick.
- Space, or a double tap on either thumb, steps you off where it has landed or pushes you off the skid at the helicopter's speed. The HUD shows how far the snow is below, so you can see how big a drop the body plate will stand.

You fly it by hand, with nothing assisting: a mode of its own, and hard. The collective is a lever that stays where you leave it. The cyclic tilts the rotor disc and leaves it there, and the air blows it back as you speed up. Pulling collective swings the nose, so the pedals work with it. It gets translational lift at speed and ground effect near the snow, the wind pushes it about, and your weight on the skid hangs it to your side. It has its own keys (OPTIONS ▸ KEYS ▸ HELICOPTER), and on touch the right thumb becomes a cyclic stick. Land it on snow flat enough and step off, or push off the skid where it cannot land. When you jump it lurches up off you, and its pilot flies it home. Its rotor wash blows the snow up into a whiteout and shoves anyone standing near it.

Fly it into the snow, a tree or a slope too steep to land on and it goes up in a fireball and burns. You are thrown clear, and you start again on the pad.

It is modelled in Blender after the helicopters heli-ski operators fly. You hear it too: the whop of its rotor, the whine of its turbine, the buzz of its tail rotor, the roar of its wash over the snow, and the explosion.

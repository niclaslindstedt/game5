PRESET ▸ AUTO now fits the picture to your machine using costs measured for this game. Until now it used prices carried over from the sibling game. On a fast machine nothing changes, and you still get the whole picture. On a machine that draws the top picture at half the speed of the reference, AUTO now gives up the skiers' sharp shadows, part of the draw distance and some of the far woods. Before, it gave up only the sharp shadows. On slower machines it lowers the resolution sooner than it did, and on the slowest it also turns off the shadows and the tracks in the snow.

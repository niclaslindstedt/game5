title: Start a free ride at the afterski
---

The free ride's start card has a new stop at the end of its RUN row, AFTERSKI, on every mountain with an afterski lodge in the valley. Pick it and the ride begins inside the lodge with the party under way: the beers come round on their own (the jump orders another), and the machine key takes you out of the door onto your skis to ski the mountain with the buzz you drank. The chart marks the lodge's door. Restarting the ride puts you back on the snow at the top of the slope, not at the bar.

title: The jump plane is a modelled airframe
---

The jump plane is now a full model rather than the faceted stand-in. It is a single-turboprop, high-wing utility plane on wheel-skis, painted in red and white, with its jump door open. The propeller spins behind a blur disc. The elevator, rudder, ailerons and flaps move with the controls, and the door slides shut. The navigation lights, beacons and strobes flash, and after dark its landing light lights the snow ahead.

From the cockpit you look out over the cowling and the panel, and the view from the door now looks out and down past the strut to the snow below. `VITE_MODEL_PLANE=0` builds the game with the old stand-in.

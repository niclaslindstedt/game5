---
type: Changed
title: Every tree is built procedurally, chunky and faceted, with a trunk as thick as it is old
---

The woods are now built entirely in code, in a chunkier, faceted low-poly look, instead of from the Blender models. Every tree has an age, and its trunk is as thick as that age: saplings with wrist-thin stems stand beside veterans a metre across, and the trunk you see is the trunk you hit. Each tree is drawn in three levels of detail (full, middle distance, far), and OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ FOREST now sets how many variants of each kind are drawn — all ten on HIGH, five on MEDIUM, two on LOW, never fewer kinds — so a cheaper picture holds far fewer distinct tree meshes on the GPU. A wood draws a fraction of the triangles it used to.

title: Blood pours in streams out of the clothes and pools round the body
---

With INJURIES on, blood no longer sprays far in drops. It pours in unbroken streams: out of a torn wound where it is, pumped on the heartbeat, and from a part hit hard enough to split the skin, soaked into the clothes round it first and then running out of the lowest gap in them (the collar, the jacket's hem, a cuff, a trouser leg over the boot, from under the helmet). A torn organ or a closed break bleeds inside and is never seen. What comes out pools in the snow round him, spreading wide on the packed piste and on thin snow and soaking away in deep powder, and a body torn open goes on emptying onto the snow after the heart stops.

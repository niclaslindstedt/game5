title: The army's helicopter and soldiers, drawn
---

The army that hunts a wanted skier now has a look. It has a military utility helicopter: a twin-engine machine with a four-bladed rotor, painted olive with a white winter wash. It has sliding doors on both sides, a door gun and its gunner in each doorway, fast-rope bars and blinking lights. It has winter soldiers too. They wear white smocks with a snow camouflage, covered helmets, goggles, chest rigs and packs, and they carry rifles and a rocket launcher. They ski, aim standing or kneeling, fire from the door, sit in the doorway and slide down the rope. Nothing in the game sends them out yet; they arrive in later updates.

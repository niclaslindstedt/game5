title: Pick your run on a free ride
---

The free ride's start card has a RUN row: picking a GRADE brings it to the first run of that colour on the mountain, and its arrows step through the others of that colour — so asking for a black puts you on a black run even where no black course reaches the valley. Where you will start is marked on the chart by a dot in your run's colour with rings pulsing out of it, and the line under the chart gives that run's length and vertical. The free ride's HUD now shows the mountain's SEED, so any picture of it says which mountain it is.

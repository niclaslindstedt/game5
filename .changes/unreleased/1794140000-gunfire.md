type: Added
title: Gunfire for the army's shooters
---

The army that hunts a wanted skier now has guns: a rifle, a door-mounted machine gun, a heavy machine gun, a shoulder-fired rocket and a tank's gun, each with its own range, rate of fire and spread. Rounds fly with travel time and drop, the shooters lead a moving target, and machine guns fire tracers. A round can hit you, the machine you ride, the snow or a tree. Enough hits on a stolen helicopter bring it down, and a rocket can set a snowmobile or a balloon on fire. With INJURIES on, a hit is a gunshot wound that can kill you, and a rocket landing close throws you off your skis or tears you apart. With SAFE FOR WORK on, the army only fires warning shots into the snow around you and never at you. Muzzle flashes, tracers, rocket smoke and explosions are drawn, and every weapon has its own sound. The soldiers who fire these guns arrive in later updates.

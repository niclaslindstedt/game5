The snow lab now scores the spray against real footage. For each stop, carve and powder turn it measures how high, how far and how wide the drawn snow goes, compares that with the same moment on film and gives a score out of 100. It also reports which way the snow went relative to the skier's travel and the fall line. Scenes can also be shot under a low sun, an overcast sky and at night.

# Architecture

Fall Line follows the shape of its sibling repos: a headless engine that IS the game, a thin browser shell that draws it, and tooling that measures it. One direction of dependency:

```
tests/  scripts/(sim, the labs)            pwa/ (Preact + three.js shell)
        \                                   /
         `----------->  engine/  <---------'
           (framework-free TypeScript — imports nothing but itself
            and @niclaslindstedt/oss-game-framework's core/ and racing/)
```

The generic parts every sibling game shares — the engine-safe pool (the PRNG, the math, noise, heightfields, quaternions, the sun, the output sink), the racing primitives (the control tape, the record book's policy, the standings), the synth, the screenshot roll, the update watch, the input plumbing, the run clock and the lab shelf — come from [`@niclaslindstedt/oss-game-framework`](https://github.com/niclaslindstedt/oss-game-framework), a git dependency on a release tag. A bug in them is fixed there and pulled in by moving the tag. The engine may import only its `core/*` and `racing/*` (never `core/clock`); `tests/imports_test.ts` holds that line.

`tauri/` and `native/` — the desktop and store shells — stand beside these, outside the npm workspace ([platforms.md](platforms.md)); the one line of `pwa/` that knows either exists is `pwa/src/shell-host.ts`.

## `engine/` — the game, headless

A pure TypeScript module with no framework, no renderer, no DOM and no `node:` — it imports nothing outside its own tree but the framework's engine-safe `core/` and `racing/`, which is what lets the browser bundle, the headless simulator and the test runner host it unchanged. Its public surface is `engine/index.ts`, spelled `@engine` by every host.

- `createGame({ seed?, level?, mode?, heat?, rivals?, countdown?, contact?, spec?, region?, grade?, quiet? })` builds a run: the mountain the seed generates (or the one handed in — the tests and the labs stage synthetic ones), the player's skier standing on the start line's first slot, and the rules of its MODE (`MODE_RULES` in `game/defs/modes.ts`; each option still overrides its own rule). With no mode it is the field on the start line (`fieldRules`: `RACE.rivals` = 3 rivals on the other slots, one run, three seconds of lights) — the suite's, the labs' and the benchmark's run, and no mode the player picks. `mode: "slalom"` sets a SLALOM over the map (R31, `mapgen/slalom.ts`'s `setSlalom`; a seed of its own is built to a red piste unless `grade` says otherwise) and DEALS its start list instead of skiing it (`field.ts`) — `heat` carries the first run into the second, set afresh on the same stretch; `mode: "timeTrial"` is the lights and the piste with nobody else on it. A mode is a name for a bundle of `RunRules` and nothing in the engine branches on the name.
- `step(state, input)` advances exactly one fixed 120 Hz step (`TUNING.dt = 1 / TUNING.physicsHz`) and leaves that step's events on `state.events`. The app's frame loop and the headless simulator call this same function; there is no other way to advance a run.
- `placeRun(state, moment)` stands a run at a moment instead of skiing to it — a plan point, a heading, a speed, optionally a height, a climb, a pitch and a roll, and the lights still to run in front of it (`lights`) — with the skis flat and the body standing. The tests and the ride lab stage through it; the moment itself is still the engine's to emit.
- `botInput(state)` is the bot's answer for the same state (`sim/bot.ts`); `simulateRun(seed, options)` skis a whole mountain with it (`sim/simulate.ts`, [simulation.md](simulation.md)).

**Determinism is a hard invariant.** Everything random draws from the seeded stream in the state (`state.rng = createRng(seed)`, the framework's `core/prng`), never `Math.random`; `state.t` is the only clock the engine knows. The only draws a run makes are the rivals' paces and pairs at the start line, and — off a stream of the start's own seeded beside it, so the field a seed deals is untouched by it — how late each rival goes after GO (`RACE.reactBand`) and the phase his first stride lands on. A slalom draws nothing from the run's stream: its board is dealt off the map's seed on streams of the field's own (`field.ts`), and its gates off a stream of the run's own (`mapgen/slalom.ts`). A seed and a list of inputs reproduce a run to the bit, which is what the sim digests rely on and `tests/determinism_test.ts` holds.

### The step

`step.ts` runs, in order:

1. the clock (`t += dt`, `tick += 1`) and the input copied onto `state.input`;
2. THE LIGHTS: under `countdown` a `count` event as each of its seconds begins (three on the start line; a slalom's starter takes four), then `go` and the phase to `racing`;
3. THE PLAYER'S RUN (`run.ts`'s `stepRun`): on a free ride, the HELICOPTER first (`heli.ts`'s `stepHeli`: flown, flown home by its pilot, parked or burning — and while the skier sits on its skid the step is its own; `docs/helicopter.md`), the SNOWMOBILE (`sled.ts`'s `stepSled`: ridden, left where it was, or lying where it threw him — and while he stands on its boards the step is its own; `docs/snowmobile.md`), the PARAMOTOR (`para.ts`'s `paraPress` before the skier and `stepPara` after him: the release press and the summit restart, then the wing on its lines over him — the skier's own body is the pilot, the wing a second body joined to him by an inextensible line; `docs/paramotor.md`), then the LIFT that carries him, if any (`lift-ride.ts`'s `stepLift`: taken into a load zone, taken to the carrier, carried up the rope, stood off at the top — the step is the lift's while it has him); a reset asked for; else the wind tunnel he rides, if any (`wind-tunnel.ts`'s `stepTunnel`: taken in, carried, let go), on an interval start the racer HELD in the start house after GO and thrown out of it when the tuck asks (`start-push.ts`: one push and a hop off it, timed by `SkierState.launch`; no skate and no double pole on such a run), the skier (`skier.ts`'s `stepSkier` — under the lights held on the line (and held IN the gate — the step's way over the snow taken out, however steep the pitch), after the finish with the controls let go — and, on a run that lets him trick the mountain — the free ride and tricks — the strokes and the grab, `strokes.ts`), the trees, the posts (a lift's towers, the floodlight masts — `posts.ts`) and the map's edge (`collision.ts`), a slalom's flex poles knocked over and swinging back up (`gate-poles.ts`'s `stepGatePoles`: the `pole` event, a little speed for each knock), the edge stakes bent over or snapped (`edge-stakes.ts`'s `stepStakes`: the `stake` event, a little speed and a twist for each), the wipeout — or, with the skier already off, his own body — a ragdoll — on the snow and the skis let go, each on its own (`crash.ts`, `ragdoll.ts`, `lone-skis.ts`) — the damage the step's blows cost on a run with damage on (`damage.ts`), what his body took (`body.ts`: the g of every blow, the injuries it did), the run's air record; and while racing, the run clock (on an interval start only once the wand is open — the clock waits for the racer), the buzzer (`RunRules.limit`), the course (`course.ts`'s `stepCourse`: the gate owed, the finish — or, under the STRICT gates, `strict.ts`'s `stepStrict`: the feet judged through every pole gate, and a gate missed, a pole straddled, a gate skipped or a start outside the window an `out` that ends the run) and the automatic reset (lying still, bogged — which waits longer — or once a thrown skier has lain long enough) — under the strict gates every reset, asked for or automatic, is a DID NOT FINISH instead (`course.ts`'s `outRun`): nobody is stood back on a slalom course;
4. THE SCORE (`tricks.ts`'s `stepTricks`), off the player's run's events: the air, the turns, the grabs, the landing judged (a clean or a perfect one an element of its own, `landingGrade`), the combo banked or lost;
5. THE FIELD (`rivals.ts`'s `stepRivals`): every rival's own run by the same `stepRun`, skied by the bot under the player's lights — none on an interval start, whose field (`GameState.field`) was dealt at `createGame` and is never skied;
6. SKIER AGAINST SKIER (`clipSkiers`), once every skier has moved;
7. THE CROWD, on a run whose rules ask for one (`RunRules.crowd` — the free ride's): the lifts first — every queue at a lift's foot shuffled up and its front taken by the carrier passing the load line, every rider carried on his carrier, every one let go at the top skating to his run (`crowd-lift.ts`'s `stepCrowdLifts`) — then every amateur's decision as it falls due and his way down his run (`crowd.ts`'s `stepCrowd`), then the player against them (`clipCrowd`: a `bump`, an amateur knocked down, a `skier` wipeout when it is hard). An amateur down is thrown onto the player's own ragdoll and stepped on it until he gets up (`crowd-down.ts`).

### The state (`game/state.ts`)

`GameState` carries the map (`level`), the player's `skier`, the `input` last given, `progress` (the gate owed, the clock, the splits — and `out`, a `RunOut`: disqualified or did not finish under the strict gates, why — `missed`, `straddle`, `start` or `fall` — and at which gate; null while the run is in it or home), the `rules` (`RunRules`: rivals, the lights, contact, whether the course and the tricks count, the buzzer, how the field starts — `start`, `line` or `interval` — the gates' law — `gates`, `arcade` or `strict` — and the start `window`), the score (`tricks`, `TrickState`), the field (`rivals`, each a whole `GameState` of its own sharing the player's level, rules and stream), an interval start's field (`field`, `Field`: which run of the two, every racer's `FieldRun` in start order — his slot, his skis, his time or how he went out, the clock at every gate, the time carried in from the first run — and the player's own carried time and his slot in the order), a slalom's flex poles (`gatePoles`, `GamePoles`: every pole's tilt, its spin and the way it lies), the crowd (`crowd`, `CrowdState`: the free ride's amateurs and their groups, on a stream of their own), the `countdown`, the `phase` (`countdown` → `racing` → `finished`), the snow dial (`snowDepth`), the new snow the sky has laid (`fresh`), the help (`assist`), the damage switch and this step's `events`.

`SkierState` (also exported as `CraftState`) is what the renderer, the HUD and the audio read:

- the body: `x, y, z` (the CoG), `vx, vy, vz`, the orientation quaternion `q` (body → world; x right, y up, z forward), body rates `wx, wy, wz`, and `heading`, `pitch`, `roll` derived from `q` each step, with `incline` (the roll against the SNOW under him rather than the vertical — the angle between his body and the line his skis stand along, which the drawn pair reads to stay on the snow) and `balance` (the inclination the turn he is making balances, off the snow's own grip over its push — what `incline.ts` holds his roll toward);
- the readouts: `speed` (|v|, vertical included, written once at the end of the step), `way` (along the skis, signed), `tuck`, `crouch` (the body's actual crouch after its lag), `brake`, `skid` (how far across the way the skis are pivoted), `steer`, `edge` (the angle the skis stand on, signed like the steer), `lean`, `skiAngle`, `packed` (the share of the load on groomed snow);
- the skier's weight: `hipRight` (the angulation into the turn), `hipAft` (fore and aft);
- the snow: `contacts` — every station (the tip, mid and tail of each ski, each named by its `station`) with its footprint on the snow surface, how deep it is pressed (`sink`, the track's depth), its width, its leg's `compression`, its `load` and whether it is `touching` — and `skiCompression` (left, right) for the legs as drawn;
- the air: `airborne`, `airTime`, `landing` (seconds since the last one);
- the wind tunnel: `tunnel` (the one he is carried along — its place, its id, where along it and how far off its line — or null);
- the lift: `lift` (`LiftRide`: the one he rides on a free ride — which, its kind, the phase `board` / `ride`, his grip's place up the line and its speed, the carrier's swing on its hanger, the next tower and where he boarded from — or null: stood off at the top, the skis are his);
- the start: `launch` (the seconds since he threw himself out of a slalom's start house, −1 before he has — what the start clip is timed by);
- the crash: `thrown` (the ragdoll's points once he is off), `stuckFor`, `trench` (how far he has sunk when bogged), `rolledFor`; the `damage` the skis and the legs have taken; the `body` — every part's worst injury, the injuries in the order taken, the blow on the g meter and the run's hardest.

`GameEvent`: `count`, `go`, `air`, `land` (air time, impact into the slope, `harsh`, the share of way `lost`), `hit` (a trunk, closing speed, where), `bump` (a rival), `checkpoint` (a gate: index, split), `missed` (and on a slalom gate the `penalty` it cost), `tunnel` (taken into a wind tunnel or let go: its id, `phase`), `lift` (on a lift: its id and kind, `phase` `board`, `tower` — a carrier over a tower's sheaves — or `off` at the top, where the skis are his again), `lap` (the finish line), `finish` (time, place), `out` (out of the race under the strict gates: the `RunOut`), `pole` (a flex pole knocked: its gate, how hard), `reset` (the gate, and whether the engine did it), `wipeout` (how: a trunk, over the tips, a fall, an edge caught), `stuck`, `damage`, `injury` (the part, which, its AIS rank — `body.ts`), and the score's `trick` (an element won), `combo` (a combo banked, `sketchy` on a harsh landing) and `bail` (one lost).

### By module

- **`game/step.ts`** — `createGame`, `rulesFor`, `step`. **`game/run.ts`** — one skier's step, the player's and every rival's alike. **`game/rivals.ts`** — the start line, standing and stepping the field, skier against skier, the standings (`raceProgress`, `racePlace`, `fieldOrder`, over the framework's `racing/standings`). **`game/defs/modes.ts`** — the modes and their rules (`MODE_RULES`; `fieldRules` and `RACE`, the field on the start line a run naming no mode is dealt), the SLALOM's (`slalomRules`, `SLALOM`: a start list of 29 beside the player, the starter's four seconds, the ten-second start window, two runs, the top 30 into the second, the slalom ski for the field; an interval start, the strict gates), the disciplines the game names (`DISCIPLINES`: the slalom, the giant slalom, the super-G, the downhill, the ski cross and the speed run, every one built — `giantSlalomRules` and `GIANT_SLALOM` beside the slalom's: two runs, the top 30 into the second, the giant slalom ski for the field), the FREE RIDE's (`freeRules`: no field, no lights, `course` off — nothing owed, and a reset stands the skier on the nearest point of the nearest run he has skied) and the snow dial (`SNOW_DIAL`: `GameState.snowDepth`, the powder's sink as a multiple of the ordinary snow's, read by `snow.ts` and never drawing from the stream). The TRICKS run's (`tricksRules`: no field, `course` off, the strokes read, a buzzer of `TRICKS_RUN.limit`). A free ride can also be stood anywhere on the mountain (`course.ts`'s `freeSpawn`) and on another day (`mapgen/sun.ts`'s `withDay`, a copy of the level with only its sun moved — at an hour or at a named time of day, `hourOfTime`, whose NIGHT is past the daylight on purpose).
- **`game/skier.ts`** — one step of the rigid body: every force summed, then one semi-implicit integration ([riding.md](riding.md)) — the edge the steer asks for and the carve's curvature off the sidecut, the skid the brake pivots the skis into, the tuck's drag area and the crouch it lowers the body by, the hips' angulation and the fore-aft weight. **`game/suspension.ts`** — THE LEGS: the six stations' layout and the body's points, off the spec. **`game/snow.ts`** — the sink, the resistance and the grip an edge holds. **`game/poles.ts`** — the push at a crawl. **`game/flight.ts`** — the skier's levers in the air, and what a landing costs the legs. **`game/chassis.ts`** — the body's points (the hips, the shoulders, the helmet, the tips and tails) meeting the snow, as impulses. **`game/collision.ts`** — the trunks and the posts and the map's edge. **`game/upright-grid.ts`** — the hash of what stands up out of the snow. **`game/posts.ts`** — the posts: every lift's columns and every floodlight mast (**`game/piste-masts.ts`**, where the masts stand), one solid list with the trunks and the cabins' walls (`solidsOf`). **`game/cabins.ts`** — the log buildings here and there beside the runs and the transport lanes (`cabinsOf`: a station every `CABIN_LAYOUT.every` metres down every run, a hash of the map's seed deciding a group there — a cabin, its woodshed, now and then a second cabin — turned from facing its run toward the fall line, its back dug into the slope and never more than a metre of stone showing (`CABIN_LAYOUT.plinth`), clear of every run's snow, the lifts, the pads, the courses, the gates and every crown; the kinds and the layout in `defs/cabins.ts`), a pure function of the map that draws nothing from the stream and moves no digest, and their walls as posts a skier meets (`cabinWalls`). **`game/edge-stakes.ts`** — the marker stakes down both edges of every run: where each stands, and each bent over or snapped by a skier, a run's own. **`game/crash.ts`** — the wipeout: what throws the skier (a trunk, a landing over the tips, a fall at speed, an edge caught — the high-side), his body thrown, when the reset stands him up. **`game/lone-skis.ts`** — the skis let go: each its own body once its binding releases, wrenched apart, sliding on down the slope rather than bouncing, never sinking. **`game/trench.ts`** — the hole a stopped skier sinks into in deep powder, and poling and rocking out of it. **`game/damage.ts`** — what a blow dulls or hurts when damage is on, and the shares a hurt skier skis on. **`game/body.ts`** — what his body takes: every blow a stop over the body's give and the snow's, in g, the injuries off a risk curve per part (`defs/anatomy.ts`, the twenty parts and their ladders on the Abbreviated Injury Scale), the injury severity score, the g meter's blow — a readout the physics never reads. **`game/strokes.ts`** — the strokes on a free ride and a tricks run (the pump, a whole loop a tap; the twirl, half a turn a tap) and the skier's grabs. **`game/switch.ts`** — riding switch: the skis backward down the hill, the steer read the way he is going, the tails held to his line, and the tail dug into loose snow unless it is turned up. **`game/tricks.ts`** — the score: what the air, the turns and the grabs are worth, the combo and the landing it is judged on ([riding.md](riding.md)).
- **`game/course.ts`** — the gates, the finish, the arrow (`bearingToNext`), a slalom gate skied past charged on the clock (`course.missPenalty`, `Progress.penalty`), the line through a course's slalom gates (`gateLineAt`, which the bot skis), the reset (`resetPose`, `standSkier` — on a free ride onto the nearest run he has skied). **`game/skied.ts`** — the runs a free ride has skied (`Progress.skied`, noted by `noteSkied` off `runUnder` and seeded with the run it is stood up on), where its reset stands the skier (`skiedResetPoint`, or off every run `leftRunPoint`: back where he left the runs, `Progress.lastOnRun`) and the top of the slope its restart stands him at (`lastPiste`, `pisteHead`). **`game/wind-tunnel.ts`** — the wind tunnels (R30): the ride (`stepTunnel`, before the skier is stepped — taken in, carried, let go, each with its `tunnel` event) and the wind (`tunnelWind`, `tunnelBlow`, summed with every other force in `skier.ts`: the air moving at the tunnel's speed or his, the blowers' thrust — full to its speed, a fixed power past it, with no ceiling — and the centring), `TUNING.tunnel`. **`game/lift-line.ts`** — a lift as a plan (R26): its towers on the ground, its rope (`planLift`, `ropeAt`, `liftPlans` once a map), its speed and each kind's load zone (`LIFT_LOOK`), and where carrier `k` is at the engine's clock (`carrierAt` — the lifts always run, a pure function of the clock, never stepped). **`game/lift-ride.ts`** — the lift ridden on a free ride (`RunRules.lifts`): boarded in its load zone (a chair's load line, a gondola station's door, a drag's track), carried — a chair's hanger swinging as a pendulum off a grip slowed into the terminal and kicked over each tower — stood off at the top (a chair onto its unload ramp, a gondola out of its station, a drag let go), and the free ride begun ON one when no spot was picked (`arriveByLift`: the last seconds of the lift, of any kind, serving the run picked — `freeRunOf` / `pickFreeRun` over `freeRuns`, the run named, else the first of the colour asked, else the course's first; `arrivalOf`) — and let go at the top: nothing leads a rider off a top, every run off it starting under it with a ramp down to it (`runsOffTop`), `TUNING.lift`. **`game/limits.ts`** — what a skier CAN do, stated once for the physics and the bot: the terminal speed on a grade, the edge at a speed, the carve's curvature, the grip, the harsh speed. **`game/footprint.ts`** — a pair's footprint on the snow. **`game/place.ts`** — `placeRun`. **`game/clock.ts`** — where the sun and the moon stand over a run (at the map's hour, still for the whole run). **`game/snowfall.ts`** — the fall at a moment (`snowAt`: the squalls, the view it leaves) and the new snow it lays (`GameState.fresh`, stepped by `step`; the physics reads it through `snow.ts`'s `packedUnder` / `depthUnder`). **`game/jury.ts`** — the weather a race is run in (`juryDay`): the map's wind eased so the strongest gust at the course's start is inside its discipline's `JURY` row and a speed race's storm eased to a steady fall, applied by `createGame` to every run whose rules carry a `jury` (`startGustOf` reads the gust). **`game/heli.ts`** — the free ride's helicopter (`RunRules.heli`, `GameState.heli`): boarded on its pad (`heli-pad.ts`'s `helipadOf`), flown by hand on four controls over momentum theory's thrust along the rotor disc (`heli-rotor.ts`'s `thrustMost`), landed and stepped off or pushed off with the jump, flown home on the same controls by its pilot (`heli-pilot.ts`), crashed into the mountain and stood up again (`startAgain`); its rotor's wash as air (`heli-wash.ts`'s `washAt`, which `air.ts` drags the skier against); the numbers and the model's geometry in `defs/heli.ts` (`docs/helicopter.md`).
- **The slalom (R31)** — **`game/field.ts`** — an interval start's field: the start list dealt off the map's seed (every racer a skill and a grit, a time about the course's par, a share of DSQs and DNFs at gates dealt down the course), the first run's order (the best seeds drawn among themselves, then the rest, the player last), the second's (the first run's top `SLALOM.qualify` in reverse, the leader last; `Heat` carries the first run in), the bib (`startNumbers`), the standings on combined time (`fieldPlace`, `fieldOrderOf` — which `rivals.ts`'s `racePlace` and `fieldOrder` answer with on such a run). **`game/par.ts`** — the course's PAR (`slalomPar`): the clock a good racer takes down the racing line and at each gate, worked out off the pitch and what a pair can do on it rather than skied. **`game/race-line.ts`** — the line a racer takes round the poles (`raceLineAt`): the setter's line rounded out on a steep pitch, which the bot skis and the par is reckoned on. **`game/strict.ts`** — the strict gates (`stepStrict`): both feet (the skis' mid stations) through every pole gate's line, a miss, a straddle or a skipped gate disqualifying, the start through the wand within `RunRules.window`. **`game/gate-poles.ts`** — the flex poles (`polePlan`, `freshGatePoles`, `stepGatePoles`): two hinged springs a gate, knocked by the line from his feet to his shoulders, a small speed loss, `TUNING.flex`. **`game/start-push.ts`** — the start push out of the house (`heldInHouse`, `stepStartPush`, `TUNING.start`).
- **The downhill (R32)** — set over a built map by **`mapgen/downhill.ts`** (`setDownhill`, `downhillCourseOf` — the resort's course with the most vertical — its racing line a minimum-curvature relaxation of the course's middle inside its corridor, read by `mapgen/speed-course.ts`'s `speedLineAt` with its signed bend), over the course preparation the slalom shares, **`mapgen/course-prep.ts`** (`prepareCourse`: the start drop, the combed and groomed stretch, the trees cleared, and on a downhill the crests shaved so a jump lands on the downslope); `mapgen/race-course.ts`'s `raceCourseOf` is the one question "which race is set on this map". Its gates are SPEED GATES (`Checkpoint.panels`), held by the same `strict.ts`. **`game/field.ts`** deals its field too (`DOWNHILL_FIELD`: a tight spread, a speed through the trap a racer, and a TRAINING run's slower times, `Field.training`), about `par.ts`'s `downhillPar` (a forward pass of the speed down the racing line, capped by what a pair can hold round each bend and stood up where a racer would). **`game/speed-trap.ts`** — the speed through the trap (`stepTrap`, `Progress.trap`, the `trap` event); **`game/nets.ts`** — the A-nets either side that catch a racer (`stepNets`, `DOWNHILL_NETS`, a DNF `net` and the `net` event): a hard drive is a wipeout (`net`), and the body and the skis thrown into the mesh are caught there (`catchInNets`: the give, the drag, the pocket, a ski end hooked — `LoneSki.hooked`), the drawn net bulging round them (the app's `net-bulge.ts`). The bot follows the line with **`sim/downhill-steer.ts`** (a feed-forward of its bend plus a correction onto it).
- **The super-G (R33)** — set over a built map by **`mapgen/super-g.ts`** (`setSuperG`; `superGCourseOf` — the downhill's hill — and `superGStart`, the start lowered until the drop to the finish is in the band), thin over the TURNING COURSE it shares with the giant slalom, **`mapgen/turn-course.ts`** (`mostVerticalCourseOf`, `loweredStart`, `turnCourse`: the start house, the same preparation with its crests shaved for its speed, the line swung half a cosine from apex to apex round the gates, the nets — a discipline's `TurnRule` row and its salt its only differences); what it shares with the downhill is **`mapgen/speed-course.ts`** (`speedCourseOf` — the one question "which speed course is on this map", asked by the nets, the trap, the field, the par and the bot — `racingLine`, `speedLineAt`, `trapArc`, `gateArcs`). Its gates TURN the racer: the least-bending line swung to each gate's side in turn, each gate two panelled pairs (`Checkpoint.panels` with a `turn`) red and blue, its turning pole inside the line's apex. One run, no training; the field is `field.ts`'s `SUPER_G_FIELD` about `par.ts`'s `superGPar`; the bot is `downhill-steer.ts` read closer (`SUPER_G_STEER`). Its nine pinned maps are the app's `race-maps.ts`, picked on the race card's level card.
- **The giant slalom (R36)** — the same turning course with a technical race's numbers, set by **`mapgen/giant-slalom.ts`** (`setGiantSlalom(level, run)`; `giantSlalomCourseOf` — the super-G's hill — and `giantSlalomStart`, the start lowered to a 400 m drop): gates every 18–42 m, a 2.5–4.5 m swing never tighter than a 22 m bend, nets, no speed trap — and, its row alone, the line held 4 m off any lift tower standing on the piste (`TurnRule.towers`). TWO RUNS on the one hill, the second's gates and swing dealt afresh off a salt of the run's own (`Level.giantSlalom`, a `GiantSlalomCourse` with its `run` and `turns`), the first run's best 30 into it in reverse, on combined time as the slalom's. ## `pwa/` — the browser shell

A Preact app over a three.js renderer, built by Vite into `pwa/dist/` with a hand-rolled service worker (`pwa/pwa-plugin.ts`; the framework's update watch, read through `pwa/src/lib/pwa-update.ts`). It imports the engine as `@engine` and nothing deeper; it READS `GameState` and never writes one, and it never steps physics except through `step()`. Preact is spelled `react` (`pwa/tsconfig.json`'s `paths` alias it to `preact/compat`).

### The shell (`App.tsx`, `game/shell.ts`)

Seven SURFACES over one canvas and ONE engine state: `splash` (the attract card), `menu` (the front door), `loading` (a run being stood up), `pause` (the run held), `run` (the player on his skis, the HUD over the top, the finish plate over that once the finish line is crossed), `replay` (a run being WATCHED, stepped off its tape — `watching`) and `bench` (DEVELOPER ▸ BENCHMARK timing its pinned race: the benchmark's pump steps and draws it, so the app's own loop does neither — `appDraws`). `shell.ts` is DOM-free and says what each one means — who skis the state (`botInput` under a card, the input manager under a run: `playerRides`), whether the engine steps at all (`simulates` — false under the pause card and the bench), whether the HUD is drawn (`hudOver`), whether the run's events make a sound (`soundsLive`) and which camera a surface is seen through (`cameraFor` — the slow orbit under a card). `App.tsx` decides only WHEN one surface gives way to the next. **The snow never stops behind a card except the pause card**: the front door stands over a live run the bot is skiing, and leaving a run for it hands the same skier back to the bot rather than tearing anything down.

- **The clock** — the framework's `loop/run-clock` (§37), driven from `App.tsx`: `requestAnimationFrame`'s time into whole fixed steps of `TUNING.dt`, the frame delta clamped and the excess dropped, a hidden tab pausing the run, and the leftover fraction handed to the renderer as `alpha` for interpolation only. Each step samples the input once (`input.ts`) and calls `step`.
- **Standing a run up** — `game/run-loader.ts` sequences a load into phases, each spending a share of a frame (DOM-free); `game/app-load.ts` is what the phases ARE (generate the mountain, stand the field, build the terrain, the forest and the gates, compile every shader) as a factory over `App.tsx`'s closures. The renderer is FETCHED behind the attract card (`import("./game/renderer.ts")`), so three.js is not on the first-render path.
- **The URL** — `game/url-params.ts` states every parameter the app reads (`seed`, `region`, `start` — `slalom` (or `race`, or `1`), `downhill` or `free` — `mode` — `trial`, `downhill` or `tricks` — `run` — a slalom link's second run, a downhill link's race — `t`, `pose`, `hold` — a ride held at a speed in a move, `hold-input.ts` — `shot`, `paused`, `camera`, `skis`, `menu` — including `gallery` and `dev` — `bench`, `splash`, `update`); [configuration.md](configuration.md) is the table.
- **What is remembered** — `game/settings.ts`: the camera rung, the skis picked, the outfit worn, the sound switch, the HUD switch (H) and every OPTIONS row, merged field by field (`mergeSettings`). The ski card (`menu-skis.tsx` over `ski-picker.tsx`, its turntable `ski-turntable.ts` fetched as its own chunk, its sheet `ski-stats.ts`, and its CUSTOMIZE SKIER press to the DRESS card, `menu-dress.tsx`, whose rows step `outfit.ts`'s catalog), OPTIONS (`menu-options.tsx`) and its KEYS page (`menu-keys.tsx`) are the `menu` surface's pages, the last two built from `menu-knobs.tsx`'s rows. So is the FREE RIDE's start card (`menu-start.tsx`): its answers are `free-ride.ts`'s (DOM-free — what is stored, and `freeGameOptions`, the one place they become a run), and its chart is `seed-preview.tsx` over `seed-maps.ts` and `seed-preview-worker.ts` — the worker generates the mountain off the main thread and hands the map back with its chart, `seed-maps.ts` keeps it so a free ride on that seed is stood up on it rather than generated again on the loading card (`portableLevel` / `boundLevel`), builds the next of the free ride's fixed mountains (`free-ride.ts`'s `FREE_SEEDS`) in the background once the one on the card is done, and keeps every chart between visits in IndexedDB (`seed-store.ts`, never load-bearing); the worker and bakes its ground with the minimap's own `bakeMinimap`, with `seed-chart.ts` (DOM-free) cutting the piste, the start line and every kicker for the summit-up PLAN and mapping a tap back to the snow, and `panorama.ts` (DOM-free) painting the PANORAMA — the mountain from out over the valley, an orthographic view marched column by column with a running horizon — and cutting the ski area's runs, lifts and marks against its depth, and its pick grid turning a tap back into the snow.
- **What the picture costs** — `game/settings-video.ts` (DOM-free): eight ladders and the presets over them; `renderer.setVideo` applies a picture live (the ground and its trail maps are REBUILT for a TERRAIN or TRAILS row; ANTIALIAS is read only when the canvas is made). `game/video-probe.ts` times a first visit drawing MEDIUM and `picture-fit.ts` fits an untouched picture to sixty frames a second off `PICTURE_PRICES`.
- **The trick maps** — `game/trick-maps.ts` (DOM-free): the six maps a TRICKS run skis, each a seed with its terrain park on a generator version and a digest (`tests/trick_maps_test.ts` holds both), skied on a date, an hour and a sky of its own; the trick map card (`menu-tricks.tsx`, routed with the campaign and level cards by `menu-pinned.tsx`) picks one, `Settings.trickMap` remembers it, and `pinned-run.ts`'s `tricks` stands the run up behind the loading card. The render stack itself arrives through `use-render-kit.ts`, the one dynamic import of `renderer.ts`.
- **The developer page** — let out by holding the front door's title for seven seconds (`menu-hold.ts`); `menu-dev.tsx` is the page, `dev-tools.ts` drives its instruments every frame (the frame rate, the renderer's own `FrameCost`, the physics' stations, `trail-overlay.ts`'s maps in the corner, the engine log, `free-fly.ts`'s camera), `debug-hud.tsx` draws them over the picture with the REPRO line (`debug-readout.ts`, DOM-free) at the foot, and `dev-app.tsx` is the whole of it `App.tsx` sees. UNLOCKS sets the campaign's board by hand (`campaign-unlocks.ts`).
- **The benchmark** — `benchmark-plan.ts` pins the race (a seed on the current generator, the whole field, chase, a fair late morning, 1800 frames at a sixtieth); `bench-run.ts` stands it up behind the loading card with its lights counted out there as the warm-up; `benchmark.ts` pumps its frames through a MessageChannel with a one-pixel fence on each, billing every frame its whole period; `benchmark-index.ts` scores it (100 is real time), `benchmark-report.ts` writes the report (`FrameCost` is stated there so the suite can read it without three.js), `scene-tally.ts` walks the scene once at the end, and `benchmark-history.ts` keeps the last twenty runs whole.
- **One button, wherever pressed** — `game/run-actions.ts`: a key, a HUD thumb or a desktop menu-bar row (`shell-host.ts`'s `sh-shell-command`) all land on one handler.
- **The shutter and the gallery** — `game/shot-request.ts` takes a picture in two halves: at the PRESS it reads the caption (`run-news.ts`'s `shotLabel`), rasterizes the HUD (the framework's `shots/shot-hud`: the live chrome serialized into an SVG `<foreignObject>` with the page's own stylesheet, cards left out) and claims the clipboard; at the FRAME, right after `renderer.draw` in the same task, it lifts the drawing buffer (`screenshots.ts`'s `grabFrame` — the context keeps no back buffer), then stamps the mark and encodes the PNG. What is DECIDED about a picture — its size, its name, where the stamp goes, the HUD layer as a document — is the framework's `shots/shot-plan` (DOM-free). The roll is its `shots/shot-roll` (the policy, storage-free) over `shots/shot-store` (IndexedDB, in memory where there is none); `menu-gallery.tsx` is the viewer, with thumbnails shrunk off the main thread (`shots/shot-thumbs`) and the three ways out in `shots/share-image`.

### The picture (`game/renderer.ts`)

The one thing `renderer-api.ts` promises the shell, built from modules that each own a part of the picture. Everything that depends on the map is built in `load`; `draw` only moves things. Every skier is drawn `alpha` of a step on from the step before (`interp.ts` reconstructs the previous pose the engine does not keep), and so is a machine he rides (`sled-view.ts`, `heli-view.ts`), by the same track, so the two never part between frames (`make judder`).

- **The ground** — `terrain.ts`: a camera-centred CLIPMAP of nested grids (a quarter metre a vertex at the lens, doubling per level, eight levels past the ridges), nothing baked into the mesh: the vertex shader reads the generator's heightfield out of a float texture and morphs each level's edge onto the next. A ground map (gradient, packed, wooded) and the piste's direction ride beside it.
- **The snow** — `snow-glsl.ts`, grafted into a `MeshStandardMaterial`: the slope read per pixel from the gradient texture, wrap lighting with a blue terminator, world-space GLITTER, the groomed piste greyer and combed with corduroy, a fine grain of patches a hand to a couple of metres across close in (the texture the eye reads pace off, faded before it aliases), the rock on the steep faces above the tree line, the forest tint past the far band, and snow painted brighter than white (`GLARE`) so a low sun does not arrive grey.
- **The tracks** — `trail-stamp.ts` (three-free) turns every skier's `SkierState.contacts` into capsules from the last touch to this one, at the physics' sink or the powder's own furrow, whichever is deeper, cut narrower and deeper the more edge the ski stands on; `trail-map.ts` keeps them on the GPU in world space — a COARSE map over the whole mountain for the life of the run and a FINE window round the player — with MAX blending, and the terrain lowers the snow by it and shades the trough. A thrown skier's slide gouges a wider furrow of its own (`bodyStampOf`).
- **The woods** — `forest.ts`: every `Level.trees` trunk drawn as the kind it is (`TreeDef.kind`: twenty kinds, conifer and bare broadleaf) in one of that kind's ten VARIANTS (`tree-variants.ts`, three-free: the numbers and the silhouette `crownAt`), every one BUILT PROCEDURALLY by `tree-shapes.ts` over `tree-mesh.ts` — chunky, faceted, low-poly — at three levels of detail (full, mid, far) and painted per map in the region's colours; its trunk is drawn to the tree's own GIRTH, the engine's `TreeDef.radius`, which grows with its AGE (R14): every trunk vertex carries a stem tag and every instance its girth, and the vertex shader (`tree-mesh.ts`'s `graftGirth`) widens the ring about its axis, so a sapling and a veteran of one height stand side by side at no cost in meshes; each trunk is one solid, a ring at every joint shared by the spans either side. Every tree LEANS off plumb about its foot (`tree-tilt.ts`, three-free: a half-normal off a hash of its place — a median under three degrees, a tree or two in a hundred past fifteen, the rare one near thirty, nine in ten on a slope leaning down it), presentation only: the trunk a skier meets is the engine's upright cylinder. One instanced mesh a shape the map grows: FOREST's rung is how many VARIANTS of each kind are drawn (ten, five or two — never fewer kinds), each at the full and the mid cut, and the far band and the casters draw one shape a kind; in three bands of distance (the full tree, the mid cut of the same variant, then a sketch), binned into 64 m cells and frustum-tested per frame; a tree crossing a band's edge is never swapped in a frame but DISSOLVES into its next cut over 0.4 s, both cuts drawn under complementary screen-door dithers (`tree-bands.ts`, three-free: the hand-over, its hysteresis, and a tree first seen taking its cut at once), so a lens at rest leaves every tree whole; what CASTS is a separate, shadow-only set — every tree whose shadow can land in the key light's circle, in front of the lens or behind it (`shadow-box.ts`), so no shadow is switched on by skiing nearer its tree. Nothing grows above the tree line (R14, an altitude the region sets) and the krummholz band under it is stunted.
- **The wildlife and the marks, built** — every bird (`bird-shapes.ts`), animal (`beast-shapes.ts`) and mark of the course (`mark-shapes.ts`) is built procedurally on the trees' bench (`tree-mesh.ts`'s `Shape`: keyed lofts, flat-coloured faces lit half by their own normal and half by the part's mass, and MARKS on the vertices the shader moves — a wing's flag, a leg's phase and hip, the head, an antler's root and the age its tine comes at), at a near and a far cut. A boom is hung on second-order springs (`camera-spring.ts` — a natural frequency, a damping ratio and a response per reading, which place its two poles): its yaw, its height (told the descent the fall line predicts, so a steady schuss is tracked without lag and only the rollers are smoothed), the fall line it leans to and the pitch of its look. It reads the fall line ahead of the skier, swings the arm up the slope behind him by a share of it (`incline`) and COMPOSES him at one place in the frame (`place`, a share of the half-fov under the axis) on every pitch — so the look tips down an ordinary piste by about half its pitch, so the drop under the skis reads, and further on the steepest faces (`inclineSteep`), so the piste ahead still shows over his head, never all of it, and the horizon stays near the top of the frame; a guard (`frame`) tips the look after a drop no spring can follow. On a slalom `camera-start.ts` (three-free) takes the lens for the START as television shows it: OVERHEAD over the door for the first two seconds of the starter's word, then a move (1.4 s, eased, no cut) down to BEHIND the racer inside the house looking out of the door down the course; on the wand it follows him out (0.7 s) and flies onto the player's rung (1.4 s) — a pure function of the clock and where he is, so a restart, a replay and a pause frame alike.

### What is read and pressed over a run

`hud.tsx` draws a DOM-free snapshot (`snapshot.ts`, taken ~12 times a second): the run clock, the position, the gates taken of the total and the split top left, the air clock top centre, the lights and GO in the middle (on a race the RUN — 1 / 2 on a slalom, TRAINING or RACE on a downhill — where the position was, a downhill's SPEED TRAP chip for a few seconds past the trap, the starter's READY and GO small at the top centre and out of the start shot, and at the course's timing points — a slalom's two, a downhill's four — the split and the gap to the LEADER there, green ahead and red behind — the snapshot's `raceOf` over `slalom-board.ts`'s `timingGates` and `timingSplit`), a missed-gate arrow, the EDGE bar over the speed bottom left (`hud-dial.tsx`: how far over the skis stand, with the skid's word on it while the brake is on), the WIND METER to the right of the speed (`hud-wind.tsx` over the snapshot's `windOf`: the air where he is and the wind he feels — the engine's `airAt` and `airflowAt`, that air less his velocity — each as an arrow on a heading-up dial, the felt one in km/h), the news column bottom right (`run-news.ts`, pure — a wipeout and how he fell, a bog and how to pole out, a dulled edge are news too), BOGGED where the missed arrow stands while he is sunk in, the DAMAGE instrument beside the speed on a run with damage on (`hud-damage.tsx`: the two edges and the legs), THE BODY at the left edge (`hud-body.tsx` over the DOM-free `body-tile.ts`: an anatomy plate from the front, made from a whole 3D body (the generated `body-model.ts`, `make hud-body`: one man's CT, the skin's silhouette the outline cut into the twenty parts, the trunk's bones cut along the spine so the front shows the front's, a back view beside it off by default; `body-figure.ts`), each part's flesh painted by its worst injury that is not a fracture, every bone as it lies, lit and shaded, cracked by a fissure or broken through with its fragments displaced (`fractureOf`); the word for the whole of him off the injury severity score, only what the bones cannot show in plain words — `strings-body.ts` — and the run's hardest blow), THE G METER over the skier the moment a blow lands and someone goes down on it (`hud-gforce.tsx`, shaking by how hard it was), the combo line over the tips on a tricks run (`hud-combo.tsx` over `trick-tile.ts`), and top right, over the presses, the MINIMAP (`minimap.tsx`) — pressed, it pauses: the map turned heading-up about the skier, its window opening with speed, every run of the resort in its colour and every lift under the raced piste, every gate with the owed one marked and a chevron on the rim when it is off the plate, the finish, and the field in its slot colours (`skier-colours.ts`). The ground under it is painted once per map — hillshade, contours, rock on the faces too steep to hold snow, the packed piste, the woods as a green mass and every tree in it — by `minimap-bake.ts` in `minimap-worker.ts` (on the main thread where a worker cannot run), started as the map is put into the renderer; `minimap-view.ts` is the DOM-free pose and marks, and `minimap.tsx` paints the world on a canvas, gliding between snapshots on the animation frame. `hud-actions.tsx` is the presses under it (reset and camera, with a pause disc heading the row only while the readouts are off and the map with them), fired — like the map — from `pointerup` (the framework's `input/hud-press`) because a skiing phone's primary finger is always on a control. `hud-result.tsx` is the finish plate — on a slalom which run it was, on the second both runs and the total, the gap to the leader, a downhill's speed trap (`downhill-run.ts`: `trapOf`, `isTraining`, `trainingOf`), the SECOND RUN press — over a downhill's training, RACE — or why there is none (`slalom-heat.ts`, DOM-free: `secondRunOf`, `heatAfter` — the first run handed to `createGame` as its heat — and `heatOf`, which a restart and a replay hand back so a second run stands up again as itself), and under it the BOARD (`hud-board.tsx` over `slalom-board.ts`'s `boardOf`: every racer by his bib in the engine's order, his times and his gap, the racers out of it billed DSQ or DNF and where, the player's row lit and scrolled to); a run that went OUT has a plate of its own, DISQUALIFIED or DID NOT FINISH, why, and TRY AGAIN alone. The slalom's words are `strings-slalom.ts` and the downhill's `strings-downhill.ts`, spread into `strings.ts`. THE RECORD BOOK AND THE GHOST: `records.ts` (storage-free above its storage line) is the book — one row per seed, skis, mode and laps (and a slalom's second run a row of its own, `runKey`), with the clock at every gate the snapshot's `gap` is read against; `ghost.ts` is the tape — `snapInput` puts every axis on the tape's grid where the input is made (`input-model.ts`) and again where `App.tsx` hands the engine any input, and a `ControlTape` is five RLE'd, base64'd streams; `ghost-run.ts` is the rig `App.tsx` arms on every player's run before its first step, which files the run at the finish, keeps the time trial's record run as a tape, and steps the ghost — one more `GameState` over the same `Level`, on the recording's own skis and help — a step of tape per step of the player's. The renderer draws it through `ghost-model.ts`, see-through, casting no shadow and stamping no track. THE CAMPAIGN: `campaign-levels.ts` is the ladder (four shelves, each one SKI AREA — a seed on generator version 3 in its region — and six of its courses, every level pinned to its version and the digest of its map — `engine/mapgen/versions.ts`, `digest.ts`), `campaign.ts` the policy (storage-free above its line: the map built, `pinnedFor` — which map a SLALOM, a DOWNHILL or a TIME TRIAL skis — the points, the locks, the table, the board), `campaign-run.ts` the rig `App.tsx` steps beside a run on a rung, which books it at the player's finish and writes the finish plate's lines, and `pinned-run.ts` the factory that stands a pinned run up through the loader and again on a restart, and a slalom's second run off its first (`secondRunOff` for a `?run=2` link; the rung is booked at the first run's flag). The cards are `menu-campaign.tsx` and `menu-levels.tsx`, with each map's piste drawn behind its box from `campaign-routes.ts` (generated by `make routes`). How hard a map is — `engine/rating/` — is dev-time only: the labs read it and the game does not. Input: `settings-input.ts` (which key is which action), `input-model.ts` (the ramps, the lever, the edge thumb and the one sign flip between screen and engine — DOM-free), `input.ts` (the listeners), `hud-touch.tsx` + the framework's `input/thumb-guard` (the two thumb zones and their grip). Every word is `strings.ts`'s (§39.1). THE REPLAY: `replay.ts` arms a recorder (the ghost's own `ControlTape` codec — one tape, never two encoders) on every slalom, downhill, time trial and tricks run at its first step, reads the afternoon off the run (`recipeOf`: the `Level`, which already carries the day, the sky and the region, the seed, the rules, the skis, the help, the damage switch, the snow dial, and on a slalom's second run its heat, on a downhill whether it was the training) and on WATCH REPLAY rebuilds it — checked against a fingerprint of the first step (`startPrint`) — so a field is dealt the same again — a line start's paces skied by the same bot to the same finish, a slalom's board. `replay-shots.ts` collects the moments off the player's events (a flight back-dated to its take-off, a pass off `racePlace` held for a second, a trunk, a rival, a wipeout, the finish), `planShots` ranks and spaces them, and `directAt(plan, step)` is the whole edit: which moment holds the frame and the TIME RATE — slow motion is fewer steps per frame and nothing else. `camera-tv.ts` plants the broadcast's lens at the edge of the wood beside the kicker, the arch or the spot, kept out of the trunks and the crowns by `camera-clear.ts`; the renderer applies it through `setShot` (`replay-run.ts` pushes it, on the `tv` rung of `WATCHING_CAMERAS` only). The adopted state is fresh, so the trail map starts from empty and is stamped again as the replay runs; `hud-replay.tsx` is the bar over it.

### The sound and the hands

`game/audio/` — every sound synthesized on one WebAudio instrument (the framework's `audio/synth`, under two faders in `bus.ts`) from authored parameters: the event one-shots (`bank.ts` routed by `route.ts`), the wind and the snow as steered layers (`wind-voice.ts` — the rush in the helmet rising with speed and closing in with the tuck; `snow-voice.ts` — the edges' hiss, chatter and scrape and the powder's whoosh; `ride-bed.ts`, which also raises the poles' clicks at a crawl), the wind tunnel's gale, body and fan (`tunnel-voice.ts`, off the same plan the picture is drawn from), the birds' cries off the same plan the renderer draws (`bird-voice.ts`, `bird-bed.ts`), heard from the camera's seat (`listener.ts`). There is no engine voice and no music. [audio.md](audio.md) is the account. What is FELT is `rumble.ts` (which moments are worth a pulse, DOM-free) over `haptics.ts` (the one motor: the Vibration API, or the store app's haptics through `shell-host.ts`).

## `tests/` and `scripts/`

Root vitest suites, one topic a file (`skier_test`, `flight_test`, `collision_test`, `course_test`, `crash_test`, `rivals_test`, `determinism_test`, `simulation_test`, …). The physics is staged on the SYNTHETIC maps in `tests/support/synthetic.ts` — a slope with a packed piste, a kicker, rollers and a lone tree, and flat drag strips on a grade of their own, packed or powder — so the rule suite passes with the generator deleted; `simulation_test` also skis generated mountains, `mapgen_test` holds the course a generated map is raced on to R1–R24 and `resort_test` the ski area round it to R25–R30 in every region (sharing their maps through `tests/support/levels.ts` and asking `tests/support/resort.ts` which runs cover a spot).

The app's DOM-free halves are tested the same way (`hud_test`, `minimap_test`, `input_model_test`, `menu_system_test`, `audio_test`, `rumble_test`, `world_render_test` — the camera rigs, the skier's pose, the sky's colour model, the track's arithmetic).

`scripts/` is tooling and may import anything, every flag through the framework's `tooling/cli`: `npm run sim` (the balance table), `npm run ride` (the ride lab: scripted scenarios on the synthetic maps, numbers and a picture each to `previews/ride-*.png`), `npm run level` and `npm run analyze` (a mountain from above; a map scored against the rule book), `npm run world` (one seed skied by the bot and photographed through the renderer from its own harness page), `npm run skis` (the pairs and the skier on contact sheets), `npm run audition` (the audio review page), and `scripts/screenshot.mjs` / `profile-render.mjs` over the built site. The README's Usage table is the list.

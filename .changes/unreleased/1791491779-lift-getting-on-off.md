type: Changed
title: Getting on and off the lifts reworked to move like the real thing
---

Getting on and off every lift is smoother and truer. Waiting on a chair's load line or a T-bar's track the skier looks back over his inside shoulder for the carrier coming round, and a chair scoops him from the station rail at a crawl — the very chair the lift is running, so no second chair appears. The safety bar comes down once the chair is clear of the station and goes up again before the top. Chairs and cabins sway on their hangers as they slow into a station, are pulled away from it and pass over the towers. At the top the chair comes down to the unload ramp and the skier stands up off it and slides away down the ramp instead of being popped off. An empty T-bar is reeled back up into its spring box rather than snapping short, and a gondola's rider steps in and walks out of the door at an unhurried pace. The ski area's crowd gets on and off the same way. Several small jumps in the skier's arms and legs while boarding are gone.

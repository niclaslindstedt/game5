type: Changed
title: A lift's rope cuts through a skier who comes down astride it hard enough to tear him apart
---

With injuries on, coming down astride a lift's rope so hard that the steel tears the skier apart no longer hangs him on it by the crotch: the rope goes through him, and what is left of him carries on down at the speed he fell. A softer drop still stops him on the rope and tips him off it.

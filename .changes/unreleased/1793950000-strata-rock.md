type: Changed
title: Mountain rock in bedded ledges
---

The bare rock on the mountain's steep faces is no longer a field of blocky spikes. It now stands in beds: thin and thick layers in packets, each a bare riser over a tread that holds a little snow, cut by buttresses and gullies running down the face. Cliff walls show irregular joints, dark undercuts and rime on their ledges. Loose rock lies in fans at the foot of the walls, fine stones by the wall and big blocks at the toe. Far faces read as dark banded rock with white ledges instead of a pale wash. The rock you ride on is exactly the rock you see.

A ski jump is now shown as television shows it: a broadcast director cuts from the bar to the profile camera beside the take-off, a rail camera beside the flight, a low camera at the landing and a crane over the out-run, keeping the jumper big in the frame (a camera press hands the run back to your own cameras), and the replay's broadcast runs the take-off and the landing in slow motion. The in-run now stands on a raised deck — a truss on concrete piers, its tower with an elevator and a stair — with the ground falling away beneath it.

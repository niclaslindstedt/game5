title: The rail jam, the fourth freestyle contest
---

The tricks card now has the RAIL JAM. The game builds a set of rails and boxes into the mountain: a short drop-in onto a gentle deck with five features side by side — a down box, a flat-down rail, a kinked box, a kinked rail and a rainbow rail — and a run-out to the finish line. The drop-in is sized so you meet the features at the speed a rail is ridden at.

It is a three-minute JAM against seven other riders, as the knuckle huck is. Every hit starts on the platform: pick a feature, ride onto its end and work it — swap between a 50-50 and a slide, press the nose or the tail, spin onto it, turn out a half turn at its end — then ride over the finish line and you are stood back up on the platform. Each hit is judged on the feature and how you rode it, and the session is ranked on your best three hits, how many different features and tricks they were, and your falls. The HUD names each hit's trick as a rider calls it, and the plate shows the board at the buzzer.

Picking the rail jam opens the ski card on the Hare at the medium build. `?start=railjam` boots straight into it.

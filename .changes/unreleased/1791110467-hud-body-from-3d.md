type: Changed
title: The body on the HUD is made from a real 3D body
---

The anatomy figure at the left edge of the HUD is now made from a whole 3D body scanned from one man, instead of a drawing fitted into a traced outline. The skin, every bone and the proportions between them come from that same body, so the bones sit in the flesh as they really do. The bones are grey and lit, with shadows in their hollows. The front shows only the front of the skeleton: the rib arcs, the breastbone, the vertebral bodies and the front of the pelvis. The feet are tipped toward you so every toe and metatarsal shows. A view from behind (the shoulder blades, the spine and the backs of the ribs) is built as well, but is not shown yet.

type: Fixed
title: A snowboarder skydives with his board on
---

A rider on a snowboard no longer falls out of the jump plane frozen in his riding pose. He stands sideways on the board in the door, facing out, with his hands on the door's posts, and turns into the slipstream as he leaves. In freefall he boxes with the board flat across his raised feet, tracks with his knees half bent and dives head down. He reaches for the pilot chute and throws it, punches the cut-away pad and pulls the reserve the way a skier does, and hangs on the toggles under the canopy with the board across under him. At the flare he turns side-on, the board along his line of flight, ready to ride it away.

title: The fall look, and steep faces that take a long fall
---

A long fall — pushed off the helicopter's skid, let go of the paramotor's wing, sent off a cliff band — now turns the camera to show where you will land: the chase, far and high lenses climb up over your back and tip the look down so you and the snow under your fall are both in the picture, and the tips and helmet lenses look down at it the way a skier spots a drop. And a steep face takes a long fall the way a big-mountain skier lands one: the skis run on down it while the legs fold, so turned down the fall line onto a 45° face you ride away a drop that folds your legs on the flat, at a speed that is hard to hold a line at. Falling, the skier now lays his skis onto however steep a slope he is about to land on, and does it sooner. A kicker's landing is judged exactly as before.

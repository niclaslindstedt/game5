title: A steadier snowmobile
---

The snowmobile is much harder to crash on snow. Rollers, whoops, kickers onto the flat, drops into powder and riding over the edge of a steep face are all ridden out now: in the air the machine sets its nose for the snow it will land on, and a landing only throws you if it comes down very hard — off a big drop onto the groomer or a cliff — or lands on its side or its nose. Hold the brake or lean back in the air and the nose is yours to drop or loop. Riding into a rock wall at speed now throws you too.

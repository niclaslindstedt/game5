type: Added
title: Hurt too badly to ski on, you are not stood back up
---

With INJURIES on, a fall that leaves the skier unable to get up (a broken leg or pelvis, a hurt spine or neck, a torn organ) ends the run INJURED, even if it happened while you were still on your skis: the injury cam follows him down, the picture fades to white, and the next run starts at the top.

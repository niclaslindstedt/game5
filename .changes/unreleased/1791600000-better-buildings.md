title: Real buildings at the lifts and the races
---

The lift stations are real buildings now instead of plain boxes. A chair's foot has a timber drive house with a roller door for the chairs. Its top has an operator's house beside the way off, and the white terminal hood with its red band stands over the bullwheel. A gondola stands in a glazed hall with a platform roof over its wheel. The drags have timber huts, and the operators sit in red glazed booths. Every wall shows what it is made of (weathered boards, ribbed steel, cast concrete, standing-seam roofs, windows, glass), every roof has snow on it, and the panes light up after dark.

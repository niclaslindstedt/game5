title: The dead left lying on the slope
---

With OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ INJURIES on, a rider who dies is no longer tidied away when the next one is stood up: his body as it lay, the pieces torn off him, his guts and his blood stay on the snow where they fell, so the next rider skis down past them. The last four dead are kept; they are cleared when the run goes back to the menu.

With INJURIES on, a skier who hits a tree, a rock, steel or a wall faster than 100 km/h is crushed where he hit it: the skull bursts, the limbs that met it are pulped, and the brain, guts and blood are left smeared on the bark or the stone. Met straight on, his middle is crushed: his arms and legs fly on past the tree, and three to four litres of blood spray out across the snow.

title: The army's snowmobiles come after you
---

From three stars the army sends military snowmobiles out of the village, two up: a driver and a gunner behind him, in olive drab daubed with white, with a rack of kit on the back. They read the snow as they come, going round rises, drops, water and trees, slowing in the woods and digging themselves out when they get held up, and the woods hide you from them. The gunner fires a light machine gun from a short halt: they stop side-on, fire a few bursts and drive on, so if you keep moving you are hard to hit. Ski into one and you knock its crew off. An empty one is yours to take with the machine key, though stealing it is another offence. With SAFE FOR WORK on they never hit you: they run you down, and the crew get off and arrest you.

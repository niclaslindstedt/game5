type: Added
title: The free ride's map hangs summit-up, and paints the mountain as a piste map does
---

The map on the free ride's start card now has the top of the mountain at the top, so every run falls down the picture instead of climbing it. The run lines on the campaign and level cards are turned the same way.

The card now opens on a PANORAMA: the mountain painted from out over the valley, the way a ski area's piste map hangs at the foot of its lifts. You see the lit snow and its blue shadows, the woods, the rock, the far ranges behind the summit and the village at the bottom. Over it is the whole ski area. Every run is drawn in its colour with its number in its grade's sign, and the course the map is raced on is drawn widest. The lifts are dark lines between their stations, and the start, the finish and the kickers are marked. A chip in the corner turns the card to the PLAN, the same mountain from above, and back. You can tap either view to start your ride there.

title: Snowboarder poses that read like a rider
---

A snowboarder on a toeside carve now drives his hips forward and his knees into the slope, and he never kneels on his shins. The heelside grabs are compact, with the hips over the board. In the method, the knees drive forward and the board is kicked up behind him. The mute's rear knee now bends as a grab's should.

The one-foot skate is ridden side-on. The board points along his way, the free rear foot pushes on the toe side, and he looks down the nose over his front shoulder.

The tuck sits lower and quieter, with the hands kept in.

The crowd's snowboarders no longer lie so far over on a toeside carve.

A rider no longer snaps round when he steps off his board in town or at a gondola's station, or when he clicks back in. He also holds the T-bar's stem in one hand.

title: Coarse rock on the drops
---

The cliff faces and the walls too steep for anyone to ski now have real
rock standing out of them: coarse, faceted blocks and slabs about as wide
as they are tall, packed into bands across each cliff and knots on the
walls, with broken rubble round their feet and snow on every ledge that
faces up. The rock is solid: a skier, his thrown body, his lost skis and
the machines hit it as they would a trunk. None stands on snow anyone
skis: not on the runs, not on the drops a run goes over, not on the steep
powder. Each block is a few triangles and is built only near the camera,
and a cheaper FOREST setting draws less rubble. The maritime country,
whose snow holds on everything, has none.

title: What is left of an animal you run down
---

With INJURIES on, an animal you hit hard on a free ride is drawn crushed: it lies on its side with its spine broken into a V, its flank stove in and opened to the meat, its legs flung every way and its coat spattered. Its blood sprays out along the way it was thrown and back over you, its organs, ribs, coat and (for a deer, a reindeer or a moose) the chewed browse in its stomach are thrown across the snow, a big one's guts drag behind it as it slides, and it leaves a red smear and a growing pool where it stops. A hare or a fox hit hard bursts, leaving only a red fan on the snow. You hear an animal you knock down thump and cry out, and one you run through crunch. The controller rumbles with the blow, and a hit that does not throw you gets its own news line. Safe for work, the animal is only knocked over and gets back up.

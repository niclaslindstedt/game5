title: Gore and death with injuries on
---

With OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ INJURIES on, a crash can now kill. A blow far past a broken bone tears a limb, the head or the body in two, crushes the skull or opens the chest, and landing on a bare tree top or a post runs the skier through. Blood sprays from the wounds on the heartbeat and soaks the snow and the clothes, organs and bone shards fly out, and broken legs and arms show the bone through the skin. The grimbear now tears the skier in two. The HUD takes the blows with him: it jolts, cracks from serious injuries on, and when he dies it falls apart, DIED comes up with the cause, the picture goes dark and the run starts again from the top with a new rider. With INJURIES off, none of it is there.

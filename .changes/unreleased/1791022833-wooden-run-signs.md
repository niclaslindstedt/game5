type: Changed
title: Wooden piste signs with the names burned in by hand
---

The signs at the top of every run are now wooden boards with the run's name and arrow burned into the grain in thick black hand lettering, the grade's mark painted on the wood. Each kind of country signs its runs in its own hand and its own wood: the alpine in a fat rounded hand on oiled larch, the fells in a tall solid hand on pale birch, the continental ranges in a heavy slanted brush on dark pine, and the maritime ranges in a thick marker on grey cedar. The boards hang on square timber posts.

The signs no longer glow at night. They are lit like the wood they are made of: the sun rakes across the burned letters by day, and after dark a sign shows only where the moon, a floodlight or a headlamp falls on it.

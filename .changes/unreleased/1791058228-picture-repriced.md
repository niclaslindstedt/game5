PRESET ▸ AUTO now fits the picture to your machine using costs measured for this game. Until now it used prices carried over from the sibling game. On a fast machine nothing changes, and you still get the whole picture. On a machine about twice too slow for the top picture, AUTO now gives up the skiers' sharp shadows, part of the draw distance and some of the far woods. Before, it kept all three and gave up less of the shadows. On slower machines it lowers the resolution sooner than it did.

title: Wingsuit and skydive cameras chase from behind, and speed is felt
---

The wingsuit's and the skydive's chase cameras now fly straight behind the rider, looking where he is going; a wingsuit jump opens on a dramatic shot and is behind him within two seconds. In the air the view widens and pulls in with speed, lags behind a dive, rocks in the air and banks into turns, and on skis and a snowboard the chase cants a little into a carve. At pace the ice crystals in the air streak past the lens.

title: The lifts' stations are built out, and a free ride starts on the chairlift
---

Every top station stands on a level pad cut into the slope, its edge a lip onto the face, with a chair's unload ramp, the terminal's hood, the operator's glass booth, the stop gate, orange netting and a wind mast, and the foot of every lift has its corral and blue load line, a gondola its station door. Every chair, cabin and T-bar moves all the time. A free ride now starts seated on the chair up to the run you picked: it lurches over the last tower, slows into the top, stands you up on the ramp — the camera held level so the drop off the top reads as the drop it is — and any lift on the mountain takes you up when you ride into its load zone.

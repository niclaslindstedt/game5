The phone app never shows a wound, a death or the X-ray cam, plays safe for work throughout, and has no INJURIES, X-RAY CAM or DAMAGE HUD rows in OPTIONS nor injury figures on STATISTICS. Its App Store build has no developer page, so PLAY is the free ride. The website and the desktop app are unchanged.

title: The knuckle huck, the second freestyle contest
---

The tricks card now has a KNUCKLE HUCK. The game builds a jump with no kicker: a short drop-in onto a level deck that rolls over a sharp knuckle onto a steep landing. On it you ride a three-minute JAM against seven other riders, and hit the knuckle as often as the clock allows. You are stood back on the platform after every hit.

New tricks for it: BUTTERS AND PRESSES. On the deck, hold the lean hard to press the nose or the tail. Steer while you press to butter, pivoting on that end. Pop off the knuckle mid-butter and the turn carries into the air and is landed square.

The judges call every hit, for example "NOSE BUTTER RIGHT 360", and mark the whole session rather than each hit: the best three hits, a little more for variety, a little less for each fall. The HUD shows the session's mark, the clock, your hits and your place. At the buzzer, the plate shows the board.

Picking the knuckle huck opens the ski card on the Hare, the soft park twin-tip that presses easily, at the medium build. `?start=knuckle` boots straight into a jam.

On the MEDIUM and HIGH forest settings the woods are drawn with their needles on cards: drooping boughs with snow lying in shelves on their tops over a dark core, bare trunks under old spruce, larch and birch as a haze of twigs. The cards come in three cuts that dissolve into one another with distance — the fullest tree nearest on HIGH, a lighter one with fewer, wider cards nearest on MEDIUM, then a cloud of a few crossed silhouettes — and past the middle distance the faceted far sketch takes over. Every card is built two-sided so the trunks and cores inside a crown are never drawn from inside, a low sun no longer lights a crown or its snow near-white, the shadows are cast by a cheap faceted cut, and LOW keeps the faceted woods.

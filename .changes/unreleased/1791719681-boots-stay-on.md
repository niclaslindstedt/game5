type: Fixed
title: The boots stay on the skier's feet when his skis come off
---

When a fall throws the skier off his skis, the bindings now let go of the boots and the boots stay on his feet, as a real ski binding works. The skis slide off with empty bindings and their brakes down, and he lies, gets up and walks back to them in his boots. A snowboard still stays strapped on through a fall, because snowboard bindings do not release.

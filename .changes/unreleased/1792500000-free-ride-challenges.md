type: Added
title: Free ride challenges, with gold, silver and bronze on every mountain
---

Every free ride mountain now deals its own challenges, each with a bronze, a silver and a gold. Ride a timed LINE between a pair of start flags and a pair of finish flags down one run: the HUD shows the clock against the medal still in reach and how far down you are, and a fall, a reset, a lift or wandering off the run calls the attempt off. Land a trick off a chosen KICKER (a clean air for bronze, a spin or a flip sized to the kicker for silver and gold). Find the HIDDEN SPOTS off the pistes, which appear on the minimap only once found. Stand at the top of EVERY SUMMIT. The news column calls every start, half-way split, finish, medal and new best, a plate with the medal comes up at the finish, the minimap marks the flags, the kickers and the summits still to reach, the pause card has a CHALLENGES list with every target and your best (and RIDE to go straight to a line's run), and the STATISTICS card counts your gold, silver and bronze. Medals are kept per mountain on this device.

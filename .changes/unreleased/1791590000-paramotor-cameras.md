type: Changed
title: Paramotor cameras
---

Under the paramotor's wing the first-person cameras now look like a pilot's own footage. HELMET is a wide helmet camera with the horizon high and your knees, boots and skis hanging in the bottom of the frame; it banks with the wing and turns a little into a turn. TIPS looks down past your skis at the drop, with your gloves on the toggles and the risers in view. HIGH is a canopy camera, under the wing's middle looking down the lines at you over the mountain. On the summit, before you fly, your eyes stay up on the slope ahead.

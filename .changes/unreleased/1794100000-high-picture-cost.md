---
type: Changed
title: The HIGH picture draws about a fifth faster, and no longer stalls as a run gets going
---

On a fast laptop at 1080p, a race at the HIGH preset went from about 80 to over 100 frames a second, with the same picture. The snow round your skis is drawn with far fewer, larger triangles wherever no ski has touched it; the other skiers' lines on the piste, your own skis' grooves and the bumps' shadows skip the work that could not change a pixel; and the game no longer warns itself into a slower frame on every draw of the ground. Starting a run no longer stutters in its first seconds while the clouds and the sky finish loading, and skiing past a cliff for the first time no longer freezes a frame.

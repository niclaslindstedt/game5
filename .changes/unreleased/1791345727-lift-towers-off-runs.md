---
type: Fixed
title: Lift towers no longer stand in the middle of the runs
---

A lift's towers are now slid along its line until they stand clear of the
runs, and where a line crosses a run the rope spans it instead of planting
a tower on the snow. A tower that still has to stand on or right beside a
run is wrapped in a red foam pad, as a real ski area pads one, and a skier
who hits it hits the pad.

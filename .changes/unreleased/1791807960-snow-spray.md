The **SNOW A TURN THROWS** looks like real spray now, off skis and off a snowboard alike: the snow is cut by an edge sliding across its own line, so a hockey stop or a skidded turn throws a fan of streaking grains ahead of the rider and down the hill — and a board's whole long edge throws it as a pair's two do — while a clean carve flicks only a thin tail. The fine mist rises off the same edge and flies the same way, in smaller, lower puffs on the groomer than in powder.

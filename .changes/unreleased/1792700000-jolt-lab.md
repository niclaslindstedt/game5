**Smoother skiers.** A wipeout no longer throws the skier's limbs half a metre in one frame as he goes from his skis onto the ragdoll and back. His fist no longer turns half round on the pole when his forearm swings through the pole's line. A snowboarder no longer hops on his board as he lands. A thrown skier's elbow no longer snaps across his arm. A new lab, `make jolt`, finds snaps like these in every animation.

title: The army's helicopters hunt you
---

Steal a helicopter and the army's helicopters now come for you. A few seconds after the stars go up they fly in from the edge of the map, and more come as the stars climb: one at first, two at four stars, three at five. They circle you with a door open and the gunner on his gun, and they fire on you: warning shots at two stars, aimed fire from three. Fly off in the stolen machine and they sit off your tail and shoot it down. Ram one and you bring it down in flames, and your own machine with it. Lose them, and they circle where they last saw you; get away, and they fly home. You hear their heavy rotors and twin turbines come and go. With SAFE FOR WORK on they only ever fire warning shots.

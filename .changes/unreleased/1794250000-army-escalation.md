title: The army hunt builds a star at a time, can be hidden from, and fights on from its wrecks
---

Stealing the air ambulance now starts the hunt at one star, and a police helicopter at two. The stars climb on their own a step at a time, so a hunt that keeps you in sight reaches five stars in under four minutes. The army's aim tightens with each star: at three stars most of its fire cracks past you, and at five it shoots to its weapons' best.

Stop under a tree and you are hidden from the helicopters, the paramotors and the balloons, though not from the men on the snow. While the army searches, the HUD shows OUT OF SIGHT over a bar that runs down to the moment you lose them, and HIDDEN when you are under cover.

The carriers, trucks and tanks drive round the wind tunnels instead of through them. The trucks bring their men in close and send them straight after you. A vehicle knocked out by a rocket or a shell puts its crew out onto the snow to fight on. A balloon can now be brought down by a burst near it or by a stolen helicopter's blades. It falls, burning with the injuries on, and its crew climbs out to fight on foot. The paramotor's rifleman shoulders his rifle to fire.

The army's vehicles, snowmobiles and balloons are painted a lighter olive that reads on the snow. A truck's tailgate clanks open as its men get out, and a torn wing or envelope rips. Every army vehicle, snowmobile and soldier is built behind the loading card, so the first one onto the snow no longer stutters.

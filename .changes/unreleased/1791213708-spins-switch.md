title: Tap spins and loops in the air, and riding switch, on the free ride and tricks
---

In the air on a free ride or a tricks run, a tap of left or right now throws a quick half turn: tap once and the skier lands with the skis backward and rides away switch, tap twice for a full 360. A tap of the lean (or W / S pressed in the air) throws a whole loop — a backflip or a front flip — and tapping again makes it a double. Every throw stops square on its angle and is timed to finish before the landing; one that could not come round in the air left is not thrown, so a lean for the landing stays a lean. The free ride now flies under the same real gravity as the tricks run, so the jump key's own pop gives the air to come round. Riding backward, the chase camera swings round behind the way the skier is going, left and right steer the way you press, and the yaw keeps the tails on the line. On the groomer any pair rides switch; in loose snow the tails dig in and throw the skier, except on the Hare, whose twin tips ride over it. A 180 landed scores on a tricks run. Races are unchanged: no spins, no loops and no riding switch.

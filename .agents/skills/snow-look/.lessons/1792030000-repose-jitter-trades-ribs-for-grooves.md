---
title: The deformable field's repose jitter trades a crater's ribs for its shallow grooves
date: 2026-10-15
scope: pwa/src/game/deform-field.ts
concepts: [deform, crater, repose, jitter]
---

A repose jitter drawn per cell set every column of a crater's wall at its
own slope, and the wall stood in vertical ribs a cell wide. Drawing most of
it as value noise over 30 cm broke the wall into lumps instead — and also
let a lone ski's shallow bounce mark beside the crater slump away, which
only the per-cell hash's few stiff cells had held. A narrow groove in loose
snow is above the loose repose by its shape; holding one honestly wants a
cohesion term (a narrow wall standing steeper than a wide one), not luckier
noise. Judge a repose change on the snow lab's `crater` `over` lens at day
and low sun, and look beyond the crater for the marks round it.

---
type: Changed
title: Pause on the minimap, redrawn reset and camera marks
---

The pause button is gone from the top-right corner: press the minimap to pause instead (Escape still does it, and with the HUD off a pause button stands in the map's place). The reset mark is redrawn as a whole ring with its arrowhead on the curve, no longer sliced flat down its right side on a phone held sideways, and the camera mark's two reels now sit over the camera's body instead of hanging off its back. The video wall at the finish now fades into the haze, a fog or a storm like everything else around it, instead of standing out sharp and dark through the weather.

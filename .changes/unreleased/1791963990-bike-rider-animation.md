type: Changed
title: A more natural downhill bike rider
---

The rider on the downhill bike now rides in a real attack stance: knees and elbows bent and out, never locked, and his eyes on the trail ahead. His body follows the bike smoothly instead of snapping between poses, his head stays steady in the air, getting back on is a proper mount (hands to the grips, a leg swung over the saddle), walking beside the bike has soft knees and a natural stride, and the cranks sit level when coasting with the outside pedal down in hard turns.

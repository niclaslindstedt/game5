title: A mountain guide to ride as
---

The rider card has a new body, GUIDE: a backcountry mountain guide modelled in Blender — a black hardshell with mustard shoulders and sleeves, a white avalanche pack with a shovel, a probe and flagging tape on it, a radio on a chest harness, a helmet and goggles. He is posed by every animation the game already had, a broken arm hangs from the break, and his pack, the radio's antenna and the tape swing on springs as he turns, lands and tucks while his jacket's hem flaps harder the faster he goes. Picking him leaves only the build to choose; every other skier is still dressed in code.

**The horizon stands where it really is.** Up a mountain the Earth curves away under you, so the sky now ends a little under the level — about a degree from a summit, as the real dip is — and what lies below it is no longer a flat wall the colour of the sky: it is far country seen through the air, the horizon's own haze where it meets the sky and darker further down — blue-grey by day, lit on the far ranges facing the sun, dim at dusk — with no hard line between them, in every weather and at every picture setting.

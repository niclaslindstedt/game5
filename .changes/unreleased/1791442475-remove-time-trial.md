---
type: Removed
title: The time trial is gone
---

The TIME TRIAL is no longer a mode the player picks: its tile is gone from the front door, along with its level card, its `?mode=trial` link and its finish plate. The ghost raced beside a trial and the trial's laps setting went with it. The campaign's eight time-trial rungs and their medals are gone too. Every shelf is now races only (three, five, four and four of them, sixteen in all), and the next map opens behind a podium. A board kept from before still counts its races.

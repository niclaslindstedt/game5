A spin can be started at any point in a jump, not only right at take-off. Lean hard left or right in the air and you wind up a spin that way. Hold the lean for longer to spin faster, and let go to keep spinning. Leaning the other way slows the spin a little, so landing it square depends on when you let go. The skier now leads the spin with his shoulders and head and pulls his arms in.

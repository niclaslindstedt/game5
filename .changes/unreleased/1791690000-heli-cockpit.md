The helicopter has a cockpit view. On the HELMET camera the player sits in the
pilot's seat of a light helicopter's cockpit: the cyclic, the collective and
the pedals moving with the controls being flown and the pilot's hands and feet
on them, a panel of working instruments (airspeed, attitude, altitude, rotor
speed, heading, vertical speed, radar altitude, the engine display and a
terrain map), the pedestal, the overhead and the seats behind. The nose camera
looking down at the snow moves to TIPS.

**A bike park in summer.** A summer free ride's ski area now has a bike park. Trails leave the tops of the chairs and gondolas, each from its own signed head on the lift's apron. Greens and blues are pale gravel flow trails with berms, rollers and tabletops. Reds and blacks are narrow dirt trails through the woods with rock gardens, roots, drops and step-downs. A taped downhill race line runs 400–700 m down the mountain to a finish jump. The trails are cut into the mountainside as real ground, felling the trees in their way, and they show on the minimap in their grades' colours.

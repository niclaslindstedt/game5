type: Changed
title: A mountain 1420 m and more tall, and the altitude on the HUD
---

Every free ride's mountain is built by a new generator (v8): its peak stands 1420–1620 m over a valley floor whose lowest ground is only 10–20 m above the sea, in every kind of country, on a map 4 km a side so its runs fall about as steeply as before over half again the vertical; its hills, spurs and gullies, bowls, headwalls and side ridges rise and fall about twice as far, and a run the steeper face will not carry at its colour comes down a colour harder. The tree line stands the same share of the mountain as before. The HUD reads the skier's height above sea level under the speed (ALT) on every map. The campaign's and the race cards' pinned maps stay on the generators they were curated on.

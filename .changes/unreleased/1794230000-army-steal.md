title: Steal the army's carriers, trucks, tanks and helicopters
---

When the army hunts you, you can take its own machines. Walk up to a parked tracked carrier, a parked truck or a halted tank and press the machine key at its door to steal it. You can also take an army helicopter once it has set down. Each theft raises your wanted level, and a tank or a helicopter takes it to at least four stars.

The ground vehicles drive on the same controls as the snowmobile and keep their own limits: the truck stalls on a slope the tank climbs. In the tank, lean to turn the turret and press jump to fire the main gun, which knocks out the army's own vehicles where its shell bursts. The army hunts the vehicle you drive, and enough fire knocks a carrier or a truck out under you.

The army helicopter flies as your own, in its army colours. From the driver's eye you sit in a full carrier cab: live dials, switches, the radio and the gear selector, with your hands turning the wheel. In the helicopter you sit on a two-pilot flight deck with a full instrument panel, consoles overhead and between the seats, and the cyclic, collective and pedals moving under your hands. The HUD shows the machine, its speed and the tank's turret and gun.

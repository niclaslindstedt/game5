title: Six snowboards, and the rider picked before the gear
---

There are six snowboards now, each a real kind of board with its own shape, stance and topsheet: the Lynx (all-mountain), the Pika (a soft, forgiving beginner's board), the Stoat (a park twin), the Ptarmigan (a powder board that floats first), the Wolf (a stiff big-mountain charger) and the Goshawk (an alpine carving board ridden in hard boots, the fastest and the hardest to ride).

Picking a run now opens the RIDER card first — the body, the weight and the kit — and NEXT goes on to the GEAR card. On a free ride and the park run the gear card has a SKIS / SNOWBOARD switch across its top, each with its own rack and spec sheet, and each remembers the last one picked. Poles are picked with the skis.

The free ride's START row has ON A SNOWBOARD beside ON SKIS: it starts the ride on the board you last picked.

Skiers ride as before.

The minimap shows the mountain again, not just the lines of the runs. In some browsers its ground went missing, leaving a bare grey disc with only the runs and lifts drawn on it, and the piste's broad band was gone too. The plate is now painted on a canvas, so it looks the same in every browser, and if the background worker that paints the ground cannot run, the game paints it itself. The ground also shows more of the country: the woods as green forest with every tree in them, rock on the faces too steep to hold snow, and a sharper picture on the big ski-area maps.

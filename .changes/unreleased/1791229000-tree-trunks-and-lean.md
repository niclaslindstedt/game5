title: Solid tree trunks, and trees that lean
---

Tree trunks are solid again. They were built inside out, so you saw the inside of the far side through the near side. Each section of a trunk was also turned against the one below it, which left notches at every joint. Now every trunk is one unbroken stem, lit in its own bark.

The trees no longer all stand dead upright. Most lean a few degrees, and on a slope nearly all of them lean down it, the way mountain woods grow. About one tree in a hundred leans hard, past fifteen degrees, and a rare one goes as far as thirty. None of them is fallen. Only the picture changes: you still hit a trunk at its foot, exactly where you did before.

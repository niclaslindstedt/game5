type: Added
title: A skier you run into lies broken and bleeding, and it sounds like a body
---

With INJURIES on, a skier you hit at speed now looks, sounds and feels like a real body hit. Someone you kill lies broken on the snow: legs and arms bent where no joint is, a broken thigh lying short with the foot turned out, the head at a wrong angle, the skis off his boots, his clothes soaked with blood. The harder the hit, the flatter his body lies. Someone you only hurt stays down with a broken leg and a bleeding face, and still moves a little. Blood sprays out at the hit, leaves a smear down his slide and pools where he stops. At the hardest hits, flesh and broken ribs are thrown out too. The hit sounds like a body of your own weight, with a heavy thud and the air knocked out of him, or broken ribs when he is killed. You also feel it as a strong pulse on a phone or gamepad that can vibrate. With INJURIES off, none of this happens: the skier is knocked down and gets back up. `make gore ARGS=--sheet=person` shows hits at 30, 60 and 90 km/h.

The helicopter's cockpit no longer fills with snow on lift-off: the rotor's wash, the falling snow and the spray are kept out of the cabin. Leaving the helicopter from the cockpit view now flies the camera out to the chase so the fall and the death cam show the skier and the machine instead of the inside of the cabin. The cockpit itself is rebuilt to read as a moulded interior: a shroud and coaming round the panel up to the windscreen, rubber seals round every window, a shaded liner, cushioned seats with grey shells, floor mats and an extinguisher.

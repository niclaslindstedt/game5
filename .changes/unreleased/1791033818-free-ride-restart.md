---
type: Fixed
title: A free ride's restart and reset take you back up to the start
---

On a free ride, START AGAIN on the pause card now skis the same mountain again from where you started. Before, it could leave the picture stuck where you stood when you'd picked a sky or a time of day. The reset (R, or the HUD's reset button) at the foot of the piste, on the finish line or in the arena past it, now takes you up to the start line instead of leaving you where you are. Anywhere higher up, it still puts you on the nearest run.

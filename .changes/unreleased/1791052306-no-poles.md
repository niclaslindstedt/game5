title: Skiing without poles
---

A hard mode: OPTIONS ▸ POLES OFF sends the skier out with nothing in his hands. With no poles there is no double pole — he skates at every speed on his legs alone, slower across the flat; a rise he would skate up on his poles is a crawl, and a steeper one beyond him; and with nothing to catch a lurch on he keeps less of his balance, so a hard landing, a caught edge or a trunk throws him sooner, and he rocks out of a bog in deep powder worse. The skier is animated for it: a speed skater's arm swing when he skates, a walker's when he sets off, his arms held out wide for balance as he rides and reaching for the snow inside a turn, his fists ahead of his knees in the start gate — and no pole plant anywhere, seen or heard. The time trial's ghost and the replay remember whether a run had poles. Every rival keeps his.

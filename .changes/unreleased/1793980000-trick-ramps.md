title: Trick ramps on every slope
---

The free ride's start card has a TRICK RAMPS row. Turned on, every piste of the ski area carries ramps down one side of the run, leaving the middle clear: a low roller, a medium and a big table, and two steep lips at 45° and 55° that throw you high, each with a landing ramp across a gap behind it. Come at a steep one too slowly and you drop into the gap. `?start=free&ramps=1` asks for them in a link.

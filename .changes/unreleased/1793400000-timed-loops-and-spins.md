Loops and spins are no longer snapped round onto the skier's feet. The turn is set as he leaves the lip, and after that the lean and the edge only tuck him in or open him out to speed it up or slow it. A skier who comes round near his skis spots the landing and lands on them; one who does not lands on his back, his side or his tips. The downhill bike's air assist now squares the bike up only when it is already near the ground's line.

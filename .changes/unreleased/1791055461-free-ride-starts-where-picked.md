A free ride now starts where you tap the chart. You stand on the snow at that spot instead of being carried up the nearest chairlift to the top of a run beside it. With no spot picked, the GRADE you chose decides the run: you ride whichever lift serves that colour's run (a drag, the gondola or a chair) to its top and are led onto it. Before, you always rode a chair, which on most mountains meant a black or red run near the summit even when you asked for green. Changing the GRADE or the COUNTRY on the start card now clears the spot you picked, and the chart's mark goes back to the new mountain's start gate.

type: Changed
title: Piste machines modelled at true size, and a fleet of them
---

The free ride's night piste machines are now a model made in Blender, built to the measured size of the largest groomers a ski area runs: 8.9 m long with the blade and tiller, 4.2 m wide over the belts, 2.9 m tall over the lamp bars, with a blade 5.25 m wide and 1.17 m tall and a tiller 5.5 m wide. The belts' cleats run round at the machine's speed. The blade lifts on a pivot, the tiller lifts when it is up, and a heap of snow rolls ahead of the blade while it works. Every lamp's glow sits on its own lens. A bigger ski area now has more machines out at night: three to six, one to a run.

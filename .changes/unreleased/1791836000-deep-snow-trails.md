title: Trails read by how deep the snow is — a scuff, a trough, a trench
---

A skier's track now looks different in every depth of snow. On the groomer the skis leave two thin lines pressed to a cool polish. In ordinary powder the track is a soft trough a boot deep, its walls crumbled, the two ski grooves showing in its floor. In deep fresh snow it is a trench: its walls fall back to about as wide as it is deep, so both skis leave one wide track, the walls are broken into blocks, the floor is down out of the sky's light and goes blue, and the clods it threw out lie along its rim.

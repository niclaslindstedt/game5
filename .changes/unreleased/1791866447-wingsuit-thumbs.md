**The touch controls fly a wingsuit, not skis.** In a wingsuit the left thumb's pad is the suit seen from above, turned with the bank and slid with the pitch; the right thumb's knob is the suit too, its arms swept back at a full tuck and spread wide past the shut mark. Under any canopy the pad is the canopy, and down out of a wingsuit on foot it is his boots.

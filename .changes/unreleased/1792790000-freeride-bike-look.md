A second look for the summer downhill bike, behind the `?bikelook=freeride` link: a big-mountain freeride bike with a coil shock, a beefy single-crown fork and fat tyres, ridden by a freerider in a loose jersey, baggy shorts, knee pads, long socks, flat shoes and a full-face helmet. He rides it with style: he whips the bike sideways or lays it flat in a table off long jumps, puts his inside foot out in a sliding turn and drops his weight back on the brakes.

A chair no longer stands you up at the top by itself: its safety bar swings up short of the unload, and you get off by pressing the tuck (W, or the tuck lever on touch). Touch nothing and you stay sat, riding round the top wheel and back down the line facing the valley with the bar up. Press the tuck with the bar up anywhere but over the unload ramp and you jump off the seat, landing or crashing as any drop that high would — too early, too high, and it can kill you. Looking round from the chair still works as before.

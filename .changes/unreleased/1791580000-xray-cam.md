title: X-ray cam on a hard blow
---

With OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ INJURIES on, a blow that will break a bone is now seen coming: the run slows almost to a stop, the lens closes in on the bone, the skier's skin turns to glass over his skeleton and organs, and the bone cracks, breaks or shatters in front of you. Every other big bone that goes is a shot of its own, the lens panning to it, and so is a limb torn off. Then the lens pulls out to the whole body, the skin comes back, the blood shows on the snow and the run speeds back up — and if he is dying, it stays on him until he dies. The skeleton and organs are made from BodyParts3D (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan).

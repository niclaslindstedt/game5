title: Flips that read like real ones
---

A flip now reads like a real one: the arms throw it (up into a backflip, down into a front flip), the skier pulls into a tight tuck, then opens from upside down with the legs reaching for the snow, the arms out wide and the head spotting the landing. On a snowboard the knees come up into the tuck with the grab.

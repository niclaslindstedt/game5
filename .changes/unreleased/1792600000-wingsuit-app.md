title: The wingsuit on the start card, and out of every craft
---

The start card's START row now has a WINGSUIT stop: you begin on the wingsuit's exit high on the mountain, no skis on. On the paramotor, the balloon, the helicopter and the plane a new SUIT row picks between the parachute and the wingsuit. In the suit, every way off the craft is flown in the wingsuit, at any height, and a restart puts you back in the craft. While you fly, the HUD reads the height over the snow, which turns amber as it closes on the pull and red with PULL! at 350 m. It also shows the air speed, the glide and the sink, and names the press: JUMP, then PULL. You hear the suit's low flutter rise under the roar of the air. The statistics count the metres flown in the suit. Links take `?wingsuit=1` and `?suit=wing` or `?suit=rig`.

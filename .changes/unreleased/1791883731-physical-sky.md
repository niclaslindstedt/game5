**The clear sky is now physical.** The sky's colours come from a model of the real atmosphere (the air, its haze and ozone, light scattered many times, the planet's own shadow, a ground of fresh snow), so a sunrise and a sunset glow yellow, orange and red toward the sun with a deep orange disc on the horizon, the pink Belt of Venus stands over the blue band of Earth's shadow on the other side just after sunset, the blue hour is blue, and the far ridges go dark against the glow. The sun's light on the snow is golden in a low sun and the shade blue, and the eye adapts through the twilights so dusk still reads as a lit sky before the night falls.

title: The death card names where the next rider starts
---

The death card no longer always says the new rider starts at the top: it names where the restart really stands him — the start of a race or a tricks run, and on a free ride the top of the slope he last skied, the helipad, the snowmobile, the summit under the paramotor's wing or the balloon's basket, as the start card chose.

type: Fixed
title: Every run off a lift's top can be reached
---

A free ride begun on a lift now rides only its last three seconds, the top station close ahead. Every run now starts just below the top of the lift that serves it, instead of tens of metres up the slope above it where a skier off the T-bar could not climb to it and the amateurs floated up. Riding a drag lift you now hang on a T-bar of your own, under your seat.

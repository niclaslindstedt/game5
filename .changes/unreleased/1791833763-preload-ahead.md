**Less waiting and smoother menus on the way onto the snow.** The rider and gear cards' skier turns smoothly under a finger from the start: nothing hidden behind the picture is drawn while it is up, the gear card's look is prepared before it opens, and any work being done ahead waits while the rider is being turned. A race's map is built while its map is picked, and its whole world while the skier and gear are picked, so RIDE loads much faster. A free ride's world starts building on the start card once its choices settle.

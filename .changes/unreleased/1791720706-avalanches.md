title: Avalanches on the free ride
---

A free ride's slopes off the groomed runs can now slide. A steep, open slope (about 30° to 50°) carries a slab that grows with the new snow, the snow dial, a snowing sky and, most of all, the wind drifting snow onto the slopes facing down it. Crossing one, or landing on one, can break it: the crown cracks above the skier, and the slab runs down the fall line as a dense flow. Ski out of its flank or ahead of it to outski it. Caught, the skier is swept onto his ragdoll and carried, then put down in the debris. With his head out he gets up as from any fall. Buried, he has a clock off the real survival curve, compressed for play. `?avalanche=0` turns avalanches off. `make avalanche-flow` is the new lab.

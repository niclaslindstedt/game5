---
type: Changed
title: Every tree shaped on the graphics card
---

The woods are now drawn from a handful of template meshes that the graphics card shapes into each tree: no two trees on the mountain are the same tree. Each carries its own tier count, snow load and white shelves on every tier. Exposed trees near the tree line are flagged to the lee and plastered with rime. The boughs and shadows sway in the wind, the snow glows against a low sun, and far trees are ray-cast inside a single quad each, so they still read as trees from the air. The forest takes a small fraction of the draw calls and memory it did.

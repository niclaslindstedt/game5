---
type: Changed
title: The signs at a lift's top are arrows, and the signs on the runs are plain
---

The run signs beside a lift top's piste map board are now themselves arrows: each board is cut to a point at one end, standing off its post toward its slope, with nothing painted on it but the run's mark and name. The runs that leave to the left are signed on the map's left and those that leave to the right on its right, stacked on that side's post when several go the same way. Down on the runs, the signs are plain boards with no arrow.

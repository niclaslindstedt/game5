---
type: Changed
title: Faster nights, a LAMPS row and finer RESOLUTION steps
---

After dark the snow's sparkle under the lamps is worked out once for all of them instead of once per lamp, which makes night runs lighter on every machine without changing the look. OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE has a new LAMPS row (how many lamps light the snow at once: two, four or six — your own headlamp always one of them; LOW also leaves out the snow's glitter in the beams) and RESOLUTION now steps in tenths, from 50% to 100% of the screen's pixels, so a phone that is just short of a smooth frame can give up a little sharpness instead of a lot.

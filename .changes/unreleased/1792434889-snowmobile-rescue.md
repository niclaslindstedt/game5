type: Added
title: The ski patrol comes on snowmobiles, and you can steal them
---

Where an injured skier lies somewhere no helicopter can set down, such as a steep face or the woods, the ski patrol comes for him on two snowmobiles. The first tows an empty rescue toboggan up from the nearest lift station. The patrol parks beside him, loads him into the toboggan and tows him slowly down to the bottom of the mountain, with the second machine following. While the patrol works, either machine can be stolen with the machine press. It becomes your snowmobile, in the patrol's colours, and your own stays where you left it so you can take it back. Steal the towing machine and the other one takes the toboggan down instead.

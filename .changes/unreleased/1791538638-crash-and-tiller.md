A helicopter coming down with the skier on its skid is no longer cut to a slow-motion close-up of him: it blows up first, and the lens then follows him flung out of it, kept out of the station houses and cabins he flies past. A skier under a piste machine is now seen spat out of the back of its tiller in a jet of blood, flesh and bone, smearing the fresh corduroy red, from a closer, lower lens over its tail.

# Adding a real face: a real mountainside, named by nothing

A REAL FACE is a 4×4 km stretch of a real ski mountain, from its summit
ridge down to its valley floor, turned so its fall line runs down the map
(`docs/real-faces.md` is the description; read it first). It carries two
GENERATED files of its own — its heights (`real-faces/face-<id>.ts`) and
its ski area's hints (`real-hints/hints-<id>.ts`), each listed in an index
beside them (`real-faces-index.ts`: its region and country;
`real-hints-index.ts`: the grades its pistes are signed) — and both are
baked off ONE crop, so a face is first of all a row in
`scripts/lib/real-face-crops.mjs`. A face's files are FETCHED ONLY WHEN A
MAP IS RAISED ON IT (`loadRealFace`, a chunk of their own the service worker
keeps once fetched), so the bundle carries the indexes alone and a face
costs a player nothing until it is picked. This
skill is the list of what follows from that row, in order, with what each
step owes before the next. It routes to **`mapgen-improvement`** (how the
massif lays the relief, how the lifts and runs lean on the hints, the
rules a face is held to — `RR.massif.real`), **`buildings`** (the real
houses as stood, `engine/game/real-houses.ts`) and **`lift-areas`** (the
stations the leant lifts end in). Load **`write-code`** beside them and
**`skill-reflection`** at both ends.

**Read this skill's lessons first** —
`npx ogf-skill-lessons add-real-face --list`.

## The promises

- **NO DEALT MAP MOVES.** A face is read only after every draw off the
  stream, and the hints only where `level.face` is set, so a map with no
  face builds byte-identically. `make sim`'s digests are the proof — run it
  before and after and put both tables in the PR. A moved digest is a bug in
  the change, never a version row.
- **A FACE IS NAMED BY ITS REGION AND A NUMBER, NOTHING ELSE.** No resort,
  mountain, valley, lift or piste name enters the tree — not in the crop
  row, a comment, a commit, a PR, a lesson or a lab picture's file name. The
  bakes read no `name` tag. The crop's latitude and longitude are numbers
  and stay; what they point at is never written down. The one thing filed
  beside them is the COUNTRY (an ISO code in the row, its name in
  `pwa/src/game/strings-countries.ts`), which the start card groups the
  faces by — never anything finer.
- **NOTHING IS HAND-EDITED.** Every file under `real-faces/` and
  `real-hints/` and both indexes are generated by `--write`; a fix is a fix
  to the script and a re-bake. `--write` writes only the faces baked
  (`--only`) and keeps every other face's file byte-identical, so a new
  face never moves one already shipped — and the caches of the old ones
  need not be in hand.
- **THE LICENCES TRAVEL WITH THE DATA.** The heights carry the elevation
  model's notice, the hints the ODbL notice — both in the generated files'
  headers and `docs/real-faces.md`. Nothing fetched (`previews/.dem/`,
  `previews/.osm/`) is committed.
- **THE SIZE BUDGET.** About 21 KB of heights and ~7 KB of hints a face,
  fetched only when the face is picked; the bundle carries ~150 bytes of
  index a face. The faces stop at ONE HUNDRED (about 3 MB in the tree):
  past that the start card's rows stop being a pick and the tree a place
  to read. If a new face's hints are far over the others', it is a crop
  holding a town — check the houses' count first (`real-houses.ts` stands
  at most 120 anyway).

## The quality bar

A face is DONE when all of the following hold:

- **It is a skiing face.** The bake's table prints its `drop` (ridge row
  over the valley floor) and `falls` (the share of the playable face that
  falls down the map): the drop well over 250 m (`tests/real_faces_test.ts`
  holds it), the fall share near the other faces'. A crop that faces
  sideways reads as a traverse, never a mountain.
- **It builds, and builds quickly.** `make resort SEED=1 ARGS="--face=<id>
  --hints"` and a handful of other seeds: every one builds, in a couple of
  attempts (the twenty took 1.45 on average and ~8 s a face when this skill
  was written). A face that needs eight attempts is losing its hints
  (`real.hinted`) — find which rule refuses it (below), never widen a rule
  for every map.
- **The ski area reads like the real one.** In the resort lab's picture the
  generator's lifts sit on the magenta real lifts, its runs (thick) lie over
  the real pistes (thin, in their signed colour) and the houses stand where
  the grey ticks are. Measure it, do not eyeball it (below).
- **It skis.** `make build`, then a free ride on it looked at by hand
  (`?start=free&face=<id>`): the lift ridden up, a run skied down. The
  world and screenshot labs take no face yet — teaching them one is a
  `lab-tooling` change worth making the first time a face needs it.

## The loop

### 1. The crop

Add the row to `FACES` in `scripts/lib/real-face-crops.mjs`: the next free
`<region>-<n>` id, the region (R21 — pick the kind of country the mountain
IS, `add-region` describes each), its `country` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, named
in `strings-countries.ts`), the middle it is searched round (`lat`, `lon`
in degrees, the skiing face's middle — the lifts' middle, read off the map
data), and the crop: `east` / `north` (m, the window's middle off that
point), `bearing` (° clockwise from north the fall line runs down the map
on) and `scale` (real metres a map metre; 0.8–1.2 — under 1 the real face
is stretched, which steepens it). Keep a new face's middle 4 km or more
from every other's, or it is the same mountain twice.

Pick the bearing so the summit ridge is the map's TOP edge (z = 0) and the
valley floor its bottom, the lifts' bottoms near the bottom row: the
generator's village and lifts leave from the valley floor.
`make real-faces ARGS="--fetch --search --only <id>"` tries every bearing
(10° apart), offset (450 m apart, the middle kept well inside the window)
and scale (0.9, 1, 1.1) round the middle and prints the best four rows by
`drop` and `falls`; take the best whose window keeps the ski area on the
map (step 3's lifts and pistes say whether it does).

### 2. The heights

`make real-faces ARGS="--fetch --only <id>"` (fetches the tiles it lies in
into `previews/.dem/`, once) and read the table; iterate step 1 until it
reads like a mountain. Then `make real-faces ARGS="--only <id> --write"`
(the faces named, and the index; every other face's file is kept). Check the new face's grid decodes
(`npx vitest run tests/real_faces_test.ts`) and look at it: `make level
ARGS=--face=<id>` and `make resort ARGS=--face=<id>`.

### 3. The hints

`make real-hints ARGS="--fetch --only <id>"` (its heights must be written
first: the hints are laid on them; the map data into
`previews/.osm/`, a few tiles a face, politely — the editing API is shared;
never refetch what is kept), read its table (lifts, pistes, how many have
widths, houses, bytes) — a good face has three lifts and five pistes on the map or
more — then `make real-hints ARGS="--only <id> --write"`. What is kept
is `real-hints.ts`'s `RealHints`: every lift's two ends and kind, every
downhill piste's signed colour (orange for a freeride or extreme one, which
is kept but never laid — the game's own ski routes, R42, are laid on every
face whether the real area marks one or not), its line thinned to bends,
and its width at each bend where the map draws the piste's area; every
building's middle, size and bearing.

### 4. The suite

`tests/real_faces_test.ts` counts the faces (`REAL_FACE_IDS` has the new
length), holds every face to a mountain and its hints to the map, every
country to a name and the GRADE row's filter to the grades; move its
count, never loosen a bound. Then:
`npx vitest run tests/real_faces_test.ts tests/real_houses_test.ts
tests/resort_test.ts tests/imports_test.ts tests/file_size_test.ts`.

### 5. Measure it, before and after

The numbers a PR owes, over the new face (and all faces when the change
touches how hints are used), on seed 1 and a few more:

- **attempts** and **seconds** to build (`generateLevel(seed, { face })`'s
  `level.attempt`, timed);
- **near**: the share of the generator's run points within ~40 m of a real
  piste (`resort.runs[].points` against `realHints(id).pistes`);
- **colour**: the share of runs laid on a real piste billed its signed
  colour;
- **refusals**: why attempts were refused. Set the framework's output sink
  with debug on (`@niclaslindstedt/oss-game-framework/core/output`) in a
  throwaway Node script under `scripts/` (run with `node
  --experimental-strip-types`, listed in `.git/info/exclude`, deleted when
  done) and count the `refused` lines by reason.

Keep the before table, change one thing, re-measure. A rule that refuses
the real mountain over and over (a real face's pads are rougher, it has
fewer pistes than a dealt one, a real piste is signed by more than its
steepest pitch) is relaxed for faces ONLY, in `RR.massif.real.least`, with
the analyzer reading `level.face` and the generator `face` — never for a
dealt map.

### 6. The docs and the changeset

`docs/real-faces.md` — the face count by region, the bake's sizes, any rule
relaxed and the measured numbers. A changeset fragment (`Added` for a new
face). The README and `AGENTS.md` only if a count they state moved.

## Where things live

| What | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The crops (every face's place, bearing, scale) | `scripts/lib/real-face-crops.mjs` |
| The heights' bake → the generated heights | `scripts/real-faces.mjs` (and `scripts/lib/real-face-files.mjs`, the writer both bakes share) → `engine/mapgen/real-faces/face-<id>.ts`, `real-faces-index.ts` |
| A face loaded, decoded and sampled | `engine/mapgen/real-face.ts` (`REAL_FACE_IDS`, `loadRealFace`, `realFace`, `realFaceCountry`, `realFaceGrades`, `faceHeight`) |
| The relief laid onto the massif | `engine/mapgen/massif.ts` (`readFace`, `faceLift`) |
| The hints' bake → the generated hints | `scripts/real-hints.mjs` → `engine/mapgen/real-hints/hints-<id>.ts`, `real-hints-index.ts` |
| The hints read (lifts leant, a run laid on a piste, its width) | `engine/mapgen/real-hints.ts` (`leanLift`, `leanStation`, `pisteFor`, `alongPiste`, `pisteAhead`, `pisteVia`, `pisteWidth`) |
| Where the plan uses them | `engine/mapgen/resort.ts` (`planResort`), `network.ts` (`RunSpec.widthAt`, `.signed`), `resort-build.ts` (the signed colour, the fewest runs) |
| The rules a face is held to | `engine/mapgen/resort-rules.ts`'s `RR.massif.real` (`.least` the relaxed ones), read by `engine/analysis/resort.ts` |
| The real houses stood | `engine/game/real-houses.ts` |
| Where a face is fetched | `loadRealFace`, awaited by the map and preview workers, the load (`app-load.ts`'s `LoadPlan.face`), the boot (`main.tsx`) and every lab that takes `--face` |
| The picker | the start card's SHAPE row (the countries) and PEAK row (the country's faces), filtered by GRADE (`pwa/src/game/face-picks.ts` — nothing to add but the country's name), `?face=` |

title: An easier take-off, wings that take a scrape and tear off, and a wreck that burns only when its fuel meets the hot engine
---

The jump plane lifts off when the throttle is opened and the stick pulled: a take-off hand keeps the wings level and the nose on the line through the roll. A wingtip touching the snow is now a scrape the plane flies on through. A tip dug in hard, a tree, or a load past what the wing can carry tears the wing off. The plane then flies on with one wing, and the torn wing falls and lies on the snow. A crash no longer always explodes. The wreck burns only when a wing tank is torn open onto the hot engine at speed, as most real crashes do not burn.

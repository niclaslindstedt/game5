Falls from a height are deadly now, the way real ones are: a steep face only softens a big drop when its snow is loose, a fall onto the flat groomer from 12 m or more kills more often than not, the hardest falls crush the chest, tear the aorta, shatter the liver or spleen or crush the pelvis, and many injuries together can kill even when none of them would alone. A steep powder landing is still ridden away. `make falls` charts what a drop does from every height onto every snow.

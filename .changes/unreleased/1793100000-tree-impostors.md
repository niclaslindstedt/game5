The far woods are drawn as pictures of each tree baked from every direction above it, relit by the sun every frame: past the near trees every tree is a single quad, the whole far forest one draw, and from a chair or a helicopter a far tree is still the tree from there. Their shadows come from the same pictures, the near trees sway in the wind, and the forest costs a fraction of the triangles and draw calls it did.

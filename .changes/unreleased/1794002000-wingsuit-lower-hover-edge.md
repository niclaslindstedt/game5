---
type: Changed
title: The wingsuit's helicopter hovers lower, and the map's edge always turns the suit back
---

The helicopter you step off in the wingsuit now hovers about 350 m over the snow instead of about 650 m, so the flight is down the face rather than high above it. Flying the suit straight at the edge of the map, or low over the snow near it, now always turns you back in on the wind; before, a flight aimed dead at the edge or skimming the snow could carry on off the map. The bot flying the suit also heads for the piste before it pulls, so its canopy lands on open snow more often.

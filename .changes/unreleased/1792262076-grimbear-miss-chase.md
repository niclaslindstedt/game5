---
type: Changed
title: The grimbear's missed grab shoves you and he keeps chasing
---

When the grimbear misses his grab, his paw now glances off you: you are shoved aside but stay on your skis. He no longer gives up and turns back. He picks himself up and runs after you a few strides behind until he tires and stops, roaring. His charge is also better timed: he breaks out of the trees earlier and runs at a little more than your own pace instead of bursting out at a sprint.

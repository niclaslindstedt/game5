title: The helicopter crash, reworked
---

A helicopter flown into the snow now crashes the way real ones do. The cabin stays where it struck and rolls onto its side as a charred, buckled hulk with its skids splayed. The tail boom snaps off and the blades are slung far away. The fuel goes up in a turbulent fireball of glowing soot that turns black at its edges, rolls up into a column of thick black smoke lit orange from below, and leaves the hulk burning. The camera cuts on the impact to a shot planted well back from the wreck, clear of the trees, and holds on the fire as it burns. It no longer follows the skier thrown out of it.

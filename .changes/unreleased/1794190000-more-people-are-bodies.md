title: Dog walkers, the ski patrol and everyone hit by a machine are real bodies
---

The village's dog walkers, and the children out with them, now count as real bodies when something hits them, and so do the ski patrollers who come up on snowmobiles. A person hit by your own body as you tumble, by a piste machine or by a car, the bus or a bicycle in the village is now a real collision too, at that thing's full weight. With INJURIES on they can be hurt or killed, they lie broken on the snow, and the rescue and the police come to collect them. With INJURIES off they get knocked down and get up again. Your own body is only hurt when you are the one who hits them. Someone hit by a machine you are not driving is heard from where it happens, and you do not feel it.

type: Fixed
title: Chairs come into the top from above, stay in sight, and free-ride knees no longer spin you
---

On the free ride the lift's rope now comes down into each top station from a tall last tower instead of dragging the chair up along the snow, and the towers are taller and thicker. The chair you step off keeps running on over the ramp to the wheel instead of vanishing, and it knocks you down if you stop in its way. The run signs at a lift's top now stand beside the piste map board, on the side each run lies, facing you as you come off the lift, and each run's own sign stands where its ramp meets the slope, turned to you as you arrive. Steering or leaning in the air after an unplanned launch off a knee or roller no longer throws a spin or a flip on the free ride; a trick is only thrown off a kicker or out of your own jump. The ride up now starts eight seconds from the top so you see the approach.

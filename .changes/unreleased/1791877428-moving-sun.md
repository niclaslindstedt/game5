**The sun moves as you ride.** Every run starts at its map's hour and the sky runs on from there at twelve times real time — five minutes on the snow are an hour of the sun's day. On a long free ride the sun sinks, the light warms and fades, the lamps and floodlights come on, the stars and the moon come out, and the mountain's own shadows glide across the face with the sun instead of standing still. A link that names an hour holds the sun there; `?skyclock=` sets the clock's speed (`0` holds it).

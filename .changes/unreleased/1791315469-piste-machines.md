title: Piste machines work the runs on a free ride after dark
---

After dark a free ride has the ski area's night shift out on it: up to two big tracked snow groomers working their runs lane by lane, their roof bars of LED work lamps and an amber beacon lighting up the snow, the woods and the falling snow. Behind them lies fresh corduroy, firm under the skis even while new snow falls on the rest of the run; ahead of them, the day's skied-up piste. Stop beside a cab and press Enter (a double tap on touch) to climb in and drive one up the run or down it, laying corduroy wherever it goes forward; Enter again climbs out. Ski into one and you are knocked flat by twelve tonnes of steel, and the body plate shows the damage. `?start=free&groomer=1` puts them out whatever the hour, and `0` keeps them in.

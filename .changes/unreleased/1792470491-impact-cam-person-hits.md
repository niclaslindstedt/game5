---
type: Changed
title: The impact camera cuts to a person you run into
---

With INJURIES on and the X-ray cam off, skiing hard into a person now gets the same slow-motion side shot as hitting a tree: an amateur on the slopes, a civilian, a rescue or police crew member or a soldier. The shot comes when the collision will kill or injure them, or when you hit them at 45 km/h or more. The camera stands a little wider and a little ahead, so both you and the person thrown down the slope are in the shot. Replays play it too.

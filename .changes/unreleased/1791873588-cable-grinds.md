title: Grind the lift cables, and score tricks on a free ride
---

Grind the lift cables on a free ride: land on a lift's rope (or ollie up onto a low one) and you hook onto it, slide it, swap into a slide, press, pop off to spin or flip and land back on it — and a chair coming along the rope knocks you off into the snow. Every grind, spin, flip and grab now scores on a free ride too, with the score and combo on the HUD.

title: The wingsuit flown the way its footage is shot
---

Every camera in the wingsuit now looks down along your line, so the mountain fills the frame from hundreds of metres up, where it used to show only haze. The higher you fly, the steeper the look. The chase flyer sits behind and over you, and the side flyer off your wingtip. Your own eyes look ahead and down between the arm wings. Changes of camera are flown round you, never cut. On the helicopter's skid the cameras stand out from its side and clear of the rotor. The suit's BASE container is slim and soft, in the suit's colours, with its flap, bridle and pouch. The canopy's slider is a light mesh. The head is held up with the helmet showing. The HUD's big number is labelled ABOVE SNOW.

A ski area's village now stands along STREETS below the base: a main street, a back street, the cross streets between them, a road out of the valley and a day car park, with two lanes of white, gritted packed snow and their wheel ruts, raised sidewalks of setts under snow, the plough's windrows broken at every junction, crossing and driveway, a bus stop, crossings, snow poles along the road and street lamps lit after dark. The village's buildings — the base's own, and now houses, apartments, shops and a church — front the streets, and the streets show on the minimap and the start card's chart.

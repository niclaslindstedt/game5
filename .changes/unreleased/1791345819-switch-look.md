title: Riding switch looks back
---

A skier riding switch now turns to look back over a shoulder to see where he is going: the head round past square with the chin over the shoulder, the trunk and hips turned with it, a little lower on his legs, the hands out wide and the poles trailing toward his tips. He picks a shoulder as he turns round and keeps it rather than flicking his head from side to side. The minimap now turns with the way he goes, so down the hill stays up the plate while he rides tails first.

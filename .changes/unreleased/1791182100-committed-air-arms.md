title: A skier leaning in the air is committed, and the windmill circles in front of him with his poles trailing back
---

Holding a lean in the air now reads as a skier committed to his line: the arms set forward and still, and he never windmills while he leans — circling arms are kept for a skier dropping a cliff without a lean, fighting a rotation he did not ask for. When he does windmill, his fists circle in front of his shoulders instead of swinging round behind him, and his poles stay pointed back, out and down the whole time, the way a hand can actually hold them, instead of sticking out of his fists or pointing forward.

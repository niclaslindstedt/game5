title: Organs in the body panel
---

The body panel at the HUD's left edge now shows the organs inside the bones that hold them: the brain under a lifted skull cap, the heart and the lungs behind the ribs, the liver, the spleen, the stomach, the bowel, the kidneys and the bladder, traced from the same open 3D body as the skeleton. Each sits in a soft colour of its own while sound and turns yellow, orange or red when it is hurt, and a hurt organ hidden behind another is dashed in over it. The injury model gains organ injuries researched from ski falls and falls from a height: a bruised lung, a bruised heart, a bleed in the skull, a ruptured liver, a bruised bowel and a torn bladder from blows, and the lungs, the liver and the spleen torn by the deceleration of a hard landing. Each injury names the organ it hurts, a lung or a kidney on the side the blow came from.

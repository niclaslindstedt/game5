title: Avalanches as drawn
---

A slab avalanche on a free ride is now seen as well as felt. The crown cracks across the slope with a puff of dust, and the slab breaks into blocks that open along their cracks, tip and tumble. Down the track the blocks round off into snowballs, and a powder cloud boils off the front and hangs on the wind after it stops. The slab leaves its crown wall, its ragged flanks and a bare, greyer bed behind it. Where the flow stops it lays a lumpy grey-white tongue of debris that stays for the rest of the ride and is lit by the headlamp at night. The ground shakes under the camera as the flow runs near, and the release and being caught are felt in the hands. The free ride's start card has a new AVALANCHES row (on by default) to turn them off, and `make avalanche` photographs a slide sheet by sheet.

title: The camera rides the snowmobile instead of sitting inside it
---

On the snowmobile the tips camera no longer sits inside the hood: every camera is now framed off the machine itself. Tips is a low bumper view ahead of the nose, helmet is the rider's eye looking down over the bars and the hood, and the chase, far and high cameras stand behind the whole machine. The camera flies across as you get on and off, and a ride begun on the snowmobile starts on its own camera.

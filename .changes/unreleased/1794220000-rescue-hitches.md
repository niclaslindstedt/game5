type: Fixed
title: No more freezes when the rescue comes
---

The game no longer freezes for seconds when a rescue helicopter or the ski patrol comes to a fallen skier, when the police arrive after a death and start taking photographs, when you ski up to a rescue in progress, or when you take the police helicopter and fly it away. Everything these scenes draw is now prepared on the loading card, and a camera flash or an explosion no longer forces the whole picture to be rebuilt.

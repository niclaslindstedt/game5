title: Snowboard grabs, frontside and backside spins, nollies and presses
---

On a snowboard the trick button now grabs the board: an indy with nothing else held, and a mute, melon, stalefish, method, nose grab or tail grab picked with the lean and the edge. Each is drawn with the hand on the right edge of the board, the knees drawn up and the board tweaked (the method's kicked up behind). Spins on a board are called frontside or backside by the way the rider faces, switch when taken off riding backward, and nollie when popped off the nose, so the combo reads "SWITCH BACKSIDE 360 + INDY". A spin winds up before the pop and is led by the head and shoulders in the air, and leaning hard on the snow presses the board onto its nose or tail. Skiers' tricks are unchanged.

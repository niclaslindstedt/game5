title: A parachute out of the paramotor, the helicopter and the balloon
---

The skydiver's harness-container is now worn on the paramotor, on the helicopter's skid and in the balloon's basket. Drop the paramotor's rig, push off the skid or go over the basket's side 100 m or more above the snow and you fall into the same skydive the jump plane gives: open the main, cut it away and pull the reserve, land on your skis. A restart puts you back in the craft you left. The paramotor's motor now falls away on its own when you drop the rig, instead of falling beside you as if it were still on your back.

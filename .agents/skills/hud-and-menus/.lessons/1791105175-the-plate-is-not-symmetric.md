Four traps in the plate behind `make anatomy`, each found by a bone crossing or leaving the flesh:

- **The right arm is pronated.** The thumb turns in and the radius crosses the ulna. The figure stands palms forward, so its right humerus, forearm and hand are the plate's LEFT, mirrored.
- **One fused shape for two metacarpals.** The ring and little metacarpals are drawn as one, about twice a single one's area. Laid on one digit, it sticks out of the palm; lay it between the two.
- **A finger bends at its knuckle.** One straight base-to-tip map per ray crosses the rays. Map the metacarpal base to knuckle and the phalanges knuckle to tip; the knuckle stands behind the web between the outline's fingers.
- **The feet are splayed and seen end on.** One ankle-to-toe line squeezes them into a strip. Lay the tarsus from under the ankle to the foot's two sides, and the rays from their bases to the big and little toes' tips.

The proportions table showing the forearm and shank ~25 % short is the photograph's foreshortening (arms and feet toward the camera), not a landmark error — check the gridded photo before moving a joint off the trace to chase it.

title: Snowboarders in the free ride's crowd
---

About three in ten of the people on a free ride's slopes now ride snowboards, regular or goofy. Nearly all the park regulars ride one, and a ski school rides whatever its instructor does. They stand side-on and lean into toeside and heelside turns and carves. They stop with a heelside skid, push along on one foot on the flat, stop off a lift's top to strap in, ride the chair with the board hanging off the front foot, and sometimes sit down in the snow while they wait. When one falls, the board stays on his feet. After dark, about three in ten of the keen riders out on the lit runs are on a snowboard too.

On a free ride every building's door opens: stop in front of one and press Enter (or tap the HUD's DOOR call) and the skier shuffles to the handle, presses the lever and pushes the door open — or draws it toward him and steps back round it — and steps through, the door's closer swinging it shut behind him with a creak, a thud and the latch. Each kind of building hangs its own leaf (plank, glazed, steel or a roller door) with its own sound, and it works from inside too.

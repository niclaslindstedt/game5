title: Striding, skating, double-poling and the jump
---

Below about 40 km/h on a straight the skier works for his speed on his own, to gain it and to keep it: off a standstill and up a rise he strides, the skis parallel and each leg kicking back as the opposite arm plants; rolling he skates, the skis opened into a V and a leg pushed off each stride with the poles planted; and faster he double-poles, folded over both poles — a man's push, strong off a standstill and fading as he gathers speed. Hold the tuck to set off from a standstill. SPACE loads a jump: the skier sinks onto his legs the longer it is held, and springs higher the longer it was loaded, up to two seconds' worth (on the phone, tap the tuck lever and put the thumb straight back down, then lift).

title: The ski patrol comes for skiers who fall where no helicopter can land
---

When a skier in the free ride's crowd falls and stays down somewhere no helicopter can land, such as a steep face or the woods, the ski patrol now drives up on two snowmobiles and tows them down in a toboggan. This works with INJURIES off too, and the patrol's snowmobiles can be stolen while the crew is busy.

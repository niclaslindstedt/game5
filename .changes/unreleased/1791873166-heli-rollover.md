**A helicopter turned over no longer powers itself into the ground.** Rolled upside down, the rotor used to read the fall as a descent and pushed harder the faster it fell, driving the machine down past 80 m/s. The blades now lose their lift once the air meets them from the top, so an inverted helicopter is pushed down for a moment and then falls on its own drag. A body thrown through the air, off the helicopter or anything else, is now slowed by the air too, instead of flying on at the speed it left with.

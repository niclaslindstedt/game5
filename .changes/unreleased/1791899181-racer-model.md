A modelled World Cup RACER on the rider card, in a national team's white-to-azure speed suit: a halftone print fading down the arms and legs, a front zip, a red number bib, navy shin, arm and hand guards and a chin-guard helmet with a tricolour ribbon over its crown, made in Blender on the skier's own skeleton so every move drives him. His bib streams and flaps in the air and swings with what he does — slapped onto his chest on a landing, thrown out round a turn — and the slack of his suit (the elbows, the knees, the small of the back) shivers on its lee side, all on the run's own clock so a slow replay slows it. Picked on the rider card's BODY row; a new player still starts on the dressed skier.

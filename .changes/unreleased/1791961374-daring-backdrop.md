The run behind the menu's cards is a show now: a daring rider takes the mountain you left, off the piste and into the trees, lining up the kickers and the drops, and flipping, spinning and grabbing in the air, then starts again from the top each time he is down — every lap ridden a different way. `make daring` measures it against the plain rider, and `make world ARGS=--daring` photographs his flights.

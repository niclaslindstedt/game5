title: A fall you survive stays with you, and you ski hurt
---

With INJURIES on, a fall that does not kill you no longer heals you. When you are stood back up you keep every injury you took, back on the piste where you left it and facing down it, and you ski worse for them. A hurt leg stands the skis over less and tips them in slower in the turns it carries (a bad left knee costs your right turns) and its edge holds less. A head or neck injury slows how quickly you turn in. Hurt arms weaken the push with your poles and the skate. Hurt legs and trunk keep you out of a full tuck and take less of a landing. The worse the injury, the more it costs. The injuries add up, so enough falls will kill you, and a death starts the run again from the top, healed.

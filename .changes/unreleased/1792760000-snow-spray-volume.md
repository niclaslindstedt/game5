The snow a turn throws reads as snow in the air. A hockey stop's sheet and a board's heelside spray are now a spray of grains with broken, lumpy clumps among them instead of a pile of soft white balls, and against a low sun the whole sheet lights up silver and gold, glinting as the crystals turn. The fine powder cloud behind a turn or a stop is a torn, lobed veil rather than round puffs, glows round the sun when it is backlit and stays a soft lit white in its shade.

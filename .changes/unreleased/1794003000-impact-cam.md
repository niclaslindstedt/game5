title: A slow-motion side camera for a skier crushed or sliced, with the X-ray cam off
---

With INJURIES on and the X-RAY CAM switch off, a skier about to be crushed on a trunk, a lamp's pole or a lift's tower, or sliced by a lift's rope, is now seen coming: just before he meets it the picture cuts to a camera already standing side on beside the place, the run slows almost to a stop as he arrives, the strike is held in slow motion, and the lens then draws back to the normal camera. A tap or a mash of the skier's keys skips it, as with the X-ray cam. Watching the run back as a replay plays the same cut. With the injuries off (safe for work) there is no such camera.

type: Fixed
title: No stall when the helicopter spools up
---

Sitting on the helicopter's skid no longer freezes the picture for a moment as the rotor spools up, and the first sight of anything new on a ride (the far side of the ski area as the machine climbs, a fire, a finish arena) no longer stalls a frame: every shader the run can need is now built and drawn once behind the loading card.

type: Changed
title: Log cabins in painted materials, at about half the triangles
---

The log cabins, chalets, woodsheds and afterski lodges are drawn in the same painted materials as the lift stations: the logs keep their shape, but their bark and the rings on their sawn ends are painted, and the windows, doors, shutters, stone plinths and chimneys, firewood stacks, balcony boards, render and corner stones are flat faces carrying their material rather than a stack of little boxes. Close up they read as before, with more grain; each building takes 40–80 % fewer triangles (a hut 2,247 → 1,346, a chalet 3,994 → 2,134, a lodge 7,078 → 4,318). The distant version now shows the log courses, stone and windows instead of bands of colour, so buildings switch to it nearer (130 m instead of 190 m) without a visible change. At night only the glass of a window glows, not its frame.

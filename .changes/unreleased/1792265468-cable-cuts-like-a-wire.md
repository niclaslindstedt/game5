type: Added
title: A lift's cable met too fast slices the skier and his skis like a wire
---

With injuries on, a skier who hits a lift's cable faster than about 72 km/h is cut by it rather than stopped or thrown. The cable can go through him at any height, taking his head, an arm, a forearm, a leg, a shin, or the whole lower body at the waist. Where it crosses his skis it cuts them in two, and the cut-off pieces spring away. Nothing he hits is slowed: what is left of him carries on at the speed he was going. Slower hits still behave as before, and with injuries off nothing is cut.

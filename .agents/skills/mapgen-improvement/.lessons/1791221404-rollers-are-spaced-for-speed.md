Rollers 10–14 m crest to crest and up to a metre high leave the snow at about 65 km/h (the crest's curvature asks more than the snow's pull), and the racer comes down on the up-face of the next one — a harsh landing the bot cannot pump away. Spaced 14–16 m (the long end of the measured 8–16 m) and 0.6–0.9 m high they ride clean on all but one of the nine race maps. A straight split by a small, unbanked turn is still one long run of speed: the feature after it is entered as fast as if the turn were not there, so "out of the turn" is not a speed guarantee unless the turn is a berm.

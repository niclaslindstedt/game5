type: Fixed
title: The paramotor's lean leans the pilot, and its lines no longer glow at night
---

Leaning forward or back under the paramotor now leans the pilot in the harness and tilts the motor's thrust with him, on top of the speed bar and the trimmers: reclined on the throttle he climbs harder, leant forward he drives faster. The wing's lines and the cage's net are now lit by the sky instead of drawn at full colour, so after dark they no longer glow yellow against a dark canopy.

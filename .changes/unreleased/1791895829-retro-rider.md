**A retro look for the rider.** The RIDER card has a new LOOK row. Next to the modern kit it offers an eighties one-piece in three colourways: day-glo pink, canary yellow and cobalt blue. The suit has a shoulder yoke, a slanted chest band, sleeve rings, a belt and leg stripes. He wears a striped knit bobble hat, mirrored wraparound shades and a long knit scarf. The scarf's two tails and the bobble swing in the wind as he skis. They hang when he stands, stream out behind him at speed and flap on a landing. The rider is a model made in Blender, and every move the game has drives it.

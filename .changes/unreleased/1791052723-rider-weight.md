title: Choose how much the skier weighs
---

The DRESS card has a WEIGHT row: LIGHT, MEDIUM, SOLID or HEAVY. It is not only a look — the skier is cut to it — slighter when light, broader through the chest when solid, with a stomach when heavy — and the physics skis him at that weight. A heavier skier runs faster down a pitch in a tuck (about 126 km/h heavy against 117 medium and 111 light on the all-mountain pair), skates and poles up to speed slower on the flat, lands a jump harder and folds sooner off a big drop, and shoulders a rival or an amateur harder while being knocked about less himself; he also sinks deeper in powder. The ski card's top speed reads the skier's own build, and each build keeps its own record book and ghost, so the medium skier's existing records stand.

type: Fixed
title: The camera gives a tree a metre, not a swing
---

Riding past a tree, the chase camera no longer swings its whole arm round the skier to keep its view clear of the boughs. It now keeps about a metre of free space round the trunk itself: nothing happens until the lens comes close, then it is eased out sideways, like a magnet pushed off its like pole. A skier who only just makes it past a tree sees the camera come by after him and sway out a little.

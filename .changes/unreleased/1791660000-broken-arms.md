title: A broken arm drops its pole and hangs from the break
---

With INJURIES on, a skier who gets up with an arm broken through rides without that arm's pole: one broken arm leaves him with the other pole, two leave him with none. The broken arm hangs from where it broke. A broken upper arm is held out a little at the shoulder and everything below the break swings loose. A broken forearm is held guarded against his side with the hand end hanging off it. A broken wrist leaves the hand hanging. The hanging part swings with the skier, forward as he brakes and out through a turn, then settles.

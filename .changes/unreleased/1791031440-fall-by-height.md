title: The skier rides a fall by how far it is — secure off a kicker, spotting a drop, windmilling a cliff
---

In the air the skier now rides the fall in front of him the way a professional does, and the longer he is high over the snow the further he goes through it. Off a kicker on the piste he stays compact and quiet with his hands forward. Off a drop he opens out to spot the landing: hands forward and wide, chest over his knees, head down on the snow he is aiming for. Off a big cliff he winds his arms into slow, steady forward circles to keep his tips from dropping. Before every landing he finishes the circle, brings his arms forward and reaches his legs down to take the snow, further the bigger the fall. He never flails, and he doesn't windmill while spinning or flipping on purpose.

The sky on the DISTANCE row's MEDIUM and up is now rendered on the graphics card from the same physical atmosphere the LOW sky is baked from: a finer sky with a sharper glow round a low sun, Earth's shadow and the Belt of Venus, and on HIGH and MAX aerial perspective — the air in a ridge's shadow bluer and darker than the sunlit air beyond, out to a hundred kilometres. LOW, and a machine that cannot render to float targets, keep the baked sky.

title: An anatomy plate for the body, with every bone
---

The body at the left edge of the HUD is now a classic anatomy plate seen from the front: a traced human outline with every part painted green, yellow, orange or red as it is hurt, and over it the **skeleton**, traced off a front-view skeleton plate. That's thirty bones, from the skull and jaw down the spine and ribs to the hands and feet. A whole bone is ivory, one with a **hairline fracture** turns yellow with a crack across it, and a **broken** one turns red with a gap through it. Every bone can now crack as well as break. The lines under the figure say only what the bones can't show — the organs, the torn ligaments, the sprains. The g meter shows just the number now, and injuries no longer fill the news column. A reset now stands you back up **healed**.

title: Log cabins along the runs and lanes
---

Log cabins now stand here and there beside the runs and, a little more often, beside the transport lanes: one-room huts, storey-and-a-half cabins with a porch across the front, stone-footed chalets with a balcony, and woodsheds stacked with firewood. They are built the way a mountain log cabin is built, with saddle-notched corners and the log ends run on past them, chinking between the courses, a stone plinth terraced into the slope, a steep roof under a blanket of snow, a stone chimney and small shuttered windows that are lit after dark. They stand in clearings off the snow, clear of the lifts, the courses and the trees. They are solid, so a skier who runs into one is stopped as he would be by a trunk, and they are drawn on the minimap.

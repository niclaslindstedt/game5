title: The stolen truck's cab and the tank's driver's and gunner's stations
---

A stolen army truck now has its own cab. From the driver's eye you sit high over the front wheels behind a split windscreen. The cluster has live dials, including the brakes' air gauge. The tyre inflation panel switches to SNOW on the snow and to STUCK when the wheels spin. The cab also has the differential locks, the radio, the gear selector and the transfer lever, and your hands turn the wheel.

In a stolen tank, the driver's eye is now hatch down. You look out through the vision blocks, steer with the T-bar and watch a green driver's display. The tips camera puts you in the gunner's seat in the turret, beside the breech, the sight and the control handles. Hold the trick key to lay the gun and you look through the sight: a narrow view along the barrel under a reticle, with the range to where the shell will land. The view holds on the target for a moment after you fire.

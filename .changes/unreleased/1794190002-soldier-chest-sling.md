---
type: Changed
title: Soldiers carry their rifles on a chest sling
---

Soldiers no longer carry their rifles sticking up off the back of the pack. While a soldier skis, skins, rides a chair or is on a rope, his rifle hangs low across his chest with the muzzle pointing down, clear of his poles. When he crouches or plants his poles it swings out past his left knee, and when he sits on a chair it lies along his thigh. When he aims or fires, the rifle is in his hands as before.

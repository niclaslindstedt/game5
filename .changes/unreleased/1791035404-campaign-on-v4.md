type: Changed
title: The campaign is re-cut on the new ski areas
---

Every campaign map now stands on the ski areas whose lift stations sit beside the runs, with a level pad at every top. The four shelves keep their names but each is a new mountain: the Rime Woods a maritime area opening on a blue in the fog, the High Cirque an alpine one with its first two blacks, the Frost Basin a continental basin of three reds and three blacks, and the Cold Crest two reds among the four hardest blacks in the game. Each of the twenty-four maps is ridden on its own day, with medals set against the new runs. Because the maps behind every rung have changed, the campaign board starts fresh, including a board carried in from another device.

Skiers gather round the fires on a free ride: two to five at a hut's camp fire and four to ten round a bonfire, sat on the log benches or stood facing the flames — grilling sausages on long sticks over the embers, eating them in bread, warming their hands and chatting, their skis and poles stuck upright in the snow behind them. The camp fires are busiest round lunch and the bonfires toward evening.

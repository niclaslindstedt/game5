A second downhill bike can be drawn on a summer free ride with the link `?bikelook=worldcup`: a modern race bike with a white carbon frame, a dual-crown fork, a coil shock worked by a four-bar linkage, 29-inch wheels with spokes and disc rotors, a chain that runs round its ring and cassette, and a number plate. Its rider wears a racer's kit: a loose jersey and riding pants in the team's colours, a full-face helmet with a peak, goggles, a neck brace and flat-pedal shoes. He stands out of the saddle and sways when he sprints, and pushes his hips back when he brakes.

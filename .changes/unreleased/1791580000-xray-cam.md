title: X-ray cam on a fatal blow
---

With OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ INJURIES on, a fall the skier will die of is now seen coming: a second before the hit the run starts slowing almost to a stop and the lens closes in on the part of him the blow lands on, the skier drawn as he is for the first half second and his skin dissolving into glass over his skeleton and organs in the last, and the bone cracks, breaks or shatters in front of you while the blood from the torn vessels pools round it and fills the body's cavities. Every other big bone that goes is a shot of its own, the lens panning to it, and so is a limb torn off. Then the lens draws back out to the game's own camera, the skin growing back over the bones until he is solid, and the run speeds back up. The body plate on the HUD fades out while the X-ray has him, and the death cam now takes the lens only for a skier who is dying. The skeleton and organs are made from BodyParts3D (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan). A tap on the screen, or the skier's keys pressed again and again, skips the X-ray: the run goes on at its own pace and the body plate comes back; a restart skips it for a new run from the top.

type: Changed
title: The skier skis like a skier
---

Braking into a turn at speed no longer folds the skier's knees up past his hips: his legs lean with the skis' edge and turn with them as they skid across, and he rises out of his tuck to brake. In a carved turn his legs lean in hard while his upper body stays up over the outside ski. At speed he plants a pole as he starts each new turn. Changing edge no longer jolts his shoulders and head. Double-poling and skating, he crunches down onto his poles and rises tall into the next plant instead of standing stiff. The gallery's filmstrip loses its white scrollbar.

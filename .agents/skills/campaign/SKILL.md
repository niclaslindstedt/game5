# The campaign: the pinned maps, skied for points

The campaign is the sibling games' championship retyped for a mountain:
four SHELVES, each ONE SKI AREA (a seed's resort on the resort generator,
R25–R28) in a kind of country (R21) — RIME WOODS (maritime), HIGH CIRQUE
(alpine), FROST BASIN and COLD CREST (two continental massifs) — and three
to five MAPS a shelf (three, five, four and four: sixteen), each one of the
area's COURSES (R28, `CampaignLevel.course`), every one a race — a slalom,
or a downhill on a black. The whole mountain is skiable on every rung; the
points are paid down the one course raced. THE CAMPAIGN RACES RED AND
BLACK: greens and blues are the free ride's; the shelves blacken as they
climb (0, 2, 2, 2 blacks) and the blacks are ordered by the rating's index, so the early
shelves' blacks are the raceable ones and the last shelf's the brutal. The
FELL hosts no shelf — its areas offer no black and at most two reds. Every
map is a SEED on a generator VERSION, ridden on the day its course was
dealt (or a `sky` laid over it). A race rung is ridden against the
race's three rivals with contact off and pays the podium three, two and one
for the whole field. The next map opens behind a podium, the next shelf
behind the table. The rung ids are the ones the shelves had when they also
held time trials (dropped since), so a stored board still names the right
races and a dropped id is left lying. `campaign.ts`'s header says why each
of those is the shape it is.

A RACE off the race card does not ride these maps: every built discipline is raced
on its own NINE RACE MAPS (`race-maps.ts`, `docs/specs/RACE_MAPS.md`),
curated for the discipline the way a rung is curated here — the same row
shape, the same digest contract, the same `make routes` — and a campaign
rung that is a slalom or a downhill stays on its own shelf. A seed of one's
own is the free ride's, and a link's `?seed=` (`pinnedFor`).

**Read this skill's lessons first** — `npx ogf-skill-lessons
campaign --list`. Load **`skill-reflection`** at both ends, **`write-code`**
beside this one for any code change, **`level-rating`** whenever a map is
being chosen or judged, **`mapgen-improvement`** when a generator change is
what moved a map, and **`menu-system`** for anything about the cards
themselves.

## The curation loop — adding or moving a map

```
   1. make rate COUNT=96 ARGS=--sim                    the pool, the bot's run as the time axis
   2. shortlist on the AXES, not the index            level-rating's brief
   3. make difficulty SEED=<seed> ARGS=--sim          LOOK at it
   4. edit campaign-levels.ts: seed, mode, laps, name, blurb
   5. make rate CAMPAIGN=1           every rung asks more? no pair the same map twice?
      — it prints the DIGEST each map builds to and the bot's time over the
        rung's laps; write the digest and the day (`day`) into the row
   6. make routes                     the piste behind the box
   7. make difficulty CAMPAIGN=1      the sheets, in the PR
   8. npx vitest run tests/generator_version_test.ts tests/campaign_test.ts
```

**The day is the cheapest lever.** Every rung rides the day its seed dealt
(R15, R19) unless its row lays a `sky` over it; a weather and a start hour
cost nothing that has to be re-verified and carry a fifth of the index
(`RATING.weight`), so when two rungs sit level, try the day before the seed:
a seed change re-rolls the map, the sheet, the bot's time, the route and the
name. The box bills the day it is ridden in (`day`), and the version test
holds that to the built map, so a `sky` added to a row owes its `day` too.

**The ladder is read on the MEASURED run.** The rating's time axis prefers
the bot's run to the estimate off the piste's length, and the bot's pace
varies a lot from map to map — so a ladder that climbs under `make rate
CAMPAIGN=1` can step down under a bare `rateLevel`. The audit is the
measurement; the test suite does not ride the bot on every rung.

**A map is named for what it is like, never for where it is** — the snow,
the light, the shape of the ask; a ski area's name is invented and generic.
Each shelf is dealt its REGION (R21) on purpose — a shelf is a ski area in a
kind of country, a deliberate curation, never a side effect of `add-region`.
To find one: `make resort COUNT=40 REGION=…` lists every seed's courses by
colour; keep the areas with six red-and-black courses that stand clean and
read as a ski area on the plan, then ride every course with the bot and rate
it (`make rate CAMPAIGN=1` once pinned) and order the six by the index.

## The version contract, and the digest

Every map names the generator version it was curated under and carries the
digest of the map that came out. `tests/generator_version_test.ts` rebuilds
every one and compares. When it goes red there are exactly two cases, with
opposite fixes:

- **You moved the map** (a new seed, a deliberate move onto a newer
  version): run steps 5 and 6 above and write the new digest down. The
  picture was meant to change.
- **The rules moved under a map that did not**: NEVER regenerate the digest.
  Add a row to `GENERATOR_VERSIONS`, keep the old behaviour on the old row
  as an optional trait read through `generatorTraits(opts.version)` at the
  one place it differs, and leave the map where it stands — or move it onto
  the new version as a curation, one map at a time. Bumping every map
  because the suite went red is the implicit re-roll the scheme exists to
  prevent, and it puts every player's board and record book on water nobody
  rode.

And **delete a version nothing names any more**, row and trait branches
together — the test refuses a museum.

## What a change here obliges

- `make rate CAMPAIGN=1` and `make difficulty CAMPAIGN=1` in the PR, and
  `make routes` for any map that moved.
- `docs/getting-started.md`'s campaign paragraph; a renamed shelf is
  renamed there.
- A new word on the cards goes in `strings-campaign.ts`; a new mark in
  `menu-glyphs.tsx`.
- A changed board shape (`CampaignProgress`) needs a case in
  `tests/campaign_test.ts` for what an older blob does to it — the board is
  a player's save — and a re-cut shelf (ids are rung numbers) a new
  `PROGRESS_KEY`.
- Anything the player sees → a `.changes/unreleased/` fragment.

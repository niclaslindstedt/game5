title: The giant slalom
---

The race card's GIANT SLALOM box is open. A giant slalom is two runs against a field of thirty on the giant slalom ski, one racer on the course at a time, from a start lowered down the ski area's biggest hill until some 400 m of vertical is left. Its gates come every twenty-odd metres, panelled pairs in red and blue turn by turn, and swing the racing line into short carved turns at about seventy kilometres an hour, with the nets along both sides. Both feet must pass every gate: a gate missed is a disqualification and a fall a did-not-finish. The best thirty of the first run start the second in reverse order on a course set afresh, and the race is decided on combined time. It has nine maps of its own, all open, picked on its level card. `?start=gs` boots straight into one and `?run=2` into its second run.

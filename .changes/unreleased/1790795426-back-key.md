type: Changed
title: The back key brakes or cuts harder, by the order it is pressed
---

S pressed first is the brake — a snowplough, and a hockey stop that turns the skis across the slope when an edge goes on after it; S pressed with an edge already on cuts the edge harder instead: the skis stood further over, a tighter arc that costs almost no speed. On the phone, drag the edge thumb down for the same key. SPACE is no longer a second brake: it is the jump. A carve bleeds far less speed than it did, a hard landing costs less, and the chase camera sits lower and closer, so the game runs faster.

The skier moves more like a skier. His fists dip a hand's breadth when he lands and come back up. Riding switch, he keeps his poles carried through a turn round instead of letting them drop to hang and swinging them up again. He gives up double-poling at about 40 km/h, so at speed his arms no longer flail. After a landing, his skis come away from his body as fast as legs straighten rather than all at once. He opens out of a flip smoothly when the lean is let go.

The skier's gloves and ski boots are rebuilt: every glove now closes round the pole's grip with four fingers and a thumb, a leather palm, a knuckle band, a wrist strap and its own cuff (a gauntlet's flared rim and drawcord, a mitten's fleece rim, a short undercuff, a race glove's hard plates), and every pair's boots are real alpine boots — a lugged sole, a shell with its rubber band, a tilted cuff with its spoiler and tongue, four buckles on their ladders and a power strap, the spine in the pair's colour. The snowboarder's soft boots are rebuilt the same way: a rubber outsole with a midsole band and a toe bumper, a mudguard and heel counter over the padded upper, laces crossing up the front between their eyelets to the speed hooks, the tongue standing proud with its pull loop, a padded collar and a heel loop.

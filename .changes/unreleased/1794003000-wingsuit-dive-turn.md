type: Changed
title: The wingsuit dives straight down, turns tighter and starts lower
---

Leaning all the way forward in the wingsuit now takes you into a steep dive that goes all the way to straight down, head first, at up to about 375 km/h. Full edge turns at about 40° a second, more than twice as tight as before, with the bank rolled in faster. The helicopter you step off now hovers about 100 m lower, some 275 m over the snow.

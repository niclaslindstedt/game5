The wingsuit is its own way to start a free ride, like the paramotor: the SUIT row is gone from the helicopter, the plane, the balloon and the paramotor, which always jump in the parachute. A wingsuit ride no longer asks for skis or a snowboard, since nothing goes with you in a wingsuit: the rider card's way on is FLY and the gear card is skipped. Once you are down on your boots, a plate asks what next: fly again, open the piste map to pick a run, or quit. The START row's ON SKIS and ON A SNOWBOARD are one stop now, SLOPE, ridden on whatever the gear card picks.

---
title: A snow layer barely moves the bed's meter — the presets are the wind first
date: 2026-09-30
scope: scripts/audition.mjs
concepts: [meter, mix, snow-bed, presets]
---

Every `--meter` bed preset plays the wind AND the snow together, and from
15 m/s up the wind is most of the level: tripling the crust's crunch moved
"across the crust" by 2 dB. So a new snow layer that meters "too quiet" in a
preset is usually a preset whose wind is loud, not a layer that is missing.
Judge a snow layer's own level with the wind preset at 0 (or read its
`snowTargets` level beside the hiss at the same pace), and read the combined
presets only for the SHAPE: crust, slush and new snow near cruising, ice a
touch under it, nothing over the flat-out tuck.

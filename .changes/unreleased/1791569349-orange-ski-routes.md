ORANGE, the grade past black: every mountain marks up to two SKI ROUTES off its chairs' and gondola's tops — steeper than a groomer can work (38–48°), never groomed, never over a cliff, open of trees and down onto a piste — staked in orange, signed SKI ROUTE · NOT GROOMED with an orange double diamond at its head and drawn dashed orange on the start card, the piste map board and the minimap. ORANGE on the start card's GRADE row (or `?grade=orange`) starts a free ride up the lift its route leaves.

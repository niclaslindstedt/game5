title: An anatomy plate for the body, with every bone
---

The body at the left edge of the HUD is now a classic anatomy plate seen from the front: a traced human outline with every part painted green, yellow, orange or red as it is hurt, and over it the **skeleton**, traced off a front-view skeleton plate. That's thirty bones, from the skull and jaw down the spine and ribs to the hands and feet. A whole bone is ivory and intact. A **hairline fracture** turns it yellow, with a real crack opened into the bone. A **broken** one turns red, cut right through, its pieces knocked out of line the way a broken bone's are: shifted, angled, a long bone's splinter knocked loose. Every bone can now crack as well as break. The lines under the figure say only what the bones can't show — the organs, the torn ligaments, the sprains. The g meter shows just the number now, and injuries no longer fill the news column. A reset now stands you back up **healed**.

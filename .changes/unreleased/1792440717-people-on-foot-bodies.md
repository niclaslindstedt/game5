title: Civilians and rescue crews are real bodies to run into
---

On a free ride the people on foot are no longer ghosts at speed. Ski into a civilian, a man of the air ambulance's crew or a police officer faster than a brisk cruise and it is the same collision as hitting another skier: two bodies of about the same weight, you slowed or thrown and hurt where his body met yours, and him thrown off his feet. With INJURIES on he is hurt past getting up from about 30 km/h and killed and crushed from about 60, lying broken with blood on the snow, and both of you are left for the rescue to collect. On the snowmobile the machine hits him with all its weight: it kills from about 40 km/h and barely slows. With INJURIES off he is knocked down and gets up again.

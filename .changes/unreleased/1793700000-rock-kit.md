The mountain's bare rock walls are built of bedded LEDGES now: blocks and slabs set into the face as steps, each riser standing up out of the wall and each tread holding snow, banded along the beds, with a talus of half-buried boulders at a wall's foot, the biggest run out furthest. The skier rides the same stones he sees, and the near ones are drawn in finer cuts than the far ones, which sink back into the wall with distance.

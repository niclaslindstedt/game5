A third body on the rider card: the GUIDE, a mountain guide modelled whole. He wears a red hardshell with black shoulders and a charcoal shell pant scuffed at the ankles, and a grey helmet with a lamp. Over that goes a lime harness with a belay loop, carabiners and a blue cord, and an orange rope coiled round his chest and tied off at the harness, its tail loose in the wind. On his back is a graphite pack with crampons strapped to its face and an ice axe at its side, and a radio rides on the shoulder strap. He is picked like any other body. With the guide picked, the card only asks his weight, because his kit comes with him. No rival is ever dealt him. He rides every animation the code's skier does. A broken arm hangs from the break inside his sleeve. The rope's tail and his pack swing on the run's own clock, so they slow with a replay's slow motion and stop when the run is paused.

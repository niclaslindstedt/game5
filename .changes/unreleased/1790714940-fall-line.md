title: Fall Line
---

The first playable cut of Fall Line, a downhill skiing game for the browser: one generated mountain per seed with a piste graded down it from the summit ridge to a finish arena on the valley floor, gates down it to the finish line, six pairs of skis picked on a ski card (an all-mountain pair, a slalom, a giant slalom and a downhill ski, a powder ski and a soft twin-tip, each in four topsheets), a RACE against three rivals, a TIME TRIAL against the record book and its ghost, a FREE RIDE anywhere on the mountain in any of four kinds of country, and TRICKS on six terrain-park maps; the campaign's three shelves (the nursery, the ridge, the glacier — eighteen pinned maps on a ladder where a podium on a race or a medal on a trial opens the next map and a shelf's table opens the next shelf) and the record book that keeps the best time on every map per pair of skis and mode, with the split's gap to it at every gate; the weather, the night and the finish's floodlights; snow that deforms under every ski; crashes (a trunk, a landing over the tips, a fall, a caught edge) and the ragdoll; a replay on a broadcast camera; the shutter and the gallery; the developer page and the benchmark; and the desktop and store apps.

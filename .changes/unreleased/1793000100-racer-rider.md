Two more bodies on the rider card: the RACER, as a man or a woman, each modelled whole in a national team's race kit. The kit is a skin-tight race suit printed red fading to white down the legs, a back protector under it, a yellow bib with a big number, and a gloss red race helmet with goggles. The kit follows the discipline the pair is for. On a slalom pair he wears lime shin guards, knuckle shields on the poles and a chin guard on the helmet. On a giant slalom or ski-cross pair he wears arm pads instead. On a speed pair he wears no guards but the airbag that speed events require under his suit. The woman is cut to the women's measures and wears her braid out from under the helmet. The weight row reshapes the racer as it does the hiker. In the air the bib streams and flaps, floats off the suit while he is light, and the suit shivers on its lee side. No rival is ever dealt a racer.
